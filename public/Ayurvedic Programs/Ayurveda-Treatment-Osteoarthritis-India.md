## Understanding Osteoarthritis Through Ayurveda

In Ayurveda, osteoarthritis is viewed as a condition rooted in *Vata* imbalance combined with the accumulation of *Ama* (metabolic toxins) in the joint spaces. This combination causes the synovial fluid to diminish, cartilage to degenerate, and the surrounding tissues to weaken — producing the pain, cracking sounds, and stiffness that characterise the condition.

Conventional medicine sees this as a structural problem. Ayurveda sees it as a systemic one — and that distinction is what makes the treatment approach so different and, for many patients, so much more effective.

### The Core Ayurvedic Therapies Used for Osteoarthritis

**Janu Basti (Knee Oil Pooling Therapy)**
A specially designed dough ring is placed around the knee joint and filled with warm, medicated herbal oil. The oil is held in place for 30–45 minutes, penetrating deeply into the joint to reduce pain, improve lubrication, and slow cartilage degeneration. Janu Basti is one of the most targeted and effective treatments for knee osteoarthritis and is a cornerstone of the program.

**Abhyanga (Medicated Full-Body Oil Massage)**
Daily full-body massage using warm herbal oils chosen specifically for your Vata constitution. Abhyanga improves circulation, nourishes joint tissue, reduces stiffness, and prepares the body for deeper therapies.

**Kizhi (Herbal Pouch Massage)**
Warm bundles filled with medicinal herbs, rice, or sand are rhythmically applied to the affected joints. Kizhi reduces swelling, relieves deep muscular tension around the joint, and significantly decreases morning stiffness.

**Pizhichil (Warm Oil Stream Therapy)**
A deeply therapeutic treatment where a continuous stream of warm medicated oil is poured over the body while simultaneously being massaged in. Pizhichil is exceptional for restoring joint mobility, relieving chronic stiffness, and nourishing the nerves around affected joints.

**Basti (Medicated Enema Therapy)**
The most powerful Vata-balancing treatment in Ayurveda. Medicated oils and herbal decoctions administered through the colon act systemically to reduce Vata throughout the body — bringing relief not just to one joint but to multiple affected areas simultaneously.

**Swedana (Herbal Steam Therapy)**
Targeted or full-body steam using medicinal herbs opens the body's channels, loosens deep toxin deposits around joints, and significantly reduces stiffness and pain.

**Key Herbal Medicines Used**
Your Vaidya will prescribe personalised internal medications, which typically include powerful anti-inflammatory Ayurvedic herbs such as Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera), Shallaki (Boswellia serrata), Guggulu (Commiphora mukul), and Rasna — all clinically studied for their ability to reduce joint inflammation and support cartilage health.

## The 21–28 Day Treatment Program — Week by Week

The program follows a structured three-phase clinical approach — every phase has a specific therapeutic purpose and each week builds on the last.

| Phase | Days | Focus | Key Therapies |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Week 1 — Purva Karma (Preparation)** | Day 1–7 | Loosening toxins, preparing joints for deep treatment | Abhyanga, Swedana, dietary modifications, oral herbal medicines, initial Janu Basti |
| **Week 2 — Pradhana Karma (Core Treatment)** | Day 8–14 | Active detox and targeted joint healing | Janu Basti, Kizhi, Pizhichil, Basti, Virechana (if prescribed), Shirodhara |
| **Week 3 — Paschat Karma (Rejuvenation)** | Day 15–21 | Tissue rebuilding, strengthening, mobility restoration | Rasayana therapies, rejuvenating oils, therapeutic yoga, dietary plan |
| **Week 4 — Extended Recovery (Optional)** | Day 22–28 | For advanced cases needing deeper systemic treatment | Extended Basti cycles, additional Kizhi, functional mobility exercises |

Your program begins with a thorough one-on-one consultation with your Ayurvedic physician, who will assess your joint condition, overall health, and dosha constitution. A carefully designed anti-inflammatory Ayurvedic diet begins on day one — warm, easily digestible foods that reduce Vata and support the digestive fire.

**Week 2 — Core Treatment**
This is the most therapeutically intensive phase. Janu Basti is typically performed daily for 30 minutes per knee. Kizhi treatments targeting the hips, lower back, and affected areas bring significant relief to most patients by day 10–12. Basti therapy begins — the systematic colon cleansing that works on Vata at its root, producing effects felt throughout the musculoskeletal system. Many international patients report noticeably reduced stiffness and improved walking comfort within this week.

**Week 3 — Rejuvenation**
With the body now cleansed and the acute inflammation addressed, treatment shifts to rebuilding and strengthening. Rasayana (rejuvenation) therapies nourish the bone tissue and synovial fluid. Daily therapeutic yoga sessions — gentle, joint-specific movements chosen by your doctor — begin to restore functional mobility and muscle strength around the affected joints.

**Week 4 — Extended Program (For Advanced Cases)**
Patients with more advanced osteoarthritis, multiple joint involvement, or those who wish to achieve the deepest possible therapeutic benefit are recommended the full 28-day program.
