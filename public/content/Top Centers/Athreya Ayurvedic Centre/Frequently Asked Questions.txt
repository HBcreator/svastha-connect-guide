### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness programs, a minimum of 7 days is recommended. For our medical treatment programs, a stay of 14 to 21 days is typically required for deep, effective, and lasting healing, as advised by our physicians based on your specific condition.

**2. What is a "Panchakarma Kottage"?**

Our *Panchakarma Kottage* is a unique concept designed for your ultimate privacy and healing focus. It is a completely private, dedicated cottage where you receive all your treatments, rest, and recover, ensuring your profound healing journey is completely undisturbed.

**3. What does your "21-generation legacy" mean for my treatment?**

It means your treatment is not based on theory, but on a living legacy of healing wisdom that is simply unmatched. Our physicians have an intuitive understanding of Ayurveda that comes from centuries of clinical experience, and they often use proprietary family formulations that are exceptionally effective.

**4. What is the accommodation like? Is it a luxury resort?**

Our focus is 100% on your healing, not on luxury frills. Our accommodations, including our unique *Panchakarma Kottages*, are simple, clean, comfortable, and designed to provide a peaceful environment for rest. We are a genuine healing sanctuary, not a 5-star hotel.

**5. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a simple, wholesome, and delicious vegetarian diet based on classical Ayurvedic principles. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are customized by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha* and health condition.

**6. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our Ayurvedic physicians will review them during your initial consultation and advise you on how to manage them safely during your stay.

**7. Will I be treated by a member of the founding family?**

Yes. Your healing is personally guided by our Chief Physician, Dr. S. Sreejith, a direct descendant of this 21-generation healing family. He and his family of physicians are directly involved in your care, ensuring you receive the highest level of expertise.

**8. How do I get to the centre?**

Our retreat is located near Kottayam, Kerala, nestled in a serene green environment. We are accessible from the Cochin International Airport (COK). We are happy to help you coordinate your airport transfers for a seamless and stress-free journey.

**9. What is Kutee Praveshika Rasayana?**

This is one of the most advanced and rare rejuvenation therapies in Ayurveda, and it is a specialty of our lineage. It is an intensive rejuvenation program performed in a specially constructed hut for those seeking the deepest level of cellular renewal and longevity. It is only offered after a thorough assessment by our physicians.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable cotton clothing suitable for a warm climate and for wearing during yoga sessions. Please also bring any personal toiletries, your medical records, and a good book. We will provide towels and any necessary attire for your treatments.
