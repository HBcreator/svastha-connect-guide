**Ayushi Ayurvedic Retreat: Where Healing is a Personal Journey**

**About Ayushi Ayurveda**

Welcome to Ayushi, an intimate and authentic healing sanctuary nestled in the serene landscape of North Goa, just moments from the beautiful Mandrem beach. Founded and personally run by two passionate Ayurvedic physicians, Dr. Urja and Dr. Sanjay, our retreat was created to offer pure, classical Ayurveda in a warm, non-commercial, and deeply personal environment. Here, your health is our only priority, and your journey to wellness is guided with genuine care and expert knowledge.



### Accreditations & Recognition

1.   We are a **physician-owned and operated** retreat, meaning your healing is personally designed and supervised by our dedicated founders, Dr. Urja and Dr. Sanjay.

2.   Our greatest recognition is the **trust and high praise from our global community** of guests, whose glowing reviews consistently highlight our personalized care and transformative results.

### Philosophy & Approach

1. Our philosophy is rooted in **deep personalization**, We are a small retreat, which allows our doctors to provide you with a level of focused, individual attention that is simply unmatched.

2. We believe in treating the **root cause of your illness**, not just the symptoms. Your journey begins with a deep, traditional diagnosis to create a healing path that is exclusively yours.

3. Our approach is to create a **warm, supportive, and non-commercial environment**. Here, you are a cherished guest, not a room number, and we treat you like family.

4. We are committed to **empowering you with knowledge**. We believe that true healing includes education, giving you the tools to continue your path to wellness long after you leave us.
