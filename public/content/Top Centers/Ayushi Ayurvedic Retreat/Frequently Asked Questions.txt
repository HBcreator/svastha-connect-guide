### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness and rejuvenation programs, a minimum of 7 days is recommended. For our medical treatment programs, a stay of 14 days or more is typically required for deep and effective healing, as advised by our physicians.

**2. Will I be treated by the founders directly?**

Yes. Our founders and expert physicians, Dr. Urja and Dr. Sanjay, personally guide the healing journey of every guest. They will conduct your initial consultation, design your personalized treatment plan, and oversee your progress, ensuring you receive the highest level of expert care.

**3. What is the accommodation like? Is it a luxury resort?**

Our focus is 100% on your healing, not on luxury frills. Our accommodations are simple, clean, comfortable, and designed to provide a peaceful environment for rest. We are a genuine healing sanctuary, not a 5-star hotel, which allows us to keep our focus entirely on your therapeutic outcome.

**4. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a simple, wholesome, and delicious vegetarian diet based on classical Ayurvedic principles. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are customized by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha* and health condition.

**5. How do I get to the retreat?**

Our retreat is located in the serene landscape of North Goa, near the beautiful Mandrem beach. The nearest airport is Manohar International Airport, Mopa (GOX). We are happy to help you coordinate your airport transfers for a seamless and stress-free journey.

**6. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, it is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our physicians will review them during your initial consultation and advise you on how to manage them safely and effectively during your stay.

**7. Are the treatments really personalized?**

Yes, completely. Because we are a small, physician-led retreat, deep personalization is at the heart of everything we do. There are no one-size-fits-all packages here; your entire treatment plan is crafted exclusively for you.

**8. Can a companion who is not taking treatment stay with me?**

Yes, companions are welcome to stay with you and enjoy the serene environment and our healthy Ayurvedic cuisine. Please note that treatments are only provided to those who have undergone a consultation and have a prescribed wellness plan.

**9. What makes Ayushi different from other centers in Goa?**

Our key difference is our intimate, non-commercial, and physician-led approach. We are not a large resort; we are a small, family-like sanctuary where your healing is personally guided by our founding doctors. This creates a much more focused and supportive environment.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing suitable for both yoga practice and Goa's warm climate. Please also bring any personal toiletries, your medical records, and a good book. We will provide towels and any necessary attire for your treatments.
