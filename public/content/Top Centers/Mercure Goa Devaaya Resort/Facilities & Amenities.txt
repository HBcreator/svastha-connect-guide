### Facilities & Amenities

**NABH-Accredited Ayurveda & Naturopathy Centre**
*   Heal with confidence in our state-of-the-art wellness center, accredited for the highest standards of quality and patient safety.
*   Benefit from well-equipped, hygienic, and dedicated therapy rooms for both Ayurvedic and Naturopathic treatments.
*   Our facilities are designed to deliver authentic, traditional therapies in a modern, clinically supervised environment.

**Riverside Yoga & Meditation Pavilions**
*   Practice yoga and meditation in our serene, open-air pavilions with breathtaking views of the Mandovi River.
*   Find your inner peace in a tranquil, natural setting designed to enhance your mindful practice.
*   Join daily sessions guided by our experienced instructors in these purpose-built, peaceful spaces.

**Well-Appointed Accommodations**
*   Relax and unwind in our spacious, comfortable rooms and suites that blend Goan heritage with modern comforts.
*   Enjoy modern amenities and attentive service, ensuring a restful and seamless stay as part of the Mercure experience.
*   Wake up to serene views of the river or our lush, landscaped gardens from your private balcony.

**Therapeutic Vegetarian Restaurant**
*   Savor delicious, healing meals at our dedicated wellness restaurant overlooking the river.
*   Enjoy a personalized menu crafted by our physicians and chefs to support your specific dosha and treatment plan.
*   Discover that healthy, nourishing food can be a truly delightful culinary experience.

**Swimming Pool & Riverside Sun Deck**
*   Refresh your body with a swim in our expansive outdoor pool, surrounded by lush tropical greenery.
*   Relax and soak up the Goan sun on our comfortable loungers, with serene views of the river.
*   Enjoy a safe, clean, and beautifully maintained facility, perfect for relaxation between your therapies.

**Fitness Centre & Recreational Facilities**
*   Maintain your fitness routine in our well-equipped gymnasium with a range of modern equipment.
*   Stay active with our outdoor sports facilities, including tennis and badminton courts.
*   Enjoy a holistic approach to wellness that includes both mindful practice and physical activity.

**On-Site Physician Consultation Rooms**
*   Begin your wellness journey in our private and professional consultation rooms with our resident doctors.
*   Benefit from daily medical supervision and progress monitoring in a comfortable and confidential setting.
*   Feel completely supported with our expert medical team available on-site throughout your stay.

**Lush Gardens & Nature Trails**
*   Heal in a sprawling, oxygen-rich environment spread across our serene island property.
*   Reconnect with nature through peaceful walks on our beautifully landscaped garden trails.
*   The tranquil, pollution-free atmosphere is a powerful medicine in itself, deepening your healing.

**Library & Relaxation Lounges**
*   Nourish your mind with our curated collection of books on Ayurveda, wellness, and philosophy.
*   Find quiet, comfortable spaces for peaceful reading, study, and contemplation.
*   Deepen your understanding of your healing journey in our serene indoor and outdoor lounges.
