### Treatment Process & Patient Journey

Your personalized path to holistic healing, guided with clinical expertise and five-star care on our serene island.

1.  **Arrival & Comprehensive Doctor's Consultation**
    Your journey begins with a deep and personal consultation in our NABH-accredited facility. Our resident physicians will conduct a thorough assessment to understand your unique health story, goals, and mind-body constitution.
    *   You will be personally guided by both our expert Ayurvedic and Naturopathic physicians.
    *   Through traditional diagnostics like pulse reading (*Nadi Pariksha*), we uncover your unique *Prakriti*.
    *   We listen deeply to understand your concerns and identify the root cause of any imbalance.

2.  **Personalized Integrated Wellness Plan**
    Based on your consultation, our physicians will craft your exclusive wellness blueprint—a detailed roadmap combining the best of Ayurveda and Naturopathy to restore your natural state of health and balance.
    *   This is not a one-size-fits-all plan; it is your unique, physician-led path to healing.
    *   It specifies your bespoke schedule of Ayurvedic and Naturopathic therapies, including any *Panchakarma* procedures.
    *   Your plan also includes a tailored diet, herbal medicines, and lifestyle guidance for holistic results.

3.  **Daily Authentic Treatments & Therapies**
    This is where your deep healing and rejuvenation truly begin. Each day, your prescribed treatments are delivered with exceptional skill and compassionate care by our highly trained therapists in our serene riverside treatment rooms.
    *   You will receive your treatments each day from our skilled and certified therapists.
    *   All therapies are performed using authentic, traditional techniques for maximum therapeutic benefit.
    *   Our serene, natural environment enhances the profound, healing effects of every therapy.

4.  **Personalized Therapeutic Cuisine**
    We believe that food is powerful medicine, and your meals are a delicious and vital part of your healing journey. Your diet is personally prescribed by your doctor to support your specific treatments and health objectives.
    *   Enjoy delicious, therapeutic vegetarian meals prepared from fresh, wholesome ingredients.
    *   Your meals are customized according to Ayurvedic and Naturopathic principles to support your healing.
    *   This nourishing cuisine helps to detoxify and heal your body from the inside out.

5.  **Daily Wellness Activities & Medical Monitoring**
    Your wellness journey is a comprehensive experience that balances active healing with mindful relaxation and is overseen with medical precision. Your progress is closely monitored by our physicians to ensure your journey is both safe and effective.
    *   Harmonize your mind and body with daily yoga and guided meditation sessions by the riverside.
    *   Your progress is reviewed daily by our resident doctors, who make any necessary adjustments to your plan.
    *   This combination of mindful practice and medical supervision ensures a truly holistic and effective journey.

6.  **Discharge Plan & Lifestyle Empowerment**
    Our support for you doesn’t end when you leave our island sanctuary; we empower you with the tools for a lifetime of well-being. Before you depart, you will receive a comprehensive final consultation with your physician.
    *   We will provide you with a detailed and personalized plan, including dietary and lifestyle advice.
    *   Receive guidance on practices you can continue at home to maintain your progress.
    *   You leave not just rejuvenated, but empowered with the knowledge to sustain your health for years to come.
