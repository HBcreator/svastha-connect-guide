**Transportation Services**

To ensure your journey to our serene island sanctuary is seamless and stress-free, our administrative team is happy to assist you in arranging transportation from the airport or nearby railway stations. The final part of your journey will include a beautiful, short ferry ride across the Mandovi River to reach Divar Island, marking your true escape from the mainland.
