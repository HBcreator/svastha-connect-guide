### Insurance & Payment Info

Making your path to our healing island clear and accessible.

**Insurance Coverage**

*   As a NABH-accredited and AYUSH Premium Mark certified hospital, our medical treatments are often eligible for insurance claims.

*   Our professional administrative team provides all necessary medical documentation to support your reimbursement process.

*   We are committed to helping you navigate the claims process as smoothly as possible.

*   We strongly advise you to confirm coverage details directly with your insurance provider before your stay.

**Payment Options**

*   We accept all major payment methods, including credit/debit cards and domestic/international bank transfers.

*   Our pricing is completely transparent, with all costs clearly outlined in a personalized quotation after your consultation.

*   Our pricing reflects our commitment to providing authentic, clinical-grade healing in a unique island setting.

*   You will receive a detailed cost estimate before your treatment begins, with absolutely no hidden charges.

**For International Patients**

International guests are encouraged to check with their home insurance providers regarding coverage for Ayurvedic treatments at our NABH-accredited facility. Our administrative team is highly experienced in providing the necessary, detailed medical reports and documentation in the required format to support your international insurance claims.
