### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness programs, a minimum of 7 days is recommended. For our medical treatment programs like Panchakarma, a stay of 14 days or more is typically required for deep, effective, and lasting healing.

**2. What is it like being on Divar Island? Is it isolated?**

Being on Divar Island is a core part of the healing experience. The short ferry ride to reach us transports you from the energy of Goa to a world of peace and tranquility. The island is serene and secluded, allowing you to completely disconnect and immerse yourself in nature.

**3. What is the atmosphere at Devaaya like? Is it a resort or a hospital?**

We are a unique blend of both. We are a fully **NABH-accredited hospital**, so our primary focus is on serious, high-quality medical treatment. However, our beautiful Portuguese-style architecture, 5-acre property, and serene riverside setting provide the peaceful ambiance of a wellness retreat.

**4. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a delicious and wholesome vegetarian diet based on classical Ayurvedic and Naturopathic principles. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are customized by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha* and health condition.

**5. What is the difference between your Ayurveda and Naturopathy treatments?**

Ayurveda and Naturopathy are sister sciences that we integrate for your benefit. Ayurveda focuses on balancing the *doshas*, while Naturopathy harnesses the healing power of natural elements. Our expert physicians will design a personalized plan for you that may include therapies from both systems for the most comprehensive results.

**6. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our physicians will review them during your initial consultation and advise you on how to manage them safely during your stay.

**7. Can a companion who is not taking treatment stay with me?**

Yes, companions are welcome to stay with you and enjoy the serene island environment and our healthy cuisine. Please note that treatments are only provided to those who have undergone a consultation and have a prescribed wellness plan.

**8. Do you serve alcohol at the retreat?**

To honor our commitment to a pure and holistic healing environment, we are a completely alcohol-free and smoking-free sanctuary. This allows for a deeper and more authentic detoxification and rejuvenation experience for all our guests.

**9. Is there internet or Wi-Fi available?**

Limited Wi-Fi is available in designated common areas. However, to fully embrace the healing energy of our island sanctuary, we gently encourage our guests to embrace a digital detox, as disconnecting from screens is a powerful way to calm the mind and deepen the healing experience.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing suitable for Goa's warm climate. Please also bring any personal toiletries, your medical records, and a good book. We will provide towels and any necessary attire for your treatments.
