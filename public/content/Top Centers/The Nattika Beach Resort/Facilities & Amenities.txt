### Facilities & Amenities

**Spacious & Private Detached Villas**
*   Rest and heal in complete privacy in one of our 52 spacious, detached villas, nestled within lush coconut groves.
*   Each villa is thoughtfully designed with traditional Keralan aesthetics, modern comforts, and a private verandah.
*   This personal sanctuary space is essential for the deep rest and quiet contemplation required for your healing journey.

**'Green Leaf' Certified Ayurveda Centre**
*   Receive your treatments in our authentic 'Green Leaf' certified Ayurveda centre, ensuring the highest standards of traditional practice.
*   Our facility features 20 well-equipped, clean, and hygienic therapy rooms designed for all classical Ayurvedic treatments.
*   Heal with confidence, knowing you are being cared for by our highly skilled and certified male and female therapists.

**Ayurvedic Restaurant 'Vapika'**
*   Nourish your body at our dedicated vegetarian restaurant, serving a delicious buffet carefully designed for holistic healing.
*   Our unique system features dishes clearly labeled for each dosha (Vata, Pitta, Kapha), making it easy to follow your doctor's advice.
*   Enjoy your meals in a serene, semi-open-air setting with views of our lush gardens.

**Beachfront Yoga & Meditation Hall**
*   Harmonize your mind and body in our beautiful, semi-open-air yoga shala, located just steps from the sea.
*   Practice yoga and meditation to the soothing sounds of the ocean waves, with the gentle sea breeze enhancing your experience.
*   Join daily sessions guided by our experienced instructors, with practices tailored to complement your Ayurvedic treatments.

**Direct Nattika Beach Access**
*   Step directly from our lush resort grounds onto the pristine, clean, and peaceful sands of Nattika Beach.
*   Enjoy the serenity of a coastline that is free from excessive crowds, perfect for mindful sunrise or sunset walks.
*   The tranquil energy of the Arabian Sea is a constant and powerful presence that aids your healing process.

**Large Swimming Pool & Sun Deck**
*   Refresh your body in our large, beautifully maintained swimming pool, a serene oasis within our tropical gardens.
*   Relax on comfortable sun loungers, offering a perfect space for rest and rejuvenation between your daily treatments.
*   Enjoy a safe, clean, and peaceful alternative for a refreshing swim if you prefer fresh water to the sea.

**Expansive Coconut Grove & Gardens**
*   Heal in a sprawling, 16-acre sanctuary of lush coconut groves and meticulously maintained tropical gardens.
*   The vast, oxygen-rich environment provides an unparalleled sense of space and tranquility for your healing journey.
*   Reconnect with nature through peaceful walks on our garden pathways, surrounded by verdant beauty.

**On-Site Doctor Consultation Rooms**
*   Begin your journey and monitor your progress in our private and professional on-site consultation rooms.
*   Benefit from the confidentiality and comfort of a dedicated clinical space for your daily reviews with our senior physicians.
*   This facility underscores our commitment to a medically supervised, safe, and effective healing experience.

**Guest Services & Library**
*   Enhance your stay with our thoughtful guest amenities, including a well-stocked library with books on Ayurveda, yoga, and culture.
*   Browse our on-site boutique for essentials, local crafts, and wellness products to take home.
*   Our friendly and attentive reception team is always available to assist with any needs or travel arrangements.
