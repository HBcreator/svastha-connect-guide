### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

To honor the depth and authenticity of our healing process, we require a minimum stay of 7 nights. For our more intensive medical or detoxification programs like Panchakarma, a stay of 14 nights or more is highly recommended for lasting results.

**2. What is the atmosphere at Nattika like? Is it a luxury resort?**

Our focus is on authentic, classical Ayurveda in a serene and natural environment. While we offer comfortable and spacious private villas, we are a true healing sanctuary, not a 5-star luxury hotel. The atmosphere is peaceful, disciplined, and entirely focused on your well-being.

**3. What is the "Green Leaf" certification?**

The 'Green Leaf' certification is the highest honor for authenticity awarded by the Government of Kerala. It is your guarantee that you are receiving the most traditional, high-quality, and authentic Ayurvedic care, which is the cornerstone of our philosophy.

**4. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a pure, delicious, and strictly vegetarian Ayurvedic cuisine at our 'Vapika' restaurant. Your meals are served as a buffet, with each dish clearly labeled for the different *doshas* (Vata, Pitta, Kapha), making it very easy for you to follow your doctor's personalized dietary advice.

**5. How do I get to the resort?**

Our resort is located on the serene Nattika Beach in Thrissur, Kerala. The nearest airport is Cochin International Airport (COK), which is approximately a 1.5 to 2-hour scenic drive away. We are happy to coordinate your airport transfers for a seamless journey.

**6. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our physicians will review them during your comprehensive initial consultation and advise you on how to manage them safely and effectively during your stay.

**7. Can a companion stay with me if they are not taking treatment?**

Yes, companions are welcome to stay with you in your private villa. They can enjoy our serene environment, our healthy Ayurvedic cuisine, and the beautiful Nattika beach. Please note that personalized treatments are only for guests on a specific wellness program.

**8. Do I need to be experienced in yoga?**

Not at all. Our daily yoga and meditation sessions, held in our beautiful beachfront shala, are designed for all levels, from complete beginners to advanced practitioners. Our experienced instructors provide personalized guidance to ensure the practice is safe and supportive of your healing journey.

**9. Is there internet or Wi-Fi in the rooms?**

To encourage a true disconnection and deepen your healing experience, we do not provide televisions or Wi-Fi in the villas. Complimentary Wi-Fi is available in our reception area for any essential communication.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing suitable for a warm, tropical climate. Please also bring swimwear, any personal toiletries, and your medical records. We will provide you with a fresh set of traditional, loose-fitting cotton pajamas (*kurta*) to wear for your treatments.
