### Facilities & Amenities

Traditional comfort crafted for your healing journey.

**Diverse Accommodation Options**

*   Choose from a range of categories, from cozy cottages to luxurious suites, many with stunning sea views.

*   All our rooms are designed for your comfort, featuring air conditioning and private, serene terraces.

*   Some of our deluxe options are historic restored wooden houses, offering a truly unique cultural experience.

**Authentic Ayurvedic Treatment Center**

*   Experience your treatments in our serene, private therapy rooms designed for authentic Ayurvedic procedures.

*   Benefit from the purity of our in-house pharmacy, where your medicines are freshly prepared for you.

*   Trust in our care, delivered in a clean, hygienic, and deeply peaceful environment.

**Ayurvedic Restaurant & Dining**

*   Enjoy a full-board of delicious, dosha-specific vegetarian meals, included in your package.

*   Our cuisine is designed to support your detoxification and healing process, using fresh, local ingredients.

*   Dine in our serene, open-air restaurant, overlooking our lush gardens and the sea.

**Yoga & Meditation Spaces**

*   Practice yoga in our stunning open-air shalas with breathtaking views of the ocean.

*   Find your inner peace with guided meditation sessions held in our tranquil tropical gardens.

*   Our complimentary daily classes are designed to perfectly complement your healing journey.

**Private Beach & Tropical Gardens**

*   Enjoy direct access to our pristine and secluded beach on the beautiful Malabar Coast.

*   Find peace and privacy as you wander through our expansive, lush tropical gardens.

*   The serene ambiance, fresh sea air, and the sound of the waves will deepen your healing.

**Swimming Pool & Relaxation Zones**

*   Enjoy a gentle swim in our outdoor pool, surrounded by lush greenery.

*   Relax and unwind on comfortable poolside loungers with panoramic views.

*   This low-impact activity is a wonderful way to enhance your circulation and calm your mind.

**In-House Ayurveda Shop**

*   Find a wide selection of our own fresh herbal medicines, oils, and beauty products.

*   Discover books on Ayurveda, yoga accessories, and traditional Kerala handicrafts.

*   It’s the perfect place to find a meaningful souvenir or to continue your wellness journey at home.

**A Space for Digital Detox**

*   To support your journey of inner connection, we do not provide WiFi in the rooms.

*   This encourages you to embrace a digital detox, rest your mind, and be present.

*   Our serene reading corners and beautiful gardens offer wonderful offline alternatives.

**Dedicated Guest Support**

*   Our unique German-Indian management ensures the highest international standards of service.

*   Enjoy the convenience of our airport transfer services for a smooth and easy arrival.

*   Our European support office is always available to assist with your planning and booking.
