### Treatment Process & Patient Journey

A personalized healing journey guided by expert physicians.

**Pre-Arrival Consultation & Planning**

Your journey to wellness begins with thoughtful planning and support before you even arrive.

*   Our process starts with a detailed health questionnaire, allowing our doctors to review your case in advance.

*   Our expert team provides guidance to help you select the perfect treatment program for your needs.

*   You will receive helpful pre-arrival instructions on diet and travel to ensure you are ready for your healing.

**Arrival & Comprehensive Consultation**

From the moment you arrive, you are enveloped in warmth and expert care as your personalized journey begins.

*   Upon arrival, you will receive a warm welcome and an orientation to our serene healing village.

*   You will have a comprehensive, 45-60 minute consultation with your personal Ayurvedic physician.

*   Through traditional diagnostics, your doctor will design a completely personalized treatment plan just for you.

**Your Daily Healing Routine**

Each day is a harmonious blend of treatment, rest, and mindful activity, creating the perfect rhythm for deep healing.

*   Begin your day with gentle yoga and meditation on our stunning open-air platform.

*   Receive 2-3 hours of personalized Ayurvedic therapies from your dedicated therapist.

*   Enjoy delicious, dosha-specific vegetarian meals from our buffet, clearly labeled to support your healing.

**Ongoing Monitoring & Adjustments**

Your healing journey is a dynamic process, with your treatment plan evolving as you do.

*   Your physician will have regular check-ins with you every few days to monitor your progress.

*   We ensure you are treated by the same therapist as much as possible to build trust and consistency.

*   Your herbal medicines and therapies may be adjusted based on your body's response for the best results.

**Final Consultation & Empowerment**

As your stay concludes, we empower you with the tools and knowledge for a lifetime of wellness.

*   You will have a detailed final consultation with your physician to review your incredible progress.

*   Receive a personalized maintenance plan with clear, written recommendations on diet, yoga, and lifestyle.

*   We provide you with a supply of your prescribed Ayurvedic medicines to continue your healing at home.

**Post-Treatment Support & Follow-Up**

Our relationship with you doesn’t end when you leave; our care continues long after your stay.

*   You will have continued access to our medical team for any follow-up questions or guidance.

*   We offer an international medicine refill service so you can continue your protocol without interruption.

*   Many of our guests return annually, becoming cherished members of the Somatheeram family.
