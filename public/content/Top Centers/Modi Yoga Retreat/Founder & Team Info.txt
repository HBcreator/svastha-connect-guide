**Dr. Bhupendra Kumar Modi (Dr. M)**

Founder-Chairman

Dr. Bhupendra Kumar Modi, affectionately known as Dr. M, is the visionary Founder-Chairman of the retreat. His creation is a manifestation of his profound 'Beyond 100' movement—a mission dedicated to helping people live a long, healthy, and happy life beyond 100 years. A renowned billionaire and philanthropist, Dr. M has channeled his passion into creating a world-class sanctuary for deep, transformative wellness on the banks of the Ganges.
