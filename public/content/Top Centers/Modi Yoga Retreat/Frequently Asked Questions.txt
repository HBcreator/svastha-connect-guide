### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness programs, we recommend a minimum stay of 3 to 5 nights to experience tangible benefits. For our more immersive or medical-focused programs, a stay of 7 nights or more is typically required for deep and lasting results.

**2. What makes Modi Yoga Retreat different from other retreats in Rishikesh?**

Our key difference is the perfect fusion of a luxurious, modern retreat with a deep, authentic spiritual focus. Our serene location on the banks of the holy river Ganges, our private ghat for sacred rituals, and our world-class facilities create an unparalleled environment for transformative healing.

**3. How do I get to the retreat?**

Our retreat is located in Rishikesh, the world capital of Yoga. The nearest airport is the Jolly Grant Airport in Dehradun (DED), which is approximately a 45-minute scenic drive away. We are happy to arrange your private luxury airport transfers for a seamless journey.

**4. What kind of food is served?**

You will be served a delicious, gourmet wellness cuisine that is purely vegetarian. Our chefs work in close collaboration with our wellness experts to create a menu that is customized to your health goals, is deeply nourishing, and incredibly flavorful.

**5. Do I need to be experienced in yoga or meditation?**

Not at all. Our daily yoga and meditation sessions are designed for all levels, from complete beginners to advanced practitioners. Our experienced masters provide personalized guidance to ensure the practice is safe, enjoyable, and perfectly suited to your body and goals.

**6. What is the "Ganga Aarti" at your private ghat?**

The Ganga Aarti is a beautiful and powerful ancient Hindu ritual of worship to the river Ganges. Experiencing this sacred ceremony at our own private, tranquil ghat, away from the crowds, is a unique, deeply moving, and spiritually uplifting experience that is a highlight for many of our guests.

**7. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our physicians will review them during your comprehensive initial consultation to create a safe and effective treatment plan for you.

**8. Can a companion stay with me if they are not on a wellness program?**

Yes, your companion is welcome to stay with you in our luxurious accommodations. They can enjoy our serene environment, our gourmet wellness cuisine, and participate in our daily complimentary wellness activities like yoga, meditation, and the Ganga Aarti.

**9. Is this a suitable place for a casual holiday?**

While our setting is as beautiful and luxurious as any five-star hotel, our primary focus is on providing a deep and authentic wellness journey. We are a sanctuary for those who are serious about their health and are seeking a peaceful, spiritually-focused environment for their healing.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing for yoga and other activities, as well as smart casual attire for evenings. Please also bring any personal toiletries and your medical records. We will provide all other necessary amenities for a luxurious and comfortable stay.
