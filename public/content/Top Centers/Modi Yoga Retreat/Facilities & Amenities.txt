### Facilities & Amenities

**Luxurious Ganga-Facing Accommodations**
*   Rest and rejuvenate in our exquisitely appointed rooms and suites, each offering breathtaking, panoramic views of the holy river Ganga.
*   Experience five-star comfort with bespoke amenities, elegant decor, and a serene ambiance designed for deep relaxation.
*   Your private sanctuary is a perfect blend of modern luxury and timeless, spiritual tranquility.

**Integrated Wellness & Spa Centre**
*   Heal in our expansive, state-of-the-art wellness centre, offering a unique integration of Ayurveda, Naturopathy, and international therapies.
*   Our facility features numerous private and hygienic treatment rooms, including dedicated suites for Ayurveda, hydrotherapy, and acupuncture.
*   Benefit from a professional, medically-supervised environment where every detail is curated for your healing journey.

**Riverside Yoga & Meditation Spaces**
*   Practice yoga in its sacred birthplace in our serene, dedicated yoga studio or on our magnificent deck overlooking the river Ganga.
*   Harmonize your mind and body in peaceful spaces designed to enhance your connection with the spiritual energy of Rishikesh.
*   These purpose-built facilities provide the perfect, tranquil environment for both group sessions and private instruction.

**Personalized Gourmet Wellness Restaurant**
*   Nourish your body in our elegant dining room, where every meal is a personalized, gourmet experience.
*   We have no buffets; our master chefs, in consultation with our doctors, craft every vegetarian meal specifically for you.
*   This unique culinary facility ensures that your food is a powerful, delicious, and bespoke part of your cure.

**Temperature-Controlled Swimming Pool**
*   Refresh your body and spirit in our stunning, temperature-controlled swimming pool, offering serene views of the surrounding nature.
*   Relax on comfortable sun loungers on our spacious deck, a perfect space for rest and rejuvenation between your therapies.
*   Enjoy a safe, clean, and beautifully maintained facility, perfect for a gentle swim in any season.

**State-of-the-Art Fitness Centre**
*   Maintain your fitness routine in our fully equipped, modern gymnasium with panoramic views.
*   Benefit from a comprehensive range of the latest cardio and strength-training equipment.
*   Enjoy a holistic approach to wellness that balances restorative therapies with invigorating physical activity.

**Private Ganga Ghat & Deck**
*   Connect with the sacred river at our exclusive, private Ganga ghat (riverside steps) and meditation deck.
*   This unique facility provides a serene and intimate space for personal meditation, quiet contemplation, or observing riverside rituals.
*   Experience the profound peace of being by the holy Ganga, away from the public crowds.

**Library & Relaxation Lounge**
*   Nourish your mind in our serene library, offering a curated collection of books on wellness, spirituality, philosophy, and culture.
*   Find a quiet, comfortable space for peaceful reading, study, and deepening your understanding of your wellness journey.
*   Our elegant lounge provides a perfect sanctuary for quiet reflection or conversation with fellow guests.

**Business Centre & Event Spaces**
*   Host exclusive corporate wellness retreats or high-level meetings in our sophisticated, fully-equipped business centre and boardrooms.
*   Benefit from state-of-the-art technology and a dedicated events team to curate a flawless and prestigious event.
*   Our serene, inspiring environment provides the perfect backdrop for productive and rejuvenating business gatherings.
