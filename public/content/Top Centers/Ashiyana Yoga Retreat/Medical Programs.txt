### Medical Programs

Profound journeys of healing and self-discovery, using the therapeutic power of yoga, breath, and community.

**Holistic Stress Recovery**
This is a deeply restorative program for those feeling depleted by the demands of modern life. It focuses on down-regulating the nervous system through a dedicated practice of Restorative Yoga, Yin, Yoga Nidra, and calming spa therapies.
*   A therapeutic sanctuary for healing from chronic stress and adrenal fatigue.
*   Helps to restore deep, natural sleep cycles and calm an anxious mind.
*   Teaches you practical tools for building long-term resilience.

**Emotional Healing**
A supportive journey designed to help you process and release stored emotional tension. This program uses a combination of heart-opening yoga practices, dynamic breathwork (pranayama), and a safe, communal space to help you heal past wounds and cultivate self-love.
*   Gently release emotional blockages and cultivate a sense of inner freedom.
*   A compassionate approach to healing the heart space.
*   Fosters profound self-acceptance and emotional balance.

**Postural alignment Relief**
This therapeutic program focuses on healing the physical body by addressing chronic pain and postural imbalances. Through mindful, alignment-based yoga and targeted bodywork, you will learn to release deep-seated muscular tension and create healthier movement patterns.
*   Find natural relief from chronic back, neck, and shoulder pain.
*   Improves body awareness and corrects postural habits.
*   A sustainable approach to creating a pain-free, functional body.

**Life Transitions**
A powerful program for those standing at a crossroads in life. Whether facing a career change, relationship shift, or personal questioning, this retreat uses yoga, meditation, and guided self-inquiry to help you find clarity, courage, and a renewed sense of purpose.
*   A supportive space to find clarity during times of change and uncertainty.
*   Reconnect with your inner wisdom and personal truth.
*   Empowers you to move forward with confidence and intention.

**Grief & Loss Healing**
A gentle and compassionate space to honor and process grief. This program uses incredibly gentle, heart-centered practices, sharing circles, and the healing power of nature to provide a safe container for you to navigate loss without judgment.
*   A tender and supportive journey for those healing a broken heart.
*   Honors the grieving process with gentle movement and community support.
*   Fosters a sense of peace and acceptance.

**Pranic Healing**
A deep dive into the subtle art and science of pranayama. This program is for those looking to move beyond basic breathing techniques and explore the profound therapeutic potential of advanced breathwork to clear energetic blockages, expand consciousness, and heal the nervous system.
*   Explore the powerful connection between breath, energy, and consciousness.
*   Learn advanced techniques to clear stagnant energy (*prana*) from the body.
*   A transformative journey for your physical, mental, and energetic well-being.

**Chakra Balancing**
This is an experiential journey through the seven major energy centers of the body. Through a combination of specific yoga asanas, sound healing (mantra), color visualization, and meditation, this program works to cleanse, balance, and realign your chakras.
*   Restores the harmonious flow of energy throughout your entire system.
*   Helps to release energetic blockages related to emotional and physical patterns.
*   Promotes a profound sense of wholeness, vitality, and alignment.

**Silent Meditation**
The ultimate journey inward. This program offers the rare opportunity to immerse yourself in the profound practice of silence (*Mouna*). Guided by experienced teachers, you will explore various meditation techniques to quiet the mind and connect with your deepest self.
*   A powerful opportunity to quiet the mental chatter and find deep inner peace.
*   Sharpens your awareness and cultivates profound self-insight.
*   A truly transformative experience of stillness and presence.

**Body Positivity**
A healing program designed to mend your relationship with your body. This journey moves away from performance-based asana and focuses on intuitive movement, self-acceptance, and celebrating your body exactly as it is, fostering a spirit of radical self-love.
*   Heal from negative body image and cultivate deep self-acceptance.
*   A focus on how yoga *feels*, not how it *looks*.
*   A joyful and empowering path to true embodiment.

**Reclaiming Creative Flow**
A therapeutic program for those feeling creatively stuck or uninspired. It uses a dynamic blend of energizing Vinyasa yoga, meditation, and creative expression workshops (like journaling or movement) to dissolve blocks and reconnect you with your innate creative fire.
*   Overcome creative blocks and rediscover your passion and inspiration.
*   Connects the freedom of the body with the freedom of the mind.
*   A vibrant and playful journey back to your most creative self.

**Somatic Healing**
This specialized, therapeutic program provides a safe and gentle path to healing for those who have experienced trauma. Led by specifically trained facilitators, it uses trauma-informed yoga and somatic (body-based) practices to help regulate the nervous system and release stored trauma from the body.
*   A highly safe, gentle, and supportive environment for healing.
*   Focuses on re-establishing a sense of safety and connection with your body.
*   Empowers you with tools to regulate your nervous system.

**Deeper Dimensions**
This is more than just a flexibility program; it is a deep dive into the meditative and energetic aspects of Yin Yoga. This therapeutic immersion explores how the long-held poses work on the body's meridian lines (energy channels) to release deep-seated tension and restore pranic flow.
*   A profound journey into the subtle, energetic body.
*   Works to release long-held physical, mental, and emotional tension.
*   A deeply meditative practice that cultivates patience and stillness.
