### Wellness & Rejuvenation Programs

Guiding your personal journey to find your practice, nourish your soul, and come home to yourself.

**Ashiyana Yoga Holiday**
This is our signature flexible experience, allowing you to immerse yourself in the Ashiyana lifestyle. With access to our full schedule of drop-in classes, you can create your own perfect blend of dynamic yoga, restorative practices, and beachside relaxation.
*   The ultimate freedom to design your own daily yoga schedule.
*   Perfect for all levels, from curious beginners to seasoned practitioners.
*   Stay for a few days or a few weeks—the journey is yours to create.

**200-Hour Training**
Embark on a life-changing journey to deepen your practice and learn the art of teaching. Our accredited trainings are held in an inspiring and supportive environment, guided by world-class teachers who will help you find your authentic voice.
*   Become a certified Yoga Teacher with a globally recognized Yoga Alliance qualification.
*   An immersive experience covering asana, philosophy, anatomy, and teaching methodology.
*   A profound path of personal growth and transformation.

**300-Hour Advanced Yoga**
For certified teachers looking to elevate their practice and teaching to the next level. This advanced training delves deeper into yogic philosophy, advanced asana, pranayama, and the subtle arts of sequencing and holding space.
*   Refine your teaching skills and deepen your understanding of yoga's wisdom.
*   Explore advanced themes and practices with a cohort of dedicated peers.
*   A powerful step forward in your professional and personal yoga journey.

**Yoga & Immersions**
Throughout the year, we host a curated calendar of retreats led by renowned international teachers. These immersions focus on specific styles or themes, from Vinyasa and Yin to AcroYoga and Jivamukti, offering a chance for a deep dive with a master teacher.
*   An opportunity to study intensively with a world-class yoga instructor.
*   Explore a specific style or philosophy in a focused, retreat setting.
*   Connect with a global community of passionate yogis.

**Holistic Spa & Bodywork**
This program is a tribute to deep relaxation and physical release. It combines daily yoga with a curated package of treatments from our holistic spa, including Thai massage, Ayurvedic therapies, and deep tissue bodywork, to melt away tension and restore your body.
*   A perfect blend of yoga practice and therapeutic bodywork.
*   Release deep-seated physical tension and emotional blockages.
*   Leaves you feeling profoundly relaxed, open, and restored.

**Yoga Foundation Retreat**
A welcoming and supportive introduction to the world of yoga. This retreat is designed for absolute beginners, breaking down the fundamentals of asana, breathing, and meditation in a safe and non-intimidating environment.
*   Build a strong and safe foundation for a lifelong yoga practice.
*   Learn key postures, alignment, and breathing techniques from patient teachers.
*   Gain the confidence to step into any yoga class in the world.

**Couples Connect & Care**
Share a journey of connection, practice, and presence with your partner. This experience combines daily yoga classes with partner-based practices and plenty of free time to relax together on the beach, reconnecting on a deeper, more mindful level.
*   A unique opportunity to deepen your bond through shared practice.
*   Combines wellness with romantic relaxation in a beautiful setting.
*   Learn new ways to connect and support each other, both on and off the mat.

**Digital Detox**
A true escape from the demands of the digital world. This program invites you to disconnect from your devices and reconnect with yourself through yoga, meditation, nature walks, and journaling, allowing you to reset your nervous system and find mental clarity.
*   Calm an overstimulated mind and restore your natural focus.
*   Reduces digital dependency and cultivates a sense of presence.
*   A powerful reset for your mental and emotional well-being.

**Women's Soulful Retreat**
A sacred space for women to gather, share, and heal. This retreat combines feminine-focused yoga practices, sharing circles, and empowering workshops to help you reconnect with your inner wisdom, strength, and the supportive power of sisterhood.
*   A safe and nurturing environment for self-exploration and connection.
*   Reconnect with your intuition and authentic feminine power.
*   Forge deep and lasting bonds with a like-minded community of women.

**Mandrem Weekend**
The perfect short escape for a powerful reset. This 2-3 day program is designed to immerse you quickly into the Ashiyana vibe, with a potent schedule of yoga, healthy food, and beach time to de-stress and recharge your batteries.
*   A quick, effective, and accessible reset for a busy schedule.
*   Wash away the stress of the week and return feeling energized.
*   Get a potent taste of the full Ashiyana experience.

**Creative Arts & Fusion**
Unleash your creative spirit in this unique fusion retreat. This program blends daily yoga and meditation with creative workshops like ecstatic dance, creative writing, or mandala art, helping you to move beyond creative blocks and express your authentic self.
*   A beautiful synergy of mindful movement and artistic expression.
*   Tap into your creative potential in a supportive, non-judgmental space.
*   A joyful and playful path to self-discovery.

**Ayurveda & Immersion**
Discover the sister sciences of life in this integrated program. It combines daily yoga practice with an introduction to the fundamental principles of Ayurveda, including understanding your dosha and learning how to apply this ancient wisdom to your daily diet and lifestyle.
*   Learn how to harmonize your life using the wisdom of Ayurveda.
*   Understand your unique mind-body constitution (*Prakriti*).
*   A holistic program for creating sustainable, lifelong well-being.
