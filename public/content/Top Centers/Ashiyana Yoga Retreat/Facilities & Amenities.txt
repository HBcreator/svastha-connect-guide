### Facilities & Amenities

**World-Class Yoga Shalas**
*   Practice in our stunning, open-air shalas, embraced by the soothing sounds of nature.
*   Enjoy breathtaking views of the Mandrem River or the Arabian Sea from your yoga mat.
*   Our fully-equipped spaces are designed to support a wide variety of yoga styles, workshops, and trainings.

**Unique Eco-Chic Accommodations**
*   Live in harmony with nature in our beautifully designed treehouses, luxurious beach villas, and charming garden rooms.
*   Each room is a unique sanctuary of peace, crafted from natural materials to create a rustic and elegant feel.
*   Fall asleep to the sound of the ocean and wake up surrounded by lush, tropical greenery.

**Organic Garden Restaurant & Cafe**
*   Nourish your body and soul with delicious, creative, and healthy vegetarian cuisine at our renowned garden cafe.
*   Savor our abundant buffets, prepared with love using fresh, organic ingredients, many sourced from our own gardens.
*   Enjoy your meals in a beautiful communal setting that encourages connection and friendship.

**Holistic Spa & Bodywork Centre**
*   Rejuvenate your body in our tranquil spa, a sanctuary dedicated to holistic healing and deep relaxation.
*   Choose from a diverse menu of treatments, including Ayurvedic massages, deep-tissue bodywork, and energy healing.
*   Our highly skilled international therapists will help you release tension and restore your natural balance.

**Tropical Swimming Pool Oasis**
*   Refresh your body and spirit in our beautiful swimming pool, nestled within our lush gardens.
*   Relax on comfortable sun loungers, the perfect spot to unwind, read a book, or simply be still between your classes.
*   Our oasis provides a peaceful and refreshing alternative to the saltwater of the sea.

**Detox & Rejuvenation Centre (Hammam)**
*   Experience the profound purifying benefits of our traditional Turkish Hammam (steam bath).
*   Our dedicated detox facilities are designed to support a deep cleansing of the body and mind.
*   A unique and luxurious amenity that enhances your holistic wellness journey.

**Direct Mandrem Beach & River Access**
*   Step directly from our property onto the soft sands of Mandrem Beach, one of North Goa’s most serene and beautiful coastlines.
*   Enjoy peaceful sunrise walks, magnificent sunsets, and the calming energy of the Arabian Sea.
*   Find moments of quiet contemplation by the tranquil banks of the Mandrem River, which borders our retreat.

**Beautiful Communal & Relaxation Spaces**
*   Connect with our warm community in our many beautiful lounges, gardens, and relaxation areas.
*   True to our name "Ashiyana" (home), these spaces are designed to foster friendship, conversation, and a sense of belonging.
*   Find countless peaceful nooks to journal, meditate, or simply enjoy a moment of quiet solitude.

**The Ashiyana Boutique**
*   Browse our thoughtfully curated boutique, offering a selection of yoga wear, books, and wellness products.
*   Discover unique, locally made crafts, jewellery, and souvenirs to remember your time with us.
*   Find everything you need to support your practice and take a piece of the Ashiyana spirit home with you.
