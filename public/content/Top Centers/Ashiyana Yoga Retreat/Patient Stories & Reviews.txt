### Patient Stories & Reviews

**Rosalind Chambers - Bristol, UK**

*"A Symbolic Threshold to a World of Healing."*

I arrived at Ashiyana carrying eighteen months of accumulated grief, and the iconic bridge crossing over the river felt like a symbolic threshold—the outside world genuinely fell away. The twice-daily Yoga sessions, combined with a one-on-one Holistic Yoga Therapy program, gave my practice a depth I had never accessed before. The natural swimming pool and the hammam between sessions made my recovery feel like pure luxury.

**Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)**
