### Insurance & Payment Info

Making your journey to our healing home clear and accessible.

**Insurance Coverage**

*   As we are a yoga and wellness retreat and not a registered hospital, our programs are typically not covered by health insurance.

*   However, some international insurance providers may offer partial coverage for wellness or therapeutic yoga programs.

*   Our professional administrative team is happy to provide you with all the necessary invoices and documentation to support any claims you wish to make.

*   We strongly advise you to confirm coverage details and reimbursement eligibility directly with your insurance provider before your stay.

**Payment Options**

*   We accept all major international payment methods, including credit/debit cards and international bank transfers.

*   Our pricing is completely transparent, with all costs for your all-inclusive program clearly outlined at the time of booking.

*   Our all-inclusive packages typically cover your accommodation, all buffet meals, and scheduled group wellness activities like yoga and meditation.

*   You will receive a detailed cost estimate before you confirm your booking, with absolutely no hidden charges.

**For International Patients**

International guests are encouraged to have a detailed discussion with their insurance providers regarding coverage for destination wellness retreats and therapeutic yoga programs. Our team is highly experienced in providing the necessary documentation to assist you in filing your claims upon your return home.
