### The Visionary Founder

### Chris Nelson
Founder, Wellbeing Coach & Author

Chris Nelson is the heart and visionary force behind Ashiyana. An experienced wellbeing coach, author, and entrepreneur, his journey began in 2004 when he discovered a magical palm grove in Mandrem. He dreamt of creating a sanctuary—a true "home"—where people from all over the world could feel safe, nourished, and free to reconnect with themselves. This dream is now a vibrant reality, where he continues to inspire guests through his signature programs like the Ashiyana Journey™ and the BEST ME Journey™.
