### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

Our signature "Yoga Holiday" is completely flexible, and you can stay for as long as you like. For our more structured retreats, like the BEST ME Journey or a detox program, a minimum stay of 7 nights is typically recommended to experience the full benefits.

**2. What is the atmosphere at Ashiyana like? Is it a strict ashram?**

Ashiyana uniquely blends the serene, spiritual energy of an ashram with the comfort and freedom of a boutique retreat. It is a place of deep peace and authenticity, but without the rigid rules of a traditional ashram. Our name means 'home,' and that is the warm, welcoming atmosphere we cultivate.

**3. I am a complete beginner in Yoga. Is this retreat suitable for me?**

Absolutely. Ashiyana is the perfect place for beginners. Our twice-daily drop-in yoga classes are designed for all levels, and our experienced teachers provide personal attention and modifications to ensure the practice is safe, enjoyable, and perfectly suited to your body.

**4. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a delicious and nourishing plant-based vegetarian buffet for brunch and dinner. Our "farm-to-table" approach uses fresh, local, and organic ingredients. The food is a vital and consistently praised part of the Ashiyana experience, designed to be both healthy and incredibly flavorful.

**5. What is a "Yoga Holiday"?**

Our signature "Yoga Holiday" is a unique concept that offers you complete freedom. There is no rigid schedule. You are free to join our twice-daily yoga classes, participate in our evening activities, or simply rest in a hammock as you wish. It is a journey designed to help you find your own natural rhythm.

**6. Do you offer Ayurvedic treatments like Panchakarma?**

Yes, we have a beautiful on-site Ayurvedic spa where you can book a wide range of classical treatments, including *Shirodhara*, *Abhyanga*, and even a full *Panchakarma* detox program. These are booked separately and are a wonderful complement to your yoga journey.

**7. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking. If you are booking an Ayurvedic program, please inform our resident doctors of all medications during your initial consultation.

**8. How do I get to the retreat?**

We are located in the serene village of Mandrem in North Goa. The nearest airport is Manohar International Airport, Mopa (GOX). The journey to our retreat is marked by the iconic wooden bridge over the Mandrem river, which acts as a beautiful threshold to our healing sanctuary.

**9. Can a companion stay with me if they are not interested in yoga?**

Yes, your companion is welcome to stay with you and enjoy our serene environment, delicious food, and the beautiful Mandrem beach. They can choose to participate in as many or as few of the daily activities as they wish.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing suitable for both yoga practice and Goa's warm climate. Please also bring swimwear for our natural pool, any personal toiletries, and your medical records. We will provide towels, yoga mats, and a welcoming, homely atmosphere.
