### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What does NABH accreditation signify?**

NABH (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers) is India's highest quality certification. For you, it is a guarantee of safety, hygiene, and standardized medical protocols. It means you receive treatments in an environment that meets the stringent quality standards of a premium hospital, ensuring your well-being is in the best hands.

**2. What is the best time to visit Kerala for Ayurvedic treatment?**

While Kerala's climate is pleasant year-round, the monsoon season (June to September) is traditionally considered the most potent time for Ayurvedic treatments. The cool, moist, and dust-free atmosphere helps the body's pores to open up, making it more receptive to the healing oils and therapies.

**3. What is the minimum stay recommended for a treatment program?**

For a comprehensive program like *Panchakarma*, a minimum stay of 14 to 21 days is essential for effective and lasting results. Shorter stays of 7 to 10 days are available for rejuvenation and de-stress programs. The exact duration will be recommended by our doctors after your initial consultation.

**4. How do I get to Ayursoma?**

The nearest international airport is Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) International Airport (TRV), which is approximately 21 km away. We are happy to arrange a private airport transfer for you upon request to ensure a smooth and comfortable arrival at our sanctuary.

**5. Are all meals included in the program?**

Yes, all our residential wellness programs include accommodation and three customized Ayurvedic meals per day (breakfast, lunch, and dinner). Your diet is an integral part of your treatment and will be personally prescribed by our doctors based on your specific *dosha* and health condition.

**6. What is included in a typical wellness package?**

A typical all-inclusive package at Ayursoma includes your private accommodation, initial and daily doctor consultations, all prescribed Ayurvedic therapies, internal herbal medicines, daily yoga and meditation sessions, and personalized Ayurvedic meals.
