### Treatment Process & Patient Journey

Your path to wellness at Ayursoma is a structured, personal, and compassionate journey. We guide you through every stage, ensuring you feel supported and informed from your first consultation to your continued well-being at home.

1.  **In-Depth Initial Consultation**
    Your journey begins with a comprehensive, one-on-one consultation with our expert Vaidya. This session focuses on understanding your unique body constitution (*Prakriti*), current health imbalances (*Vikriti*), and personal wellness goals.

    *   We employ traditional diagnostic methods to pinpoint the root cause of your concerns.
    *   This is a collaborative process where we listen carefully to your health history.
    *   We establish a clear and achievable set of health objectives together.

2.  **Creating Your Personal Wellness Blueprint**
    Based on the initial diagnosis, our doctors design a completely personalized treatment blueprint (*Chikitsa*) just for you. This detailed plan is your unique roadmap back to a state of vibrant health and natural balance.

    *   It outlines the specific sequence of therapies you will receive.
    *   It includes your bespoke herbal medicine plan and dietary guidelines.
    *   Every element is selected to work in perfect synergy for your body.

3.  **Preparation & Gentle Detoxification**
    The initial phase of your treatment focuses on preparing your body for deep cleansing. This preparatory stage, known as *Purvakarma*, involves therapies that gently loosen and liquefy accumulated toxins (*Ama*) from your tissues.

    *   This stage includes specialized oil massages (*Snehana*) and steam therapies (*Swedana*).
    *   It is a crucial step that ensures the main detoxification is both effective and comfortable.
    *   This process readies the toxins for easy elimination from the body.

4.  **Core Healing & Cleansing Therapies**
    This is the heart of your journey, where the main cleansing and healing therapies are administered. Guided by your wellness blueprint, our skilled therapists perform these treatments with precision, care, and mindful attention.

    *   These core treatments may include *Virechana*, *Vasti*, or other classical purification methods.
    *   Each therapy is designed to expel toxins and restore the balance of your *Doshas*.
    *   Our compassionate team ensures you are comfortable and supported throughout this phase.

5.  **Nourishment, Rejuvenation & Integration**
    Following the deep cleansing, the focus shifts to nourishing and rejuvenating your entire system. This phase, known as *Paschat Karma*, helps to rebuild your strength, bolster your immunity, and solidify the benefits of your treatment.

    *   It includes rejuvenating therapies, a restorative diet, and specific herbal formulations (*Rasayanas*).
    *   This is a vital stage for restoring your digestive fire (*Agni*) and energy levels (*Ojas*).
    *   Daily yoga and meditation sessions help to integrate the healing on a mind-body level.

6.  **Empowerment & Continued Wellness Plan**
    Our commitment to you extends beyond your stay. Before you depart, we provide you with a detailed and practical plan to continue your wellness journey at home, empowering you to maintain your newfound health and vitality.

    *   You receive personalized guidance on diet, lifestyle routines (*Dinacharya*), and herbal supplements.
    *   We ensure you have the knowledge to make conscious, healthy choices every day.
    *   Our team remains available for follow-up consultations to support your long-term well-being.
