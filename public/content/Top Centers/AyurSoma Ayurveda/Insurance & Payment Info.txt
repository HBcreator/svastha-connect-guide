### Insurance & Payment Info

Making your path to healing accessible with flexible payment and insurance support.

**Payment Options**

*   To confirm your booking and reserve your place, an advance payment is required. The final balance is due upon your arrival at the center.
*   We accept payments via international bank transfers and all major credit cards for your convenience.
*   Our pricing is transparent, with all treatment costs, accommodation, and meal charges clearly outlined before you confirm your stay.
*   Our reservations team will provide clear instructions and support for making your advance payment securely.

**Insurance Coverage**

*   Ayursoma is an NABH-accredited Ayurvedic hospital, which may make your treatment costs eligible for reimbursement from your health insurance provider.
*   Our administrative team is highly experienced in providing all necessary medical documentation, detailed invoices, and hospital records to support your insurance claims process.
*   We strongly recommend you contact your insurance provider beforehand to confirm the specific details and extent of coverage for treatments at an Ayurvedic hospital in India.

**For International Guests**

We have a long and proud history of welcoming guests from all over the world. We encourage international guests to check with their home insurance providers regarding coverage for Ayurvedic treatments abroad. We are fully equipped to provide the necessary paperwork to facilitate your international claims.
