**About AyurSoma**
Welcome to AyurSoma, a premium healing sanctuary where the legendary heritage of the Somatheeram Ayurveda Group meets the tranquil beauty of the Kerala coast. As the newest luxury offering from the pioneers of Ayurvedic hospitality, AyurSoma combines over three decades of healing expertise with a modern, elegant, and serene environment. Nestled on the pristine shores of the Arabian Sea, our retreat is a place to experience the profound wisdom of Ayurveda in an atmosphere of unparalleled comfort and authentic care.



### Accreditations & Recognition

1.   We are backed by the **Somatheeram Ayurveda Group**, the world's first and most awarded Ayurvedic resort, with a legacy of over 30 years and **ten 'Best Ayurvedic Centre' awards**.

2.   Our retreat is fully **NABH-accredited**, which is the gold standard for patient safety and clinical excellence, assuring you of the highest international standards of care.

3.   We hold the prestigious **Green Leaf certification** from the Government of Kerala, which is your guarantee of receiving pure, traditional, and authentic Ayurvedic treatments.

4.   Our greatest recognition is the **trust of our global community** of guests, who choose us for our unique blend of authentic healing, luxury, and the proven Somatheeram legacy.



### Philosophy & Approach

1.   Our philosophy is to provide **authentic, classical Ayurveda in a modern, luxury setting**. We honor the timeless wisdom of this ancient science while ensuring your absolute comfort.

2.   We believe in **deep personalization**. We reject the one-size-fits-all model; your entire healing journey is crafted exclusively for you after a deep, traditional diagnosis by our expert physicians.

3.   Our focus is on treating the **root cause of your illness**, not just the symptoms, to provide you with a gradual, sustainable transformation and lasting health.

4.   We believe our **serene beachfront environment** is a powerful medicine in itself. The gentle sound of the waves and the fresh sea air are an integral part of your healing journey.
