### Facilities & Amenities

To ensure your healing journey is comfortable, restorative, and holistic, Ayursoma provides a thoughtfully curated range of facilities.

 **Serene Beachside Location**
    Our center is peacefully situated in a lush, green landscape, just a short, pleasant walk from the serene Chowara beach. This pristine coastal environment provides a naturally therapeutic and calming atmosphere for your healing journey.

    *   Enjoy the soothing sounds of the ocean waves and gentle sea breeze.
    *   The pure, unpolluted coastal air invigorates the body and promotes deep relaxation.
    *   The natural beauty of the surroundings offers a perfect escape from a stressful world.

 **Eco-Friendly & Comfortable Villas**
    We offer comfortable and spacious accommodation in our thoughtfully designed eco-friendly villas. Each living space is a private haven of peace, built to be in harmony with nature while providing modern comforts for a restorative stay.

    *   Rooms are well-ventilated, filled with natural light, and feature private verandas.
    *   Each villa is equipped with modern amenities, including an attached, hygienic bathroom.
    *   The tranquil setting ensures you enjoy deep, restful sleep every night.

 **Authentic Kerala Treatment Centre**
    Our treatment center is a sanctum of healing, fully equipped to administer all classical Keraliya therapies. We maintain the highest standards of hygiene and authenticity, ensuring every treatment is both safe and profoundly effective.

    *   We have separate, private therapy rooms for our male and female guests.
    *   The center is managed by our experienced Vaidyas and highly skilled therapists.
    *   We use only high-quality, authentic herbal oils and preparations for all treatments.

  **Dedicated Yoga & Meditation Hall**
    To support the holistic integration of mind and body, we have a serene, dedicated hall for daily yoga and meditation sessions. This peaceful space is designed to help you deepen your practice in a calm and focused environment.

    *   The spacious hall offers a quiet and inspiring ambiance for your practice.
    *   Daily classes are guided by our experienced and certified yoga instructors.
    *   It provides the perfect setting to learn and practice postures, pranayama, and meditation.

  **Ayurvedic Vegetarian Restaurant**
    Our restaurant is central to your healing, serving delicious and wholesome Ayurvedic vegetarian cuisine. Every meal is considered medicine, carefully prepared to support your specific treatment plan and delight your senses.

    *   Enjoy a diet customized by our doctors to align with your personal *Dosha* balance.
    *   Meals are prepared with fresh, locally sourced vegetables, fruits, and grains.
    *   Our *sattvic* food is designed to be easy to digest, deeply nourishing, and full of flavor.

  **Pristine Swimming Pool**
    Nestled within our lush gardens is a pristine swimming pool, offering a perfect space for gentle exercise and relaxation. It provides a refreshing way to unwind and enjoy the tranquil beauty of the resort.

    *   The pool is meticulously maintained to the highest standards of cleanliness.
    *   It offers a calm and refreshing escape during your leisure time.
    *   Relax by the poolside and soak in the serene, tropical atmosphere.

  **Lush Tropical Gardens & Pathways**
    The entire property is a beautifully landscaped tropical garden, filled with native plants, coconut groves, and vibrant flowers. Our serene pathways are perfect for quiet walks and mindful moments of reflection.

    *   Connect with nature as an essential part of your healing process.
    *   The green, natural surroundings help to reduce stress and promote mental peace.
    *   Find quiet corners to read, meditate, or simply be present in the moment.

  **Library & Reading Lounge**
    To enrich your understanding of the ancient sciences, we provide a quiet and comfortable library space. It is stocked with a curated collection of books on Ayurveda, yoga, Indian philosophy, and spiritual well-being.

    *   Deepen your knowledge and complement your practical healing experience.
    *   The lounge offers a peaceful sanctuary for study, reading, and quiet contemplation.
    *   A perfect spot to unwind and nourish your mind during your stay.

  **Comprehensive Guest Support**
    While our focus is on traditional healing, we ensure your entire experience is seamless and stress-free with a range of supportive services. Our friendly team is always available to cater to your needs with warmth and efficiency.

    *   We provide complimentary Wi-Fi in common areas for your convenience.
    *   We are happy to arrange airport transfers and assist with local travel plans.
    *   Our attentive front desk staff is dedicated to making your stay as comfortable as possible.
