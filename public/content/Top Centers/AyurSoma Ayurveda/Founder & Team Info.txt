**Mr. Baby Mathew**

Chairman & Managing Director

Mr. Baby Mathew is the visionary pioneer who introduced the world to the concept of the Ayurvedic resort. In 1985, he founded the Somatheeram Group, realizing a shared dream with his late brother, Dr. Polly Mathew, who was instrumental in bringing authentic Ayurveda to Europe. As the President of the Kerala Travel Mart Society, Mr. Baby Mathew's leadership and passion have made authentic, holistic healing accessible to a global community.
