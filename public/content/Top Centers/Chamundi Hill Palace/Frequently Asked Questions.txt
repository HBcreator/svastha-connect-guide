### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness programs, we recommend a minimum of 7 days. For our medical treatment programs, a stay of 14 to 28 days is typically required for deep, effective, and lasting healing, as advised by our physicians based on your specific condition.

**2. What is the history of the palace? Is it a real palace?**

Yes, this is a property with a true royal heritage. It was formerly known as "Udayamangalam Palace" and is the ancestral home of our founder's family, who are connected to the royal lineage of the Travancore kings. It has been thoughtfully transformed into a healing sanctuary to share its unique and tranquil energy.

**3. What is the accommodation like?**

You will be staying in the exclusive and historic chambers of a former royal retreat. The accommodations are designed to be comfortable, serene, and conducive to healing, blending classic elegance with essential modern comforts while honoring the heritage of the palace.

**4. How do I get to the palace? Is it difficult to reach?**

Our palace is located high up in the serene mountains, a beautiful and scenic journey from the nearest airport. The location and the journey are part of the therapeutic experience. We are happy to coordinate your transportation arrangements to ensure your arrival is seamless and stress-free.

**5. Who will be my treating physician?**

Your healing journey will be personally guided and supervised by our expert Chief Physician, Dr. Sheela, and her dedicated team. They bring a wealth of clinical experience and a deep commitment to the principles of classical Ayurveda.

**6. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a simple, wholesome, and delicious vegetarian diet based on classical Ayurvedic principles. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are customized by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha* and health condition, using fresh, local ingredients.

**7. Is this a suitable place for a casual holiday?**

While the environment is as serene and beautiful as any holiday destination, our primary focus is on deep, authentic Ayurvedic treatment. We are a sanctuary for those who are serious about their health and are seeking a peaceful environment for their healing journey.

**8. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable cotton clothing suitable for a mountain climate, which can be cooler in the evenings. Please also bring any personal toiletries, your medical records, and a good book. We will provide towels and any necessary attire for your treatments.

**9. Can my family or a companion stay with me?**

Yes, companions are welcome to stay with you. They can enjoy the serene environment and our healthy Ayurvedic cuisine. Please note that treatments are only provided to those who have undergone a consultation and have a prescribed wellness plan.

**10. Is there internet or Wi-Fi available?**

Limited Wi-Fi is available in designated areas. However, to fully embrace the healing energy of our high-altitude sanctuary, we gently encourage our guests to embrace a digital detox, as disconnecting from screens is a powerful way to calm the mind and deepen the healing experience.
