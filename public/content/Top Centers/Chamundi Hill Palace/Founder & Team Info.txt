**Mrs. Saroja Thampi**

Founder & Custodian of a Royal Legacy

Mrs. Saroja Thampi is the heart and visionary founder of Chamundi Hill Palace. This sanctuary was once her family home, the "Udayamangalam Palace," a property connected to the royal lineage of Raja Udaya Varma. Following her own profound recovery from a serious illness in 1998, she was inspired to transform her home into a haven of healing for others. Her vision was to share the peace and therapeutic energy of this royal land, creating a space where guests could find true, holistic wellness.
