### Treatment Process & Patient Journey

Your personalized path to wellness at Ayurmana is a seamless and supportive experience, guided by nine generations of healing wisdom every step of the way.

**Initial Consultation & Heritage Diagnosis**

Your journey begins with an unhurried, compassionate consultation, rooted in our 1800-year healing heritage.

*   Our process starts with an honest pre-arrival consultation to ensure we are the right fit for your healing.

*   You will be personally guided by a physician from the esteemed Dharma healing family.

*   We use traditional diagnostics to uncover your unique constitution (*Prakriti*) and the true root cause of your illness.

**Your Personal Healing Blueprint**

Based on our deep diagnosis, our physicians will craft your exclusive roadmap to health, a path unique to you.

*   This is not a generic package; it is your personal medical prescription for deep and lasting health.

*   Your blueprint details your bespoke schedule of classical therapies and any necessary *Panchakarma*.

*   It includes a tailored diet and potent, secret family-formulated herbal medicines.

**Daily Healing with Unmatched Purity**

This is where your deep healing begins, with daily therapies delivered with skill and the purest ingredients.

*   You will receive your prescribed treatments each day from our highly skilled and compassionate therapists.

*   All therapies are performed using our own authentic, high-potency medicines, freshly prepared in our *Vaidyasala*.

*   Your physician personally consults with you regularly to monitor your progress and tailor your care.

**Therapeutic Cuisine on the Sacred Hill**

We believe that food is powerful medicine, and your meals are a delicious and vital part of your healing journey.

*   Enjoy delicious, wholesome vegetarian meals designed according to classical Ayurvedic principles.

*   Your meals are customized by our physicians to support your specific treatment plan and balance your *doshas*.

*   This nourishing cuisine helps to heal your body from the inside out, amplifying the effects of your therapies.

**Mind-Body Harmony on Dharmagiri**

Your wellness journey extends beyond the treatment room into the serene, healing energy of Dharmagiri Hill.

*   Harmonize your mind and body with daily therapeutic Yoga and guided meditation sessions.

*   Reconnect with nature through peaceful walks in our lush gardens, surrounded by birdsong.

*   The tranquil, spiritual energy of our sacred hilltop is a powerful medicine in itself, deepening your healing.

**Empowerment for Your Journey Home**

Our support for you doesn’t end when you leave; we empower you with the tools for a lifetime of wellness.

*   Before you depart, you will receive a comprehensive final consultation with your physician.

*   We will provide you with a detailed plan, including dietary and lifestyle advice, to continue your progress at home.

*   You leave not just rejuvenated, but empowered with the wisdom of our heritage to maintain your well-being.
