### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness programs, we recommend a minimum of 7 days. For our medical treatment programs, a stay of 14 to 28 days is typically required for deep, effective, and lasting healing, as advised by our physicians based on your specific condition.

**2. What does your "nine-generation legacy" mean for my treatment?**

It means your treatment is not based on theory, but on a living legacy of healing wisdom that is 1800 years old. Our physicians have an intuitive understanding that comes from generations of clinical experience, and they often use proprietary family formulations that are exceptionally effective and cannot be found anywhere else.

**3. What is the accommodation like? Is it a luxury resort?**

Our focus is 100% on your healing, not on luxury frills. Our accommodations are simple, clean, comfortable, and designed to provide a peaceful environment for rest on our sacred hilltop. We are a genuine healing hospital, not a 5-star hotel, which allows us to keep our focus entirely on your therapeutic outcome.

**4. What is so special about the Dharmagiri Hill location?**

Dharmagiri is the sacred "hill of masters," a historic meditation retreat for enlightened sages. The serene, pollution-free environment is a powerful medicine in itself, and guests consistently report that simply being on this hill, surrounded by nature and a unique spiritual energy, accelerates their recovery in profound ways.

**5. Are the treatments really personalized?**

Yes, completely. There are no one-size-fits-all packages here. Following your comprehensive initial consultation with our physicians, your entire treatment plan—from the specific family-formulated oils we use to your daily diet—is crafted exclusively for you.

**6. Do you really treat complex conditions like Multiple Sclerosis (MS)?**

Yes. Our family of physicians has developed a renowned expertise over generations in managing complex and challenging conditions where conventional medicine may offer limited hope. We have a proven track record of helping patients with MS, chronic arthritis, disc prolapse, and other serious neurological and autoimmune disorders.

**7. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our Ayurvedic physicians will review them during your initial consultation and advise you on how to manage them safely during your stay.

**8. How do I get to the centre?**

We are located in Thiruvananthapuram (Trivandrum), Kerala, on the sacred Dharmagiri Hill. We are conveniently accessible from the Trivandrum International Airport (TRV). We are happy to help you coordinate your airport transfers for a seamless and stress-free journey.

**9. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a simple, wholesome, and delicious vegetarian diet based on classical Ayurvedic principles. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are customized by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha* and health condition.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable cotton clothing suitable for a warm climate and for wearing during yoga sessions. Please also bring any personal toiletries, your medical records, and a good book. We will provide towels and any necessary attire for your treatments.
