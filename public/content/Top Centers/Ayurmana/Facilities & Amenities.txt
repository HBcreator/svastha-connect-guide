### Facilities & Amenities

At Ayurmana, every facility is thoughtfully designed to support your healing journey in an environment of authenticity and serenity.

**Healing Accommodations**

Rest and recover in clean, comfortable rooms designed to be a sanctuary for pure healing.

*   Stay in simple, clean, and comfortable rooms nestled in our serene hilltop environment.

*   Our accommodations are purposefully designed for rest and healing, free from modern distractions.

*   Enjoy the natural light, fresh country air, and peaceful ambiance from your private space.

**Authentic Treatment Rooms**

Experience traditional therapies in spaces designed for your comfort, privacy, and authentic healing.

*   Heal in comfort in our dedicated, private therapy rooms for both men and women.

*   Our spaces are equipped for classical Ayurvedic procedures, ensuring maximum therapeutic benefit.

*   We maintain the highest standards of hygiene and safety for your complete peace of mind.

**Physician Consultation Chambers**

A private space for deep, personal conversations with a physician from our nine-generation healing family.

*   This is where your unique healing journey is crafted through a deep, traditional diagnosis.

*   Experience traditional diagnostics like pulse reading (*Nadi Pariksha*) with our master physicians.

*   Benefit from our approach of regular, unhurried consultations to monitor your progress.

**The In-House Vaidyasala (Pharmacy)**

Experience the unmatched purity and potency of medicines from our own traditional herbal pharmacy.

*   All our treatments use our unique and secret family formulations, perfected over centuries.

*   Our medicated oils and herbal decoctions are prepared fresh in small batches, ensuring maximum potency.

*   We use high-quality herbs, handpicked from across Kerala's pristine landscapes.

**Therapeutic Ayurvedic Dining**

Savor delicious meals where food is treated as the first and most powerful medicine.

*   Enjoy simple, wholesome, and delicious vegetarian meals, customized by our physicians for you.

*   Your meals are designed to support your specific treatment plan and balance your *doshas*.

*   This nourishing cuisine helps to heal your body from the inside out, amplifying your results.

**Yoga & Meditation on the Hill**

Harmonize your mind and body in our serene spaces, blessed with the healing energy of Dharmagiri Hill.

*   Our daily yoga sessions are therapeutic, designed to complement your medical treatments.

*   Our expert instructors guide you through practices that calm the mind and accelerate healing.

*   Practice in the fresh country air, surrounded by birdsong and stunning countryside views.

**The Healing Hilltop Environment**

Heal in a naturally therapeutic environment, perched atop the sacred Dharmagiri, the "hill of masters."

*   Our location is a historic meditation retreat for masters, believed to have its own healing properties.

*   The serene, pollution-free atmosphere is a powerful medicine in itself, calming your nervous system.

*   This powerful connection with nature is an integral and essential part of your healing journey.

**Housekeeping & Support Services**

Feel completely supported with our attentive, professional, and discreet service.

*   Relax in a clean and hygienic environment with our meticulous daily housekeeping.

*   Our attentive and caring staff are always available to support your needs with a personal touch.

Enjoy a seamless journey to our secluded and sacred hilltop sanctuary.
