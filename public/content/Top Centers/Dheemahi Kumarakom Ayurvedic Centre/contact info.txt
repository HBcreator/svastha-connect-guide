### Distance from Major Locations

*   From Cochin International Airport (COK): Approx. 90 km mob- A scenic 2 to 2.5-hour drive through the beautiful Kerala countryside.

*   From Kottayam Railway Station (KTYM): Approx. 16 km mob- Our closest and most convenient railway station, just a 30 to 35-minute drive away.

*   From Alleppey (Alappuzha): Approx. 40 km mob- A 45 to 60-minute drive, or you can opt for a delightful backwater boat ride for a truly immersive experience.

*   From Trivandrum International Airport (TRV): Approx. 175 km mob- A 3.5 to 4-hour drive, offering another travel option for our guests.




**Transportation Services**

Complimentary pick-up and drop services available for guests from Mumbai Airport and Panvel area. Advance booking required with flight/train details. Private taxi arrangements can also be coordinated through the resort for additional charges.
