### Insurance & Payment Info

Making your path to authentic healing accessible with clear and straightforward payment support.

**Payment Options**

*   To confirm your booking for a treatment or course, a deposit is required in advance. The remaining balance is typically due upon arrival.
*   We accept payments through international bank transfers, Wise, and other secure online payment methods.
*   All program costs are transparent and will be clearly communicated to you at the time of booking, with no hidden charges.
*   Please inquire about any specific payment arrangements or for detailed instructions on how to transfer your deposit.

**Insurance Coverage**

*   While we do not offer direct billing to insurance providers, many international and Indian health insurance policies may reimburse the cost of your Ayurvedic treatments.
*   Our team is experienced in providing all the necessary medical documentation, detailed invoices, and treatment certificates required to support your insurance claims.
*   We strongly advise you to contact your insurance provider beforehand to confirm the details and extent of your coverage for therapeutic Ayurvedic programs.

**For International Patients**

We warmly welcome guests from around the globe. International patients are encouraged to check with their home insurance providers regarding coverage for Ayurvedic treatments received in India. Our administrative team is dedicated to providing the proper documentation to facilitate your reimbursement process.
