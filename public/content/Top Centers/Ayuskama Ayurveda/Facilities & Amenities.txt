### Facilities & Amenities
Our center is designed to be a true sanctuary where every detail is aligned with the principles of authentic wellness, allowing you to relax and focus entirely on your health.

  **Serene Himalayan Location**
    Our center is peacefully situated in the spiritual town of Dharamshala, nestled in the lap of the majestic Dhauladhar Himalayas. This powerful and pristine environment is a natural healer, providing the perfect backdrop for your wellness journey.

    *   Benefit from the pure, oxygen-rich mountain air that enhances vitality.
    *   Experience deep tranquility, far from the noise and pollution of city life.
    *   Enjoy breathtaking views that inspire a natural sense of peace and well-being.

  **Comfortable & Mindful Accommodation**
    We provide clean, comfortable, and well-furnished rooms designed to be your personal haven of peace. The living spaces are simple yet equipped with all necessary amenities, ensuring a restful and restorative stay.

    *   Each room features an attached, private bathroom with modern fittings.
    *   The minimalist decor is designed to promote a calm and uncluttered mind.
    *   Enjoy a safe, secure, and private space to support your healing process.

  **Authentic Panchakarma Treatment Center**
    Our treatment center is fully equipped to administer all classical Panchakarma therapies in their most authentic form. We maintain the highest standards of hygiene and procedural accuracy to ensure your treatments are both safe and effective.

    *   Separate therapy rooms for male and female guests ensure complete privacy and comfort.
    *   The center is managed by our experienced Vaidyas and skilled therapists.
    *   We use only our self-prepared, high-quality herbal oils for all treatments.

  **Dedicated Yoga & Meditation Hall**
    To support the holistic nature of your healing, we have a spacious and dedicated hall for daily yoga and meditation sessions. This serene space is designed to help you deepen your practice and connect with your inner self.

    *   The hall is filled with natural light and offers a peaceful ambiance.
    *   Regularly scheduled classes are guided by our experienced yoga instructors.
    *   It provides a perfect, quiet environment for both group sessions and personal practice.

  **Ayurvedic Sattvic Kitchen**
    Our kitchen is the heart of our healing philosophy, serving delicious and wholesome Ayurvedic food. Every meal is considered a part of your treatment, prepared with fresh, locally sourced ingredients and tailored to your specific needs.

    *   Enjoy a pure vegetarian, *sattvic* diet that is easy to digest and deeply nourishing.
    *   Your meals are customized by our doctors to align with your specific *Dosha* and treatment plan.
    *   Food is cooked with Himalayan herbs, love, and mindful attention.

  **In-House Herbal Pharmacy**
    The quality of herbal preparations is paramount to the success of any treatment. We have our own in-house pharmacy where we prepare fresh oils, decoctions, and other formulations according to classical Ayurvedic texts.

    *   This ensures the absolute purity, potency, and authenticity of the medicines you receive.
    *   Herbs are ethically sourced from the pristine environment of the Himalayas.
    *   Our commitment to quality guarantees you receive the maximum therapeutic benefits.

  **Peaceful Garden & Natural Spaces**
    The center is surrounded by lush greenery and natural beauty, providing ample space for quiet reflection and relaxation. We encourage our guests to connect with nature as an essential part of their healing journey.

    *   Enjoy quiet walks and mindful moments in our peaceful garden areas.
    *   The natural surroundings help to reduce stress and promote mental clarity.
    *   These spaces are perfect for reading, gentle strolls, or simply being.

  **Ayurvedic Library & Learning Resources**
    As an educational institute, we believe in empowering our guests through knowledge. We provide access to a curated library of books on Ayurveda, yoga, and philosophy to enrich your understanding.

    *   Deepen your knowledge of the ancient sciences during your stay.
    *   Provides a quiet space for study and contemplation.
    *   Our doctors are always available to answer questions and share their wisdom.

  **Essential Modern Conveniences**
    While our focus is on traditional healing, we ensure your stay is comfortable with essential modern amenities. We provide the necessary conveniences to make your experience seamless and stress-free.

    *   The center is equipped with Wi-Fi to help you stay connected with loved ones.
    *   We can assist with travel arrangements, including airport transfers.
    *   Our friendly front desk staff is always available to cater to your needs.
