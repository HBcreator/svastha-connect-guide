### Founder & Team Info
The healing power of Ayuskama lies in the hands of its people. 

**Dr. Arun Sharma - The Visionary Founder**

Dr. Arun Sharma is the heart and driving force behind Ayuskama Ayurveda. With a deep-rooted passion for classical Ayurveda, he established the center with a clear vision: to make the authentic healing science of Ayurveda, particularly the profound techniques of Panchakarma, accessible to all in the serene and spiritually charged environment of the Himalayas.

*   He is a highly qualified Ayurvedic doctor (B.A.M.S.) with specialized certifications in Panchakarma.
*   Dr. Sharma is a renowned expert in *Nadi Pariksha* (Pulse Diagnosis), allowing for incredibly precise root-cause analysis.
*   His extensive experience in various prestigious Ayurvedic centers across India informs the high standard of care at Ayuskama.

**Our Team of Expert Vaidyas**

Under the leadership of Dr. Sharma, our team of Ayurvedic doctors (Vaidyas) embodies a blend of classical knowledge and compassionate care. They are the primary architects of your healing journey, responsible for creating the personalized wellness blueprints that guide your treatment from start to finish.

*   All our doctors are certified practitioners with a profound understanding of Ayurvedic principles.
*   They specialize in root-cause diagnostics, ensuring your treatment is targeted and effective.
*   They maintain a close, supportive relationship with each patient, carefully monitoring progress every day.

**Our Skilled & Compassionate Therapists**

Our therapists are the gentle hands that administer your healing treatments. Each therapist is meticulously trained in the traditional techniques of Panchakarma and other Ayurvedic therapies, ensuring that every session is performed with precision, respect, and a deep sense of care.

*   They undergo rigorous training in classical Ayurvedic therapeutic procedures.
*   They are skilled in creating a safe, comfortable, and nurturing environment for patients.
*   Their dedication and mindful presence are an integral part of your healing process.

**Our Experienced Yoga & Meditation Gurus**

Completing our holistic approach, our team includes experienced yoga and meditation instructors. They are dedicated to guiding you through practices that complement your Ayurvedic treatments, helping to harmonize your mind, body, and spirit.

*   They are well-versed in various yogic traditions, including Hatha and Ashtanga.
*   They specialize in tailoring yoga practices to suit individual body types and health conditions.
*   Their calming guidance helps you unlock the profound benefits of meditation and pranayama.
