### Treatment Process & Patient Journey

Your path to healing at Ayuskama is a structured and deeply personal journey, rooted in the classical principles of Himalayan Ayurveda.

1.  **Deep-Dive Ayurvedic Consultation**
    Your journey begins with a comprehensive consultation (*Rogi Pariksha*) with our senior Vaidya. This session is a deep exploration of your health history, lifestyle, and personal wellness goals to understand you as a whole person.

    *   We utilize traditional diagnostic methods, including Pulse Diagnosis (*Nadi Pariksha*).
    *   This initial assessment allows us to understand your unique body constitution (*Prakriti*).
    *   We take the time to listen carefully to your concerns and health objectives.

2.  **Pinpointing the Root Cause (Nidaan)**
    Our primary focus is on *Nidaan*—identifying the fundamental root cause of your health issues, not just addressing the surface-level symptoms. This precise diagnosis forms the unshakable foundation of your entire treatment plan.

    *   We analyze the specific imbalances (*Vikriti*) present in your system.
    *   Our Vaidyas provide a clear explanation of their findings and the proposed healing path.
    *   This crucial step ensures that all subsequent treatments are targeted and effective.

3.  **Crafting Your Personalized Wellness Blueprint**
    Based on the diagnosis, we design a completely personalized treatment blueprint (*Chikitsa*) exclusively for you. This is not a generic plan; it is a detailed roadmap to guide your body and mind back to a state of natural balance.

    *   Your plan intelligently combines detoxification therapies (*Shodhan*) and pacifying treatments (*Shaman*).
    *   It includes your bespoke schedule for therapies, herbal medicines, and daily activities.
    *   Every aspect of your blueprint is designed to work in synergy for the best possible results.

4.  **Authentic Healing Therapies**
    You will receive authentic Ayurvedic treatments administered by our highly skilled and compassionate therapists. Each therapy is a sacred practice, performed with focused intention to purify the body, calm the mind, and awaken your innate healing capacity.

    *   We use only our own self-prepared, potent herbal oils and formulations.
    *   Treatments are performed in a specific sequence as outlined in your wellness blueprint.
    *   Our therapeutic team ensures your comfort and safety throughout every session.

5.  **Integrated Diet & Lifestyle Guidance**
    An essential pillar of your healing is a customized diet plan (*Pathya-Apathya*) that aligns with your treatment goals and body type. Food is treated as medicine, prepared to be both nourishing and delicious, supporting the detoxification and rejuvenation process.

    *   Our experts provide detailed guidance on supportive lifestyle routines (*Dinacharya*).
    *   You will learn which foods support your healing and which to avoid.
    *   This empowers you with the practical knowledge to make sustainable changes.

6.  **Empowerment & Continued Support**
    Our commitment to your well-being extends beyond your stay. We equip you with a clear and sustainable plan for continued wellness at home and offer ongoing support to ensure you maintain the benefits you have gained.

    *   You will receive a detailed post-treatment plan, including diet and lifestyle advice.
    *   We schedule regular follow-up consultations to monitor your progress.
    *   Our team remains dedicated to supporting your long-term health and well-being.
