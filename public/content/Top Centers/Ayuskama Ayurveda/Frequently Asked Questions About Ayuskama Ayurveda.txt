### Frequently Asked Questions About Ayuskama Ayurveda

**1. What is the best time of year to visit Dharamshala for treatment?**

While our center is open year-round, the most pleasant seasons are Spring (March to June) and Autumn (September to November), offering moderate temperatures and clear skies. The monsoon season (July-August) is also considered an excellent time for Ayurvedic therapies, as the body is more receptive to treatment.

**2. What is the minimum recommended duration for a Panchakarma program?**

For a genuine and effective *Panchakarma* detoxification, a minimum duration of 21 days is highly recommended. This allows for the proper execution of all three stages: the preparatory phase (*Purvakarma*), the main cleansing therapies (*Pradhana Karma*), and the post-treatment rejuvenation phase (*Paschat Karma*).

**3. Do you offer accommodation and meals with the treatment programs?**

Yes, all our residential treatment and educational programs are inclusive of comfortable accommodation and a wholesome Ayurvedic diet. Our rooms are clean and equipped with essential amenities, and meals are specially prepared to align with your specific *dosha* and support your healing process.

**4. What should I bring with me for my stay?**

We recommend bringing comfortable, modest clothing suitable for a healing environment and yoga practice, a pair of indoor slippers, any personal medications you require, and a journal for reflection. It is also advisable to bring layers, as the weather in the Himalayas can change quickly.

**5. Are your courses suitable for beginners with no prior knowledge of Ayurveda?**

Absolutely. Our "Ayurveda Foundation Programme" is specifically designed for beginners and yoga students who wish to gain a solid understanding of the fundamental principles of Ayurveda. Dr. Sharma's teaching style makes complex classical concepts clear, logical, and accessible to everyone.

**6. How do I get to Ayuskama in Dharamshala?**

The nearest airport is Gaggal Airport (DHM), about 45 minutes away by taxi. The nearest major railway station is Pathankot Cantt (PTKC), which is approximately a 3-hour drive. We can assist you in arranging a reliable taxi service from the airport or railway station to our center upon request.
