**About Ayur Bethaniya**
Welcome to Ayur Bethaniya, a modern, multi-specialty Ayurvedic hospital where the timeless wisdom of Ayurveda is intelligently integrated with the precision of modern diagnostics and the support of physiotherapy. We are a **NABH-accredited** and **ISO 9001:2015 certified** hospital, committed to providing a scientifically-validated and evidence-based approach to holistic healing. Our serene environment, combined with our unique integrative method, offers a powerful and trustworthy path to managing a wide range of chronic and complex health conditions.

### Accreditations & Recognition

1.   We are a fully **NABH-accredited** hospital, which is the gold standard for patient safety and clinical excellence, assuring you of the highest international standards of care.

2.   Our hospital is **ISO 9001:2015 certified**, reflecting our commitment to world-class quality management systems and ensuring every process is streamlined and professional.

3.   We hold the prestigious **Green Leaf certification** from the Government of Kerala, which is your guarantee of receiving pure, traditional, and authentic Ayurvedic treatments.

4.   Our greatest recognition is the trust of our guests from over **65 countries**, who choose us for our unique and effective integrative approach to complex health challenges.




### Philosophy & Approach

1.   Our core philosophy is **integrative medicine**. We masterfully blend the holistic healing of Ayurveda with the diagnostic precision of modern medicine and the functional restoration of physiotherapy.

2.   We believe in a **scientific and evidence-based approach**. We use modern diagnostics to gain a clear picture of your health, which then guides your personalized Ayurvedic treatment plan.

3.   Our focus is on treating the **root cause of your illness**, not just the symptoms, to provide you with a gradual, sustainable transformation and lasting health.

4.   We believe in **spiritual well-being** as a key part of healing. Our hospital has its own chapel, and we offer spiritual counseling to support your inner journey.
