### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness programs, a minimum of 7 days is recommended. For our medical treatment programs, a stay of 14 days or more is typically required for deep, effective, and lasting healing, as advised by our integrative team of physicians.

**2. What does "integrative medicine" mean at Ayur Bethaniya?**

It means we use the best of multiple systems for your benefit. We use the diagnostic precision of **modern medicine** (like lab tests) to get a clear picture of your health, which then guides your classical **Ayurvedic** treatment plan. We also seamlessly integrate modern **physiotherapy** to help you recover faster and more completely.

**3. What kind of food will I be served?**

You will be served a delicious and wholesome vegetarian diet. Our approach to nutrition is also integrative, where our expert dieticians and Ayurvedic chefs collaborate to create a personalized meal plan for you that is both scientifically balanced and aligned with classical Ayurvedic principles.

**4. Should I bring my current allopathic medications?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring all your current medications and recent medical records. Our integrative team of both Ayurvedic and modern medicine doctors will review them to create a safe, comprehensive, and synergistic treatment plan for you.

**5. How do I get to the hospital?**

Our hospital is located near Thrissur, Kerala, and is conveniently accessible from the Cochin International Airport (COK). We are happy to help you coordinate your airport transfers and other transportation arrangements for a seamless and stress-free journey.

**6. What is the environment like? Is it a resort or a hospital?**

We are a fully **NABH-accredited hospital**, with our primary focus on providing serious, high-quality medical treatment. However, our serene, green campus provides the peaceful and nurturing ambiance of a wellness retreat, giving you the perfect blend of clinical excellence and natural tranquility.

**7. Do you offer any spiritual or mental health support?**

Yes. We believe healing is holistic and must include the spirit. We are unique in offering an on-site **chapel** for prayer, quiet contemplation, and peace. We also provide spiritual counseling to support your mental and emotional well-being throughout your healing journey.

**8. Can a companion who is not taking treatment stay with me?**

Yes, companions are welcome to stay with you. We can arrange accommodation for them, and they can enjoy our healthy cuisine and the peaceful environment of our hospital campus. Please note that treatments are only provided to those with a prescribed wellness plan.

**9. Is this a suitable place for post-surgery or cancer rehabilitation?**

Yes, our integrative approach is ideal for these conditions. We have dedicated programs for **Cancer Rehabilitation** and post-surgery recovery. The synergy between our gentle Ayurvedic therapies to rebuild vitality and our expert physiotherapy to restore function creates a powerful and supportive healing ecosystem.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable cotton clothing. Please prioritize bringing all your **medical records** and current medications. We will provide towels, basic toiletries, and any necessary attire for your treatments.
