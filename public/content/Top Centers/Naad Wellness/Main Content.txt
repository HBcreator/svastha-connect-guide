**About Naad Wellness**
Welcome to Naad Wellness, **India’s first NABH and QCI accredited wellness centre**, a modern sanctuary of healing conveniently located in the National Capital Region (NCR), close to Delhi. Founded by Manoj Khetan, Naad was envisioned as a space where the search for well-being is guided by scientific principles and measurable results. We are a non-invasive medical wellness facility dedicated to providing a truly holistic and evidence-based path to health, built on our unique foundation of five powerful healing sciences.

1.  We are **India's first NABH-accredited wellness centre**. The National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers (NABH) is your ultimate assurance of the highest international standards in patient safety, quality, and clinical care.

2.  We are also accredited by the **Quality Council of India (QCI)**, a testament to our commitment to excellence, standardized processes, and delivering consistently high-quality wellness outcomes.

3.  We are consistently recognized as a **leading medical wellness destination** in India by prestigious international award bodies for our innovative approach and proven results.

4.  Our greatest recognition is the **trust of our guests**, who choose us for our safe, professional, and medically-supervised environment to address their chronic health concerns.

1.  Our philosophy is to practice **holistic healing with a scientific, evidence-based approach**. We bridge the gap between ancient wisdom and modern validation to create result-oriented wellness journeys.

**Systems of Medicine Practiced**

1.  **Ayurveda & Panchakarma**
    We practice classical Ayurveda for its profound ability to detoxify the body, pacify chronic ailments, and restore the body's natural state of balance.

2.  **Yoga & Meditation**
    We use Yoga as a therapeutic tool to restore the fundamental connection between the mind and body, improve flexibility, and manage stress-related conditions.

3.  **Naturopathy**
    We harness the healing power of nature's elements—such as water, earth, and air—through therapies like hydrotherapy and mud therapy to cleanse the body and support its innate healing mechanisms.

4.  **Acupuncture & Acupressure**
    We integrate the wisdom of Traditional Chinese Medicine to balance the body's vital energy, or *qi*, making it highly effective for pain management, stress, and energy restoration.

5.  **Physiotherapy**
    We include modern, non-invasive physiotherapy to address musculoskeletal issues, restore mobility, correct posture, and improve functional strength, especially in pain management and recovery.
