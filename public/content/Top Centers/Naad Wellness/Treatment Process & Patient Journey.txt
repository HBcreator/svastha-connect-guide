### Treatment Process & Patient Journey

Your journey at Naad is a structured, scientific, and medically supervised path to wellness.

**The Initial Medical Assessment**

Your journey begins with a comprehensive, multi-disciplinary assessment by our team of resident doctors.

*   This includes in-depth consultations and diagnostics.
*   We assess your health from the perspective of all five of our sciences.
*   This establishes a clear medical baseline and defines your wellness goals.

**Your Bespoke Wellness Prescription**

Our doctors then collaborate to create your unique and personalized wellness prescription for your stay.

*   This is not a generic package, but a detailed clinical treatment plan.
*   It intelligently integrates therapies from our five wellness pillars.
*   Every aspect is designed to work in synergy for your specific health needs.

**The Detoxification Phase**

The initial stage of your treatment focuses on cleansing the body using a blend of our scientific and traditional methods.

*   This phase primarily uses Ayurvedic and Naturopathic principles.
*   These therapies safely and effectively remove accumulated toxins.
*   This detoxification prepares your body for deeper healing and repair.

**The Core Therapeutic Interventions**

This is the main healing phase, where you receive your prescribed therapies from our team of specialists.

*   This is where all five pillars are actively used in your daily schedule.
*   Each therapy is administered by a skilled and certified practitioner.
*   Your progress is constantly monitored by our medical team.

**Nourishment & Re-balancing**

Your healing is supported and amplified by a personalized wellness diet and restorative mind-body practices.

*   Enjoy delicious and therapeutic meals prescribed by our doctors.
*   Daily Yoga and Meditation sessions help to re-balance your system.
*   This holistic nourishment is crucial for a complete recovery.

**The Path Forward**

Before you depart, we empower you with a detailed and practical plan to sustain your health gains at home.

*   You receive a personalized post-retreat wellness and diet plan.
*   Our doctors educate you on lifestyle changes for long-term well-being.
*   You leave not just treated, but truly empowered to manage your health.
