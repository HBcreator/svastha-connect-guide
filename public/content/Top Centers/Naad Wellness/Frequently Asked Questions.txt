### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What makes Naad different from a regular spa or resort?**

We are a medically-supervised wellness center, not a spa. This is our most important distinction.

*   We are **NABH-accredited**, which means we operate with the safety and quality standards of a hospital.
*   Your journey is led by a **multi-disciplinary team of doctors** who create a clinical treatment plan.
*   Our focus is on delivering **tangible, measurable health results**, not just temporary relaxation.

**2. How do I get to Naad Wellness?**

Our sanctuary is conveniently located in the National Capital Region (NCR), making it easily accessible.

*   The nearest airport is **Indira Gandhi International Airport in Delhi (DEL)**.
*   We are just a **short 60-90 minute drive** from the airport, saving you a long onward journey.
*   We are happy to arrange a comfortable and reliable private transfer for you upon request.

**3. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

The duration of your stay is determined by your specific health goals and is recommended by our doctors.

*   For deep detoxification or managing a specific health condition, a stay of **7, 14, or 21 days** is recommended.
*   Shorter stays of **3 to 5 nights** are available for a powerful wellness reset or a de-stress program.
*   Our doctors will provide the final recommendation after your initial medical assessment.

**4. What is included in my wellness package?**

Our packages are all-inclusive, allowing you to immerse yourself completely in your healing journey.

*   The package includes your luxurious accommodation and personalized wellness meals.
*   All initial, ongoing, and final consultations with our team of doctors are included.
*   It covers all prescribed therapies and treatments from our five wellness pillars.

**5. What is the best time of year to visit?**

Our facility is a climate-controlled, all-weather sanctuary, making it a perfect destination for healing year-round.

*   The most pleasant outdoor weather is from **October to March**.
*   The monsoon season from **July to September** is traditionally excellent for Ayurvedic therapies.
*   As a modern indoor facility, the external weather has minimal impact on your healing experience.

**6. What kind of clothes should I bring?**

We recommend comfortable, modest clothing suitable for a range of therapeutic and fitness activities.

*   Bring loose-fitting clothes (e.g., kurta, track pants) for your daily therapies.
*   Pack activewear or gym attire for your Yoga and Physiotherapy sessions.
*   Remember swimwear for our temperature-controlled pool and hydrotherapy areas.
