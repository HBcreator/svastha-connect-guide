### Medical Programs

Medically supervised, result-oriented protocols designed to manage specific lifestyle diseases in a safe, NABH-accredited environment.

**Spinal & Back Care**

A comprehensive, non-invasive program designed to manage chronic back and neck pain and improve spinal health.

*   Integrates Physiotherapy, Ayurvedic therapies (*Kati Vasti*), and Acupuncture.
*   Focuses on pain relief, posture correction, and strengthening core muscles.
*   A highly effective program for conditions like Spondylosis and Sciatica.

**Arthritis & Joint Pain**

A program designed to manage the pain and inflammation associated with various forms of arthritis and improve joint mobility.

*   Combines Physiotherapy for mobility with anti-inflammatory Ayurvedic therapies.
*   Uses Acupuncture for effective, non-pharmaceutical pain management.
*   Aims to reduce stiffness and improve the overall quality of life.

**Diabetes Management**

A holistic program designed to support the management of Type 2 Diabetes by improving the body's metabolic function.

*   Integrates a carefully designed naturopathic diet and specific yoga postures.
*   Focuses on improving insulin sensitivity and regulating blood sugar levels.
*   Designed to work with your medical advice for better long-term outcomes.

**Hypertension Care**

A supportive program designed to help manage high blood pressure and improve cardiovascular health through natural, lifestyle-based interventions.

*   Uses yoga, meditation, and acupuncture to calm the nervous system.
*   Includes a specialized low-sodium diet and heart-supportive Ayurvedic herbs.
*   A proactive and preventative approach to nurturing long-term heart health.

**PCOS / PCOD Care**

A dedicated program for women, designed to manage the symptoms of Polycystic Ovary Syndrome through a multi-disciplinary approach.

*   Our five-pillar approach holistically addresses the root hormonal imbalances.
*   Focuses on weight management, regulating menstrual cycles, and reducing stress.
*   An empowering program for taking control of your health naturally.

**Digestive Disorder**

A program designed to treat common but chronic digestive ailments like IBS, acidity, and constipation.

*   Integrates a healing Ayurvedic and Naturopathic diet.
*   Focuses on restoring the digestive fire (*Agni*) and healing the gut lining.
*   Aims to regulate bowel function and reduce inflammatory symptoms.

**Migraine & Headache**

A targeted program offering effective relief from chronic headaches and migraines by addressing their underlying triggers.

*   Integrates Acupuncture, a highly effective therapy for migraine management.
*   Includes Ayurvedic treatments like *Nasya* and stress-reducing therapies.
*   Aims to reduce the frequency, duration, and intensity of episodes.

**Respiratory Wellness**

A supportive program for managing chronic respiratory conditions like asthma and allergies, focusing on strengthening the lungs.

*   Combines therapeutic Yoga (Pranayama) with Naturopathic treatments.
*   Focuses on improving lung capacity and reducing inflammation.
*   Aims to reduce dependency on inhalers and build respiratory resilience.

**Post-Injury Rehab**

A medically supervised program designed to accelerate recovery from musculoskeletal injuries and surgeries.

**Fibromyalgia & Fatigue**
