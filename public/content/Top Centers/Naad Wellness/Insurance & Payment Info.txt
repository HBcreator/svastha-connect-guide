### Insurance & Payment Info

We believe your journey to wellness should be clear and seamless. Our processes are designed with the professionalism and transparency of an accredited medical facility.

**Our Secure Payment Process**

Our process is designed to be straightforward, ensuring your booking is confirmed and your arrival is effortless.

*   An advance deposit is required to confirm your place in our wellness sanctuary.
*   The remaining balance for your program is due upon your arrival at the center.
*   We accept payments through secure international bank transfers and all major credit cards.

**Support for Insurance Claims**

As India's first NABH-accredited wellness center, our services may be eligible for reimbursement from your health insurance provider.

*   Our NABH & QCI accreditations signify the highest standards in clinical care.
*   Our administrative team provides all necessary medical documentation for your claim.
*   We strongly advise you to contact your provider beforehand to confirm your coverage.

**A Note for International Guests**

We have extensive experience in welcoming and assisting guests from every corner of the globe.

*   Check with your home country's insurance provider regarding their reimbursement policies.
*   Our team is proficient in preparing paperwork in a clear and professional format.
*   We are here to support you in making the entire process as smooth as possible.
