### Facilities & Amenities

Our state-of-the-art facility is a purpose-built sanctuary for healing, where every amenity is designed to support your wellness journey with clinical precision and modern comfort.

**Medical & Clinical Spaces**

Our NABH-accredited facility features state-of-the-art clinical spaces for your treatments, ensuring the highest standards of safety and hygiene.

*   Enjoy private, well-equipped rooms for all five of our wellness pillars.
*   Our center uses modern diagnostic tools for accurate assessments.
*   We maintain a sterile, hospital-grade environment for your peace of mind.

**Advanced Hydrotherapy Suites**

We offer an extensive range of hydrotherapy treatments that use the healing power of water to detoxify and relax the body.

*   Experience our traditional Turkish Hammam for deep cleansing.
*   Unwind in our therapeutic whirlpool and bubble pool.
*   These treatments improve circulation and soothe muscle tension.

**Luxurious Wellness Accommodation**

Your accommodation is a private haven of tranquility, designed with your well-being in mind to promote deep, restorative rest.

*   Stay in one of our 39 spacious, elegantly appointed rooms.
*   Each room features a private balcony and health-enhancing lighting.
*   The minimalist design creates a calm, uncluttered healing space.

**The "Ahara" Wellness Restaurant**

Our restaurant serves delicious, therapeutic cuisine where every meal is a prescribed and vital part of your healing journey.

*   Enjoy personalized, gourmet meals designed by our doctors.
*   Our menus are crafted based on Ayurvedic and Naturopathic principles.
*   The cuisine is both incredibly nourishing and a delight for the senses.

**Himalayan Salt Cave**

Experience the benefits of halotherapy in our dedicated Himalayan Salt Cave, a unique therapy for respiratory and skin wellness.

*   Breathe in micro-particles of salt to cleanse the respiratory system.
*   This therapy is highly effective for allergies, asthma, and skin conditions.
*   It is a deeply relaxing and purifying experience.

**Dedicated Fitness & Yoga Studios**

We provide serene and well-equipped spaces for the therapeutic movement that is integral to our wellness philosophy.

*   Practice in our dedicated, peaceful yoga studio with expert guidance.
*   Use our fully-equipped gymnasium for prescribed fitness routines.
*   These spaces support both your physical and mental well-being.

**Outdoor & Recreational Facilities**

Our beautifully landscaped gardens and outdoor facilities provide the perfect space for gentle activity and connecting with nature.

*   Enjoy our all-weather, temperature-controlled swimming pool.
*   Take mindful walks or jogs on our dedicated outdoor track.
*   Relax in our serene gardens and green spaces for quiet contemplation.

**Specialized Therapy Rooms**

Our center features dedicated rooms for our specialized wellness pillars, ensuring you receive focused and effective treatments.

*   We have professional suites for Acupuncture and Acupressure.
*   Our physiotherapy center is equipped with modern rehabilitation tools.
*   These specialized spaces ensure the integrity of each therapy.

**Miniature Golf Course**

We believe in the therapeutic power of gentle recreation and joyful activity as a part of a balanced wellness journey.

*   Enjoy a relaxing round of golf on our on-site miniature course.
*   It provides a wonderful opportunity for mindful, low-impact activity.
*   This unique amenity adds a touch of leisure to your healing routine.
