### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Do I need to be a guest at the ITC Hotel to book a treatment at Kaya Kalp?**

While our residential wellness programs are for in-house guests, we warmly welcome non-resident guests for our individual spa therapies and day packages. We highly recommend booking in advance to ensure availability.

**2. How authentic are your Ayurvedic treatments?**

Our Ayurvedic treatments are deeply authentic. Many of our flagship spas, like at ITC Grand Bharat, have a dedicated **Kerala Suite** with traditional equipment. Your journey will be guided by a qualified resident Ayurvedic physician who will conduct a thorough, personalized consultation before your therapy.

**3. Which ITC Hotel has the best Kaya Kalp Spa for my needs?**

Each Kaya Kalp Spa is unique. For a full, immersive wellness retreat, **ITC Grand Bharat (Gurugram)** is ideal. For a royal, heritage experience, choose **ITC Mughal (Agra)**. For a beachside Goan ritual, **ITC Grand Goa** is perfect. Our wellness concierge can help you choose the best location for your goals.

**4. What kind of food is served during a wellness program?**

You will be served a delicious and healthy wellness cuisine, crafted by our expert ITC chefs in consultation with our doctors. We can cater to specific dietary needs, including sattvic, dosha-specific, and other healthy culinary preferences, all while maintaining our five-star gourmet standards.

**5. I am a business traveler with very little time. What do you recommend?**

We have designed many of our signature therapies, like the 'Imperial Iconic Massage,' to deliver profound results in just 90 minutes. We also offer our unique 'Wellbeing Series'—short, 30-minute educational sessions—perfect for busy travelers looking to gain valuable wellness insights.

**6. Do you offer yoga sessions?**

Yes. Many of our Kaya Kalp spas feature a serene, dedicated **Yoga Sanctum** and a resident Yoga Master. We focus on providing one-on-one, personalized yoga instruction that is tailored to your specific body type, fitness level, and wellness goals.

**7. Can a companion stay with me if they are not on a wellness program?**

Absolutely. Your companion is welcome to stay with you in our luxurious accommodations and enjoy all the world-class facilities of the ITC Hotel, including our award-winning restaurants, swimming pools, and golf courses (where available).

**8. What should I pack for my spa experience?**

We recommend bringing comfortable clothing. For your time at the spa, we will provide you with a robe, slippers, and any necessary attire for your treatments. Swimwear is recommended for our hydrotherapy areas and swimming pools.

**9. Can I book a treatment for a specific health concern, like sleep or stress?**

Yes. Many of our programs are designed for specific concerns. Our 'Sleeep Programme' at ITC Maurya is a great example. For general stress relief, we highly recommend our classical *Shirodhara* therapy, available at most of our Kaya Kalp spas.

**10. What makes Kaya Kalp unique compared to other luxury hotel spas?**

Our uniqueness lies in the perfect fusion of a legendary, art-filled heritage hotel with a truly authentic and deep wellness philosophy. We offer a level of clinical Ayurvedic expertise and personalization that is rare to find in a five-star hotel spa, creating an experience that is both profoundly healing and incredibly luxurious.
