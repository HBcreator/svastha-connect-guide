### Facilities & Amenities

**Kaya Kalp - The Royal Spa**
*   Indulge in one of India's largest and most opulent destination spas, spanning an expansive 35,000 sq. ft.
*   Heal in luxurious, private treatment suites and benefit from our extensive hydrotherapy facilities.
*   Experience a true sanctuary dedicated to ancient Indian wellness rituals and royal rejuvenation.

**All-Suites Palatial Accommodations**
*   Experience unparalleled exclusivity in India’s first all-suites luxury retreat, ensuring ultimate privacy and space.
*   Enjoy the indulgence of a semi-private plunge pool, available with many of our beautifully appointed suites.
*   Rest in palatial comfort with bespoke amenities, dedicated butler service, and stunning architectural details.

**Signature 27-Hole Golf Course**
*   Play in a magnificent setting at our 27-hole Jack Nicklaus signature championship golf course.
*   Enjoy a world-class golfing experience spread across the serene foothills of the Aravalli range.
*   Benefit from our extensive club facilities, professional coaching, and dedicated golf academy.

**Gourmet & Wellness Dining**
*   Embark on a culinary journey at our array of award-winning restaurants, including the European-themed India Room and the regional Apas Promenade.
*   Nourish your body with our pioneering Swasthya Cuisine, a philosophy that blends gourmet flavours with holistic wellness.
*   Enjoy bespoke dining experiences, curated by our master chefs to suit your palate and wellness goals.

**Grand Swimming & Pool Oasis**
*   Refresh your senses in our stunning, temperature-controlled swimming pool, a serene oasis for relaxation.
*   Unwind on comfortable, shaded loungers while enjoying attentive poolside service.
*   Experience the ultimate in privacy with the exclusive plunge pools attached to our luxury suites.

**Yoga & Meditation Pavilions**
*   Find your inner balance in our dedicated, open-air pavilions designed for mindful practice.
*   Practice yoga and meditation in the serene embrace of the ancient Aravalli hills, guided by our experienced instructors.
*   These tranquil spaces are purpose-built to enhance your connection with nature and yourself.

**State-of-the-Art Fitness Centre**
*   Maintain your fitness regimen in our fully equipped, state-of-the-art gymnasium.
*   Benefit from a comprehensive range of modern cardio, strength-training equipment, and free weights.
*   Engage a personal trainer for a customized workout session tailored to your fitness level and goals.

**Expansive 290-Acre Estate**
*   Heal in a sprawling, oxygen-rich sanctuary that offers an unparalleled sense of space and seclusion.
*   Reconnect with nature through mindful walks, jogging, or cycling on dedicated trails across the magnificent estate.
*   The sheer scale of the property ensures you can always find a peaceful corner for quiet contemplation.

**Regal Banqueting & Event Spaces**
*   Host magnificent corporate wellness retreats or grand celebrations in our opulent, pillarless ballroom and elegant boardrooms.
*   Utilize our stunning outdoor venues, including lush lawns and courtyards, for bespoke events and gatherings.
*   Benefit from state-of-the-art technology and a dedicated events team to curate a flawless experience.
