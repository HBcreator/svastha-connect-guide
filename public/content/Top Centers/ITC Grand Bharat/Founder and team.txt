Pioneers of Responsible Luxury

The soul of ITC Grand Bharat is rooted in the century-old legacy of its parent entity, **ITC Limited**. Since launching its hotel division in 1975, ITC has pioneered a unique "Responsible Luxury" philosophy—a commitment to delivering lavish experiences that are in harmony with the environment and local communities. This profound ethos is stewarded today by **Chairman & Managing Director, Sanjiv Puri**, and finds its grandest expression at our retreat, a magnificent tribute to the essence of India.
