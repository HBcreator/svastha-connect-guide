### Insurance & Payment Info

Making your path to our transformative wellness journey clear and accessible.

**Insurance Coverage**

*   While Shreyas is a premier wellness retreat and not a registered hospital, some international insurance providers may offer coverage for wellness, therapeutic yoga, and preventive health programs.

*   Our professional administrative team is happy to provide you with all the necessary documentation, including detailed invoices of your program, to support your claims process.

*   We are committed to helping you navigate the reimbursement process as smoothly as possible.

*   We strongly advise you to confirm coverage details and reimbursement eligibility directly with your insurance provider before your stay.

**Payment Options**

*   We accept all major international payment methods, including credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) and international bank transfers.

*   Our pricing is completely transparent, with all costs for your all-inclusive program clearly outlined at the time of booking.

*   Our all-inclusive packages cover your luxurious accommodation, personalized wellness meals, all scheduled treatments, and daily wellness activities.

*   You will receive a detailed cost estimate before you confirm your booking, with absolutely no hidden charges.

**For International Patients**

International guests are encouraged to have a detailed discussion with their insurance providers regarding coverage for destination wellness retreats, therapeutic yoga, and alternative medicine programs. Our team is highly experienced in providing the necessary documentation to assist you in filing your claims upon your return home.
