### Wellness Programs

Bespoke wellness journeys in a sanctuary of quiet luxury, using the authentic path of Yoga to harmonize mind, body, and soul.

**Authentic Yoga Retreat**

This is the heart of the Shreyas experience, an immersive journey into the complete system of Yoga. It is designed for all levels, from beginners to advanced practitioners, seeking to learn and live Yoga in its most traditional and holistic form.

*   Integrates Asana (postures), Pranayama (breathing), Dhyana (meditation), and the profound wisdom of Vedanta philosophy.
*   Includes a variety of yoga styles, such as classical Hatha, flowing Vinyasa, and disciplined Ashtanga.
*   This is not a fitness program; it is a spiritual journey designed to foster self-awareness and inner peace.

**Ayurvedic Rejuvenation**

This is a gentle yet profound program designed to nourish the body and calm the mind using the timeless wisdom of Ayurveda. It is the perfect antidote to the stresses of modern life, restoring your natural vitality.

*   Focuses on the *Rasayana* (rejuvenating) principles of Ayurveda to combat fatigue and promote graceful aging.
*   Includes a bespoke plan of deeply relaxing massages, nourishing oil treatments, and customized herbal remedies.
*   This program perfectly complements a yoga practice by preparing the body for deeper states of relaxation and awareness.

**Panchakarma Detox**

This is a mild, supportive, and restorative version of the classical Ayurvedic detoxification. It is designed to gently cleanse the body of accumulated toxins within an environment of luxurious comfort and absolute care.

*   A personalized and physician-supervised process that purifies the body without harshness or discomfort.
*   Integrates a nourishing diet of organic, gourmet cuisine to support the body's natural cleansing process.
*   The program aims to restore your metabolic fire (*Agni*), improve energy, and enhance mental clarity.

**Weight Management**

This is a scientific and sustainable approach to achieving your ideal body weight. It moves beyond calorie counting to address the root physical, metabolic, and emotional causes of weight imbalance.

*   Integrates specific yoga practices to boost metabolism, Ayurvedic therapies to reduce excess fat, and a personalized wellness diet.
*   Focuses on creating mindful eating habits and a positive relationship with your body.
*   This is a holistic re-balancing program designed for lasting results, not a temporary "bootcamp."

**Karma Yoga**

This unique program combines the joy of gourmet wellness cuisine with the spiritual practice of Karma Yoga (selfless service). It is a journey of nourishing yourself and connecting with the earth.

*   Includes interactive cooking classes where you learn to create delicious and healthy vegetarian meals from our organic gardens.
*   Offers grounding "Joy of Giving" sessions, where you can mindfully work and connect with nature on our farm.
*   This is a deeply rewarding experience that nourishes the body, calms the mind, and uplifts the spirit.

**Wellness for the Soul**

This is a profound journey designed for those seeking emotional healing, spiritual growth, and a deeper connection to their inner self. It uses the subtler, more introspective practices of the yogic tradition.

*   Focuses on practices like Yoga Nidra (psychic sleep), Trataka (focused gazing), and guided meditation.
*   Integrates sessions on chanting, sound healing, and the self-inquiry teachings of Vedanta.
*   The entire program is designed to release emotional blockages, quiet the mind, and reveal your inner light.

**Silent Retreat (Mouna)**

This is a powerful, guided retreat designed for deep introspection and inner stillness. By consciously withdrawing from external speech and distractions, you create the space for profound self-awareness and mental peace to emerge.

*   This is a structured program with guided meditation, gentle yoga, and journaling to support your inner journey.
*   All your needs are cared for in our luxurious and supportive environment, allowing you to immerse yourself fully.
*   It is a rare opportunity to quiet the mental chatter and experience a state of deep, restorative tranquility.

**Digital Detox**

This program is designed to help you disconnect from the constant stimulation of the digital world and reconnect with yourself and nature. It is a gentle reset for a hyper-connected mind.

*   Focuses on mindfulness practices, nature-based therapies, and yoga sessions that ground your energy.
*   The serene, 25-acre campus provides the perfect environment to break the cycle of digital dependency.
*   It helps to improve focus, reduce mental anxiety, and restore your natural rhythms.

**Glow & Beauty**

This is a holistic program designed to cultivate beauty from the inside out. It combines nourishing treatments for the skin with practices that enhance your inner vitality and radiance.

*   Integrates natural, organic facials and body treatments with specific Ayurvedic herbs for skin health.
*   Includes a specialized diet rich in antioxidants from our own organic gardens.
*   Focuses on yoga and pranayama techniques that improve circulation and promote a natural, vibrant glow.

**Couple's Connect**

This is a unique retreat designed for couples to reconnect on a deeper level. It provides a serene and luxurious space to nurture your relationship away from the demands of daily life.

*   Includes couple's yoga sessions, guided meditations, and shared wellness experiences.
*   Offers a blend of private time for relaxation and structured sessions to foster communication and connection.
*   It is a beautiful opportunity to grow together on a path of mutual well-being and understanding.

**Grief & Emotion**

This is a compassionate and supportive program for those navigating the difficult process of grief or emotional trauma. It provides a safe and nurturing space to process emotions and begin to heal.

*   Focuses on restorative yoga, gentle pranayama, and guided meditations that facilitate emotional release.
*   Integrates one-on-one counselling sessions and therapies designed to calm the nervous system.
*   Our serene and private sanctuary ensures you have the peace and support needed for this delicate inner work.

**Celebrating Life**

This uplifting program is designed to help you reconnect with your inner joy and creativity. It is a celebration of life, designed to combat feelings of stagnation or listlessness.

*   Focuses on heart-opening yoga practices, dynamic meditation, and expressive arts sessions.
*   Integrates nourishing, delicious cuisine and time spent in the beauty of our lush gardens.
*   It is a perfect retreat for anyone looking to rediscover their zest for life and cultivate a more joyful perspective.
