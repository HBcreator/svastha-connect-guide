### Medical Programs

Targeted wellness protocols designed to address specific lifestyle conditions through the integrated wisdom of Yoga, Ayurveda, and Naturopathy.

**Stress & Burnout**

This is a comprehensive program designed to heal the nervous system from the effects of chronic stress and professional burnout. It provides a sanctuary for deep rest and effective, lasting recovery.

*   Integrates calming Ayurvedic therapies like *Shirodhara* with restorative yoga and deep relaxation techniques.
*   Focuses on balancing the body's stress-response system and improving sleep quality naturally.
*   Empowers you with mindfulness tools and techniques to build resilience against future stress.

**Insomnia & Sleep**

This is a specialized program designed to address the root causes of poor sleep. It uses a range of holistic therapies to reset your natural sleep-wake cycle (*circadian rhythm*).

*   Focuses on the profound practice of Yoga Nidra, which is clinically proven to induce deep states of relaxation.
*   Integrates specific Ayurvedic herbs and therapies known to calm the mind and promote restful sleep.
*   Includes personalized lifestyle and dietary guidance to help you cultivate healthy, long-term sleep hygiene.

**Hypertension**

This is a gentle and supportive program designed to help manage high blood pressure and improve cardiovascular health. It focuses on the primary lifestyle factors that contribute to these conditions.

*   Uses the power of Pranayama (yogic breathing) and meditation to naturally calm the nervous system and lower blood pressure.
*   Integrates heart-supportive Ayurvedic herbs and a carefully designed low-sodium, high-fiber gourmet diet.
*   This program is a proactive and preventative approach to nurturing long-term heart health.

**Digestive Health Reset**

This program is designed to treat common digestive ailments like IBS, acidity, and bloating. It works by restoring your natural digestive fire (*Agni*) and healing the gut.

*   Integrates specific yoga postures known to stimulate and balance the digestive system.
*   Combines this with a personalized Ayurvedic and naturopathic diet of organic, easy-to-digest foods.
*   Includes gentle detoxification therapies to cleanse the digestive tract and reduce inflammation.

**Women's Health**

This is a supportive program designed to help manage lifestyle-related hormonal imbalances. It provides natural and holistic support for conditions like PMS, perimenopause, and stress-induced cycle irregularities.

*   Utilizes a specific sequence of yoga postures and breathing techniques known to support the endocrine system.
*   Integrates balancing Ayurvedic herbs and diet to help regulate the menstrual cycle naturally.
*   This is a nurturing and empowering program for women seeking to harmonize their natural rhythms.

**Chronic Pain**

This program is designed to address lifestyle-related chronic pain, such as back pain, neck tension, and general stiffness. It focuses on improving flexibility, reducing inflammation, and correcting posture.

*   Uses therapeutic yoga to gently increase mobility, strengthen supporting muscles, and release chronic tension.
*   Integrates potent Ayurvedic therapies with anti-inflammatory properties to soothe sore muscles and joints.
*   Empowers you with the knowledge of correct posture and movement to prevent future recurrence.

**Diabetes Management**

This is a holistic program designed to support the management of Type 2 Diabetes. It focuses on improving insulin sensitivity and regulating blood sugar levels through natural, lifestyle-based interventions.

*   Integrates specific yoga asanas that are known to stimulate the pancreas and improve metabolic function.
*   Includes a carefully managed diet of low-glycemic, high-fiber organic foods.
*   This program is designed to work in conjunction with your medical advice to improve overall health outcomes.

**Aging & Mobility**

This program is specifically designed for seniors or those looking to maintain vitality and mobility as they age. It is a gentle yet effective approach to preserving strength, balance, and cognitive function.

*   Focuses on gentle and restorative yoga, including chair yoga, to maintain joint health and flexibility.
*   Integrates nourishing Ayurvedic therapies (*Brimhana*) to strengthen tissues and prevent degeneration.
*   Includes mind-stimulating practices like meditation to support cognitive sharpness and memory.

**Alignment & Correction**

This is a targeted program designed for those suffering from the effects of a sedentary lifestyle and poor posture. It works to realign the body, release chronic tension, and build core strength.

*   Focuses on specific yoga techniques that teach correct spinal alignment in sitting, standing, and walking.
*   Integrates deep tissue Ayurvedic massages to release chronic knots and muscular imbalances.
*   It is a highly effective program for preventing long-term spinal issues and relieving posture-related pain.

**Anxiety Management**

This program is specifically designed to manage the symptoms of anxiety. It provides you with a set of powerful tools from the yogic tradition to calm a racing mind and regulate the nervous system.

*   Focuses heavily on Pranayama (breathing techniques) that directly influence the body's fight-or-flight response.
*   Integrates grounding yoga practices and mindfulness techniques to keep you centered in the present moment.
*   This is an empowering program that teaches you how to actively manage your own mental state.

**Fatigue Recovery**

This is a gentle and restorative program for those recovering from viral illnesses, including long COVID. It is designed to gently rebuild energy levels and support the respiratory and immune systems.

*   Focuses on restorative yoga and gentle Pranayama to improve lung capacity without causing strain.
*   Integrates powerful immune-boosting (*Ojas* building) Ayurvedic herbs and a nutrient-dense diet.
*   This is a slow, supportive, and deeply nourishing program designed for sustainable recovery.

**Grief Release**

This is a compassionate and supportive program for those navigating the difficult process of grief or emotional trauma. It provides a safe and nurturing space to process emotions and begin to heal.

*   Focuses on restorative yoga, gentle pranayama, and guided meditations that facilitate emotional release.
*   Integrates one-on-one counselling sessions and therapies designed to calm the nervous system.
*   Our serene and private sanctuary ensures you have the peace and support needed for this delicate inner work.
