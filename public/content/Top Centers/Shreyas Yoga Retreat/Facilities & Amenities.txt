### Facilities & Amenities

Our 25-acre sanctuary is a thoughtfully designed ecosystem for wellness, where every facility embodies our philosophy of quiet luxury and deep healing.

**Exclusive Accommodation**

Your accommodation is a private haven of peace, designed for deep rest and rejuvenation in harmony with nature.

*   Stay in one of just 12 exclusive cottages, ensuring absolute privacy.
*   Choose from poolside cottages or luxury garden-tented cottages.
*   Each space is elegantly appointed to be your personal, luxurious sanctuary.

**Dedicated Yoga & Meditation Spaces**

We provide serene and beautiful purpose-built spaces to deepen your spiritual practice and find your inner center.

*   Practice in our stunning open-air yoga shala, surrounded by nature.
*   Find stillness in our circular, domed meditation and chanting hall.
*   These sacred spaces are designed to enhance focus and tranquility.

**The Anaha Spa**

Our world-class spa offers a curated menu of therapies to support your wellness journey and induce profound relaxation.

*   Experience authentic Ayurvedic and classical naturopathic treatments.
*   Enjoy a range of international massages, scrubs, and beauty therapies.
*   Our expert therapists ensure a deeply healing and restorative experience.

**Organic Gardens & Dining**

Our gourmet wellness cuisine is a journey of the senses, crafted from the freshest ingredients picked from our own earth.

*   Dine on exquisite vegetarian meals from our certified organic gardens.
*   Learn the art of healthy cooking in our interactive culinary classes.
*   Our "farm-to-table" philosophy ensures food that is pure and vibrant.

**Recreation & Relaxation**

We offer a range of luxurious facilities for you to relax, recharge, and enjoy the beautiful natural surroundings.

*   Unwind by our stunning temperature-controlled infinity pool and heated jacuzzi.
*   Use our state-of-the-art gym, steam room, and outdoor jogging trail.
*   These amenities provide the perfect balance to your spiritual practices.

**Library & Quiet Lounge**

Our library is a peaceful sanctuary for the mind, offering a quiet space for study, reading, and contemplation.

*   Browse our curated collection of books on Yoga, philosophy, and wellness.
*   Relax in a comfortable and serene environment designed for introspection.
*   This is a perfect space to deepen your knowledge and quiet your mind.

**Amphitheatre & Community Spaces**

Our beautiful outdoor spaces are designed to foster connection with nature and our conscious community.

*   Our stunning stone amphitheatre is used for performances and gatherings.
*   Enjoy beautifully landscaped gardens with private nooks for relaxation.
*   These spaces allow for both personal solitude and community connection.

**The "Joy of Giving" Farm**

We offer a unique opportunity to practice Karma Yoga by connecting with the earth in a mindful and fulfilling way.

*   Participate in our "Joy of Giving" program at our organic farm.
*   Experience the therapeutic benefits of working with soil and plants.
*   This is a grounding practice that adds a deep layer of purpose to your stay.

**Personalized Guest Services**

As a Relais & Chateaux property, our service is intuitive, personal, and dedicated to your absolute comfort.

*   Our attentive team is available 24/7 to cater to your every need.
*   We can arrange seamless airport transfers and personalized excursions.
*   Experience a level of care that anticipates your needs and ensures a flawless stay.
