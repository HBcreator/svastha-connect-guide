### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness packages, we recommend a minimum stay of 7 nights to experience tangible benefits and allow for a true disconnection. For our more intensive Panchakarma or medical programs, a stay of 14 nights or more is typically required.

**2. What is the atmosphere at Shreyas like? Is it a spa or an ashram?**

Shreyas uniquely blends the authenticity and discipline of a traditional ashram with the comfort and luxury of a 5-star retreat. You will experience a serene, spiritually-focused environment while enjoying luxurious accommodations, gourmet vegetarian cuisine, and impeccable service.

**3. Do I need to be experienced in yoga to attend?**

Not at all. Shreyas is recognized as one of the finest yoga retreats in the world, and our daily Hatha and Ashtanga yoga sessions are designed for all levels, from complete beginners to advanced practitioners. Our masters provide personal attention and modifications to ensure the practice is safe and enjoyable for you.

**4. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a delicious, gourmet vegetarian cuisine that is a vital part of your wellness journey. Our farm-to-fork approach uses fresh, organic ingredients from our own gardens. While the food is incredibly healthy and sattvic, it is also flavorful and beautifully presented. We do not serve alcohol at the retreat.

**5. Should I bring my current medications and medical records?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions and recent medical records. Our team of Ayurvedic and Naturopathy doctors will review them to create a safe and comprehensive treatment plan for you.

**6. What is a "Silent Retreat"?**

Our *Mouna* (Silent) Retreat is a special program where guests observe intentional silence for a set period. It is a powerful practice for calming the mind and deepening self-awareness. It is a guided experience, supported by meditation and self-inquiry sessions with our resident guru.

**7. Can a companion stay with me if they are not on a wellness program?**

Yes, your companion is welcome to stay with you. They can enjoy the serene environment, our gourmet wellness cuisine, and participate in our daily complimentary wellness activities like yoga and meditation. Please note that personalized treatments are only for guests on a specific wellness program.

**8. How do I get to the retreat?**

We are conveniently located on the serene outskirts of Bangalore, approximately a one-hour scenic drive from the Kempegowda International Airport (BLR). We are happy to arrange your private luxury airport transfers for a seamless and comfortable journey.

**9. What makes Shreyas a "luxury" retreat?**

Our luxury is defined by our impeccable, personalized service, our stunning and spacious accommodations, and our commitment to providing a complete, seamless, and high-quality wellness experience. From our infinity pool to our 'Anaha' spa, every detail is designed for your ultimate comfort and well-being.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing for yoga and other activities, as well as smart casual attire for evenings. Please also bring any personal toiletries and your medical records. We will provide you with a fresh set of white cotton *kurta* pajamas to wear during your stay.
