### Facilities & Amenities

At Ananda, every facility is thoughtfully designed to create a luxurious and serene environment where healing happens naturally.

**Luxurious Palace & Villa Accommodations**

Rest and recover in elegant rooms and private villas, where royal heritage meets modern luxury.

*   Stay in beautifully restored rooms within the historic Viceregal Palace, the former residence of the Maharaja.

*   Opt for an exclusive private villa, many with their own temperature-controlled swimming pools.

*   Enjoy breathtaking views of the Ganges valley or the serene palace gardens from your private balcony.

**The Award-Winning Ananda Spa**

Immerse yourself in our 25,000 sq. ft. spa, a world-renowned sanctuary for healing and rejuvenation.

*   Experience a vast menu of over 80 treatments, masterfully blending classical Ayurveda with international therapies.

*   Heal in one of our 24 serene and spacious treatment rooms, each designed for your ultimate comfort.

*   Trust in the care of our world-class team of expert Ayurvedic physicians, therapists, and wellness consultants.

**Gourmet Wellness Cuisine**

Savor a healing cuisine where every meal is a delicious, personalized, and gourmet experience.

*   Our chefs and Ayurvedic doctors collaborate to create a sophisticated menu customized to your specific *dosha*.

*   Enjoy exquisite meals prepared with fresh, local, and organic ingredients from the surrounding region.

*   Dine in our elegant treetop restaurant with stunning, panoramic views of the Himalayan foothills.

**Himalayan Yoga & Meditation Pavilions**

Harmonize your mind and body in our serene spaces, blessed with the spiritual energy of the Himalayas.

*   Practice in our stunning open-air amphitheater or our beautiful indoor yoga hall with valley views.

*   Be guided by our renowned yoga masters through daily sessions of yoga, pranayama, and meditation.

*   Deepen your journey with discourses on Vedanta, the ancient Indian philosophy of self-realization.

**Temperature-Controlled Swimming Pools**

Refresh your body and mind in our beautiful pools, offering serene views and tranquil relaxation.

*   Enjoy a gentle swim in our stunning, temperature-controlled outdoor lap pool with valley views.

*   Relax and unwind in our serene indoor hydrotherapy pools, a perfect complement to your daily treatments.

*   This low-impact activity is a wonderful way to enhance your circulation and calm your mind.

**The Himalayan Golf Experience**

Enjoy a unique opportunity to play golf amidst the breathtaking beauty of the Himalayan landscape.

*   Practice your swing on our scenic 6-hole golf course, located on the palace estate.

*   It is a perfect, mindful activity that allows you to connect with nature while enjoying gentle exercise.

*   Our team can provide all the necessary equipment for your game.

**The Viceregal Palace & Library**

Step back in time and relax in the historic and elegant common areas of the Maharaja's palace.

*   Unwind in the magnificent Viceregal Tea Lounge, a place of classic elegance and serenity.

*   Find a quiet corner in our historic library, which is home to a rich collection of books.

*   Enjoy a game of billiards in a room that is steeped in a century of royal history.

**Fitness & Active Wellness**

Maintain your fitness goals with our state-of-the-art facilities and personalized expert guidance.

*   Our modern, fully equipped gymnasium features stunning views of the Himalayan valley.

*   Benefit from personalized fitness consultations and training sessions with our expert instructors.

*   Engage in a range of guided activities, from aqua fitness to scenic treks in the Himalayan foothills.

**Seamless Guest Services**

Focus completely on your healing while we take care of all the practical details with discreet, world-class service.

*   Allow us to arrange your airport transfers from Dehradun for a seamless and comfortable arrival.

*   Our attentive, 24/7 staff are dedicated to ensuring your every need is met with grace and professionalism.

*   Experience a level of intuitive and heartfelt hospitality that has made Ananda a global legend.
