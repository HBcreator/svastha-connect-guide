### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness programs, we recommend a minimum stay of 7 nights to experience tangible benefits and allow for a true disconnection. For more targeted medical or detoxification programs, a stay of 14 to 21 nights is typically required for deep and lasting results.

**2. How do I get to Ananda?**

Ananda is located in the Himalayan foothills, overlooking Rishikesh. The nearest airport is the Jolly Grant Airport in Dehradun (DED), which is approximately a one-hour scenic drive from our retreat. We are happy to arrange your airport transfers for a seamless and comfortable journey to the palace.

**3. What makes Ananda different from a luxury spa?**

While we offer world-class spa facilities, Ananda is a comprehensive destination wellness retreat. Our core focus is on creating a transformative journey by integrating the ancient Indian sciences of Ayurveda, Yoga, and Vedanta with modern wellness. Your experience is guided by a multi-disciplinary team of expert physicians, yoga masters, and chefs, not just spa therapists.

**4. What is the wellness cuisine like?**

Our cuisine is a gourmet wellness experience. Our chefs and Ayurvedic doctors collaborate to create a sophisticated menu that is customized to your specific *dosha* and wellness goals. The food is delicious, nourishing, and prepared with fresh, local, and organic ingredients, proving that healthy eating can be a true delight.

**5. Do I need to be experienced in yoga to attend?**

Not at all. Our daily yoga and meditation sessions are designed for all levels, from complete beginners to advanced practitioners. Our experienced yoga masters provide personalized guidance and modifications to ensure the practice is safe, enjoyable, and perfectly suited to your body and goals.

**6. What should I pack for my stay in the Himalayas?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing for yoga and other activities, as well as smart casual attire for evenings. The Himalayan weather can change, so a few light layers are always a good idea. We will provide you with a fresh set of white cotton *kurta* pajamas to wear during your stay.

**7. How personalized is my wellness program?**

Your program is completely personalized. Your journey begins with an in-depth consultation with our Ayurvedic and wellness experts, who then architect a unique "wellness blueprint" just for you. Your entire schedule—from therapies and meals to fitness and yoga—is dynamically calibrated based on your daily progress.

**8. Can my companion stay with me if they are not on a wellness program?**

Yes, your companion is welcome to stay with you. They can enjoy the serene environment, our gourmet wellness cuisine, and participate in our daily complimentary wellness activities like yoga and meditation. Please note that personalized treatments are only provided to guests on a specific wellness program.

**9. What is Vedanta, and do I have to participate?**

Vedanta is the ancient Indian philosophy of self-realization, which helps you find inner peace and purpose. Our discourses on Vedanta are a unique and profound part of the Ananda experience. However, your participation is completely optional and is offered for those who are interested in a deeper spiritual journey.

**10. Is there internet or Wi-Fi available?**

Yes, Wi-Fi is available in your room and in designated common areas. However, to fully embrace the healing energy of our Himalayan sanctuary, we gently encourage our guests to embrace a digital detox as much as possible, as disconnecting from screens is a powerful way to calm the mind and deepen your healing experience.
