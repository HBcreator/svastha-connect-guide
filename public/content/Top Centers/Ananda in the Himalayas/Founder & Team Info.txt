**Ashok Khanna**

Founder & Managing Director

Ashok Khanna is the visionary founder who embarked on a mission to create a spiritual sanctuary in the Himalayas. He discovered the Maharaja's Palace estate and knew it was the 'chosen place' to bring his dream to life. Together with his wife Neelam, he built Ananda on the foundational pillars of Ayurveda, Yoga, and Vedanta, creating a world-renowned destination where guests can find true inner harmony.
