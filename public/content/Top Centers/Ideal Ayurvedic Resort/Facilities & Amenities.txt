### Facilities & Amenities

**'Green Leaf' Certified Ayurveda Centre**
*   Receive your treatments in our authentic 'Green Leaf' certified Ayurveda centre, ensuring the highest standards of traditional practice.
*   Our facility features well-equipped, clean, and hygienic therapy rooms designed for all classical Ayurvedic treatments.
*   Heal with confidence, knowing you are being cared for by our highly skilled and certified male and female therapists.

**Comfortable & Serene Accommodations**
*   Rest and heal in our comfortable, air-conditioned rooms and cottages, nestled within a lush, hillside coconut grove.
*   Each room offers a peaceful sanctuary with a private balcony or sit-out area overlooking the verdant gardens.
*   Our accommodations are designed for deep rest, free from unnecessary distractions to support your healing journey.

**Ayurvedic Vegetarian Restaurant**
*   Nourish your body in our dedicated restaurant, serving delicious, home-style Keralan vegetarian cuisine.
*   Our physicians personally guide your diet, ensuring every meal is tailored to balance your specific dosha.
*   Experience the authentic taste of healing food, prepared fresh daily to support your treatment plan.

**Peaceful Yoga & Meditation Hall**
*   Harmonize your mind and body in our dedicated, serene hall designed for spiritual and mindful practice.
*   Join daily yoga and meditation sessions guided by our experienced instructors in a tranquil, purpose-built space.
*   This facility is central to our holistic approach, complementing your physical treatments with mental and spiritual peace.

**Outdoor Swimming Pool**
*   Refresh your body in our beautiful, well-maintained swimming pool, a serene oasis within our tropical gardens.
*   Relax on comfortable sun loungers, offering a perfect space for rest and rejuvenation between your daily treatments.
*   Enjoy a safe, clean, and peaceful alternative for a refreshing swim, surrounded by the beauty of nature.

**Sprawling Coconut Grove & Gardens**
*   Heal in a sprawling, 15-acre sanctuary of lush coconut groves and meticulously maintained tropical gardens.
*   The vast, oxygen-rich environment provides an unparalleled sense of space and tranquility for your healing journey.
*   Reconnect with nature through peaceful walks on our garden pathways, surrounded by verdant beauty.

**Convenient Beach Access**
*   Enjoy the therapeutic benefits of the sea with just a short, pleasant five-minute walk to the pristine Chowara beach.
*   Our ideal hillside location provides a peaceful retreat from the direct bustle of the beachfront while keeping it easily accessible.
*   The proximity allows for mindful sunrise or sunset walks, a core part of the Keralan healing experience.

**On-Site Doctor Consultation Rooms**
*   Begin your journey and monitor your progress in our private and professional on-site consultation rooms.
*   Benefit from the confidentiality and comfort of a dedicated clinical space for your daily reviews with our physicians.
*   This facility underscores our commitment to a medically supervised, safe, and effective healing experience.

**Essential Guest Services & Amenities**
*   Stay connected with complimentary Wi-Fi available in the reception and restaurant areas.
*   Nourish your mind in our small library, or let our friendly travel desk assist with your local travel needs.
*   Benefit from a range of services including laundry and currency exchange for a comfortable and hassle-free stay.
