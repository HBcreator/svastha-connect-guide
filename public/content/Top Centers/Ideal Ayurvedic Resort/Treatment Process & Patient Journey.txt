### Treatment Process & Patient Journey

Your path to authentic healing, personally guided with heartfelt care in our serene hillside sanctuary.

1.  **Welcome & In-depth Physician's Consultation**
    Your journey begins with a warm welcome to our peaceful retreat. You will then have a comprehensive, one-on-one consultation with our experienced Ayurvedic physician, who will conduct a deep diagnosis to determine your unique mind-body constitution (*Prakriti*) and any imbalances.
    *   This is a thorough medical consultation led by a highly qualified and experienced Vaidya.
    *   Through traditional diagnostics, we identify the root cause of your health concerns.
    *   This crucial first step ensures your entire healing journey is built on a solid, personalized foundation.

2.  **Designing Your Personal Path to Wellness**
    Based on your detailed consultation, our doctor will design your exclusive and personalized wellness program. This is your bespoke roadmap to healing, integrating every aspect of the Ayurvedic lifestyle for the most effective results.
    *   This is not a generic package; it is your unique, physician-led path to restoring balance.
    *   It meticulously outlines your daily schedule of specific Ayurvedic therapies, herbal medicines, and activities.
    *   This personalized approach ensures you receive the most targeted care for your specific health needs.

3.  **Daily Authentic Ayurvedic Therapies**
    This is where your deep physical healing and purification begins. Each day, you will receive your prescribed treatments, administered with exceptional skill and care by our certified male and female therapists in our serene, 'Green Leaf' certified treatment centre.
    *   You will receive your personalized treatments daily in our clean, peaceful, and traditional therapy rooms.
    *   All therapies are performed using authentic, classical techniques and high-quality herbal preparations.
    *   Our tranquil, natural environment enhances the profound, healing effects of every therapy.

4.  **Guided Ayurvedic Healing Cuisine**
    We practice the principle that food is potent medicine. Your meals are a vital part of your treatment, and our physicians personally guide you on your diet, ensuring every dish from our home-style vegetarian kitchen supports your healing.
    *   Enjoy delicious, authentic Keralan vegetarian meals prepared fresh daily.
    *   Your doctor will personally advise you on which dishes to favour and which to avoid.
    *   This guided approach to nutrition actively supports your body’s healing and detoxification process.

5.  **Integrated Wellness & Daily Doctor's Review**
    Your healing journey is a comprehensive experience that is monitored with diligent medical care. Each day includes personalized yoga and meditation sessions, and your progress is carefully reviewed daily by your consulting physician.
    *   Harmonize your mind and body with daily yoga and meditation in our peaceful yoga hall.
    *   Your progress is reviewed every single day by our resident doctors to ensure your journey is safe and effective.
    *   This constant medical supervision allows for fine-tuning of your program for the best possible results.

6.  **Departure with an Empowered Lifestyle Plan**
    Our support for you culminates in empowering you with the tools for a lifetime of well-being. Before you depart, you will receive a comprehensive final consultation with your physician to prepare you for your return home.
    *   Receive a detailed and personalized plan, including dietary, herbal, and lifestyle advice.
    *   You leave not just rejuvenated, but with a deeper understanding of how to maintain your health.
    *   You depart our sanctuary empowered with the wisdom to sustain your newfound balance and vitality.
