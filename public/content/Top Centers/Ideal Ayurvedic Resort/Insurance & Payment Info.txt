### Insurance & Payment Info

Making your path to our authentic healing sanctuary clear and accessible.

**Insurance Coverage**

*   As a government-approved traditional treatment center, some Indian and international insurance providers may offer coverage for our therapeutic programs.

*   Our professional administrative team is happy to provide you with all the necessary medical documentation to support your reimbursement process.

*   We are committed to helping you navigate the claims process as smoothly as possible.

*   We strongly advise you to confirm coverage details and eligibility directly with your insurance provider before your stay.

**Payment Options**

*   We accept all major payment methods, including credit/debit cards and domestic/international bank transfers.

*   Our pricing is completely transparent, with all costs for your all-inclusive program clearly outlined after your consultation.

*   Our pricing reflects our commitment to providing authentic, physician-led healing, prioritizing therapeutic value over luxury frills.

*   You will receive a detailed cost estimate before your treatment begins, with absolutely no hidden charges.

**For International Patients**

International guests are encouraged to check with their home insurance providers regarding coverage for Ayurvedic treatments at our government-approved facility. Our administrative team is highly experienced in providing the necessary, detailed medical reports and documentation in the required format to support your international insurance claims.
