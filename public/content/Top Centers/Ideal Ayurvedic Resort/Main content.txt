**About Ideal Ayurvedic Resort**
Welcome to Ideal Ayurvedic Resort, a serene sanctuary nestled on a tranquil hillside in the peaceful village of Chowara, just a short walk from the pristine sands of Kovalam beach. Surrounded by 15 acres of lush, sun-drenched coconut groves, we offer a genuine escape into the world of traditional Keralan healing. Here, authentic Ayurveda is practiced with heartfelt dedication, creating a blissful and peaceful environment for your complete rejuvenation.
