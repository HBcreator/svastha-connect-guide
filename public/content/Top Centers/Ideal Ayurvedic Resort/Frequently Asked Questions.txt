### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our medical treatment programs, a stay of 14 days is typically the minimum required for a deep and effective healing process. The exact duration will be recommended by our physicians based on your specific condition and health goals.

**2. What is the atmosphere at Ideal like? Is it a luxury resort?**

Our focus is on creating a traditional, "ashram-like" environment for serious, authentic healing. We are not a luxury resort. The atmosphere is quiet, simple, and deeply peaceful, allowing you to focus completely on your inner journey without distractions.

**3. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a simple, wholesome, and delicious vegetarian diet based on classical Ayurvedic principles. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are customized by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha* and health condition.

**4. How close are you to the beach?**

Our retreat is nestled in a lush, green garden environment just a beautiful, five-minute walk away from the serene Chowara Beach. This allows you to enjoy the tranquility of our gardens and the healing energy of the Arabian Sea.

**5. How often will I see the doctor?**

You will be under the close care of our expert physicians. After your comprehensive initial consultation, your doctor will review your progress every two to three days, making adjustments to your treatment plan to ensure it is perfectly attuned to your body's response.

**6. What is the accommodation like?**

You will stay in traditional, clean, and comfortable Kerala-style cottages, arranged around a beautiful open courtyard. The rooms are designed to be a peaceful sanctuary for rest and recovery, not for luxury entertainment.

**7. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our physicians will review them during your initial consultation and advise you on how to manage them safely during your stay.

**8. Can a companion who is not taking treatment stay with me?**

Yes, companions are welcome to stay with you and enjoy the serene environment and our healthy Ayurvedic cuisine. Please note that treatments are only provided to those who have undergone a consultation and have a prescribed wellness plan.

**9. Do you treat serious medical conditions?**

Yes. We are not a spa; we are a genuine healing center with a strong reputation for successfully managing a wide range of serious and chronic conditions, from rheumatoid arthritis to complex skin disorders, where conventional medicine may have offered limited hope.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing suitable for a warm, tropical climate. Please also bring swimwear, any personal toiletries, and your medical records. We will provide towels and any necessary attire for your treatments.
