### Facilities & Amenities

At SOUKYA, every facility is thoughtfully designed to create an environment where healing happens naturally and luxuriously.

**Luxurious Private Garden Accommodations**

Rest and recover in your own private sanctuary, where traditional elegance meets modern comfort.

*   Choose from 28 generously sized rooms, from deluxe to presidential suites, each opening to a private garden.

*   Enjoy spa-like bathrooms, with some suites featuring romantic outdoor showers for a truly unique experience.

*   Relax in comfort with premium amenities, including air conditioning, WiFi, and 24-hour power backup.

**Award-Winning Sustainable Architecture**

Immerse yourself in an award-winning design that seamlessly blends with the healing power of nature.

*   Our architecture was honored by Condé Nast Traveller as the "Most Spiritual Destination."

*   The design masterfully combines traditional Kerala styles with beautiful, calming, and eco-conscious elements.

*   Every space is crafted to maximize natural light, ventilation, and your connection to the outdoors.

**Certified 30-Acre Organic Farm**

Heal in a living ecosystem where the food that nourishes you and the herbs that heal you are grown.

*   Our fully certified organic farm ensures that every meal and medicine is 100% pure and chemical-free.

*   Wander through our extensive medicinal garden, home to over 150 varieties of healing plants.

*   The entire 30-acre property is a biodiversity haven, creating a naturally therapeutic and calming atmosphere.

**Integrative Treatment Infrastructure**

Experience world-class treatments in facilities designed for your privacy, comfort, and dignity.

*   We provide complete separate and mirrored therapy centers for our male and female guests.

*   Benefit from the purity of our in-house pharmacy, where your medicines are freshly and custom-prepared.

*   Our consultation rooms are designed for our multi-disciplinary team to collaborate for your unique healing.

**Traditional Yoga & Meditation Hall**

Practice mindful healing in a sacred space specially designed for deep inner work.

*   Our beautiful yoga hall is adorned with traditional Gujarati mirror work and colored-glass walls.

*   Your daily sessions are guided by qualified Naturopathic doctors who understand therapeutic yoga.

*   Receive customized yoga and meditation practices tailored to your specific medical needs.

**Organic Farm-to-Table Dining**

Savor a healing cuisine where every meal is a delicious, personalized, and therapeutic experience.

*   Enjoy your meals in one of our three distinct dining spaces, including a peaceful outdoor garden setting.

*   Your ovo-vegetarian diet is completely customized to your Ayurvedic body type and treatment plan.

*   Experience true farm-to-table freshness, with most ingredients harvested from our own organic farm daily.

**Therapeutic Recreation & Wellness**

Engage in healing activities that extend beyond the treatment room, nurturing your body, mind, and spirit.

*   Enjoy gentle exercise in our clean, well-maintained swimming pool, perfect for water therapy.

*   Take meditative strolls on our 2.2-kilometer walking track that winds through organic orchards.

*   Relax your mind in our indoor recreation rooms, featuring a library, snooker, and table tennis.

**Professional Medical Safety**

Rest assured knowing you are in a NABH-accredited facility with hospital-grade safety standards.

*   Our NABH accreditation confirms we meet the most rigorous standards for patient safety and clinical care.

*   Enjoy complete peace of mind with our qualified medical team available on call 24/7.

*   We have access to a network of specialist consultants for any additional medical needs.

**Hassle-Free Guest Services**

Focus completely on your healing while we take care of all the practical details for you.

*   Allow us to arrange your airport transfers for a seamless and stress-free arrival.

*   We offer separate, comfortable accommodation for any family members or attendants accompanying you.

*   Enjoy the convenience of our daily housekeeping, laundry services, and secure on-site parking.
