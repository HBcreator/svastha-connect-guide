**Our Visionary Founders**

Dr. Vithal Kamat & Mr. Vishal Kamat

Toyam is the wellness vision of The Orchid Hotels, guided by two generations of leadership. It was founded by the legendary **Dr. Vithal Venkatesh Kamat**, celebrated as the "Green Hotelier" who pioneered eco-friendly luxury hospitality in India. Today, his son, **Mr. Vishal Vithal Kamat**, carries this legacy forward, personally leading the growth of Toyam as a world-class sanctuary where sustainable luxury and authentic healing are in perfect harmony.
