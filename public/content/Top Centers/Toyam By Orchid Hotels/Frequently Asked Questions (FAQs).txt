**Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)**

Have a question about your upcoming visit? We've compiled answers to some of the most common queries we receive to help you plan your wellness experience with us.

**What types of treatments are offered?**
We offer a curated menu of holistic wellness experiences rooted in ancient Ayurvedic traditions and contemporary therapies. This includes rejuvenating massages, detoxification programs, bespoke facial treatments, and personalized wellness consultations. Please visit our *Treatments* page for a detailed list.

**Do I need to book my sessions in advance?**
Yes, we **highly recommend** booking your treatments in advance. This ensures that we can accommodate your preferred time and therapist, and allows us to prepare a personalized and seamless experience for you from the moment you arrive.

**What should I wear for my session?**
We suggest wearing comfortable and loose-fitting clothing for your visit. For most body treatments, you will be provided with a disposable undergarment, a robe, and slippers to ensure your complete comfort and privacy.

**How early should I arrive for my appointment?**
To make the most of your experience, please plan to arrive at least 15-20 minutes prior to your scheduled appointment. This gives you ample time to complete a wellness consultation form, change, and begin your relaxation in our tranquil lounge before your treatment begins.

**Can I customize my wellness program?**
Yes. We encourage personalization based on your goals, preferences, and schedule. After an initial consultation, our team can recommend a tailored plan and adjust sessions as needed.

**Are treatments suitable for beginners?**
Absolutely. Our programs welcome first-time guests. Therapies and routines are explained clearly, and each session is adapted to your comfort level.

**Do you provide dietary guidance during the stay?**
Yes. Nutritional guidance is a core part of holistic wellness. Our team can advise on balanced meals and lifestyle habits that complement your therapies.

**Can I bring a companion?**
Yes, companions are welcome. If you plan to bring someone, let us know in advance so we can suggest the best accommodation and schedule options.

**What should I share before my visit?**
If available, please share any relevant medical history, ongoing medications, or wellness goals. This helps our team prepare a more personalized experience.
