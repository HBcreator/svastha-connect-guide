### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What makes Atmantan different from a regular spa resort?**

We are a world-class, NABH-accredited wellness hospital that offers its treatments in a luxury resort environment. This is our most important distinction.

*   We have the clinical expertise to treat complex, chronic lifestyle conditions with measurable results.
*   Your journey is led by a multi-disciplinary team of doctors who create a scientific, evidence-based plan.
*   Our focus is on profound, lasting transformation, not just temporary relaxation.

**2. How do I reach Atmantan?**

Our sanctuary is nestled in the pristine Crystal Healing Valley of Mulshi, offering a secluded feel while being conveniently accessible from two major cities.

*   From **Mumbai International Airport (BOM):** This is a scenic 3 to 3.5-hour drive.
*   From **Pune International Airport (PNQ):** This is a much shorter and comfortable 1.5 to 2-hour drive.
*   We are happy to arrange a reliable and comfortable private luxury transfer for you from either airport upon request.

**3. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

The duration of your stay is crucial for achieving deep and lasting results, and is always recommended by our doctors based on your goals.

*   For deep detoxification programs like Panchakarma or Master Cleanse, a minimum of **7 to 14 nights** is essential.
*   For transformative results in weight management or disease reversal, **21 to 28 nights** are highly recommended.
*   Shorter introductory wellness stays of **3 or 5 nights** are also available for a powerful health reset.

**4. What is included in my all-inclusive package?**

Our packages are designed to be comprehensive, allowing you to immerse yourself fully in your healing journey without any distractions.

*   Your package includes your luxurious accommodation and all doctor consultations (initial, ongoing, and final).
*   It covers all prescribed therapies, treatments, and fitness sessions from your personalized wellness plan.
*   Three gourmet, personalized wellness meals per day, along with prescribed juices and snacks, are also included.

**5. What is the best time of year to visit Atmantan?**

The Sahyadri mountains offer a pleasant climate year-round, making Atmantan a perfect destination for healing in any season.

*   The winter months, from **October to March**, offer cool, pleasant, and sunny weather, ideal for outdoor activities.
*   The monsoon season, from **June to September**, is magical, with the valley turning lush and green, and is traditionally excellent for Ayurveda.
*   The summer, from **April to May**, is warmer but still pleasant due to our altitude, perfect for enjoying our indoor pools and facilities.

**6. What kind of clothing should I bring?**

We recommend packing comfortable clothing suitable for a range of therapeutic, fitness, and leisure activities in a luxury resort setting.

*   Bring high-quality activewear for your fitness, yoga, and physiotherapy sessions.
*   Pack comfortable, smart-casual wear for relaxing and for your wellness consultations and meals.
*   Remember to bring swimwear for our indoor and outdoor pools, and comfortable walking shoes for our scenic trails.
