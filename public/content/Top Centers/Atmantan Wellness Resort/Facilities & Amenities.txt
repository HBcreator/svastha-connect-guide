### Facilities & Amenities

Our 42-acre sanctuary is a state-of-the-art, purpose-built wellness resort, where every facility is designed to the highest international standards to support your journey of transformation in absolute comfort and luxury.

**Luxurious Lake-View Accommodation**
Your private living space is a sanctuary of tranquility, designed to provide deep, restorative rest with breathtaking views of the pristine Mulshi Lake.

*   Stay in one of our 106 luxurious, ergonomically designed rooms and villas.
*   Each room features a private balcony or garden, offering stunning, uninterrupted views of the lake and valley.
*   The wellness-focused design promotes restful sleep and a calm, uncluttered state of mind.

**Expansive Wellness Pavilion**
Our world-class spa is a 65,000 sq. ft. haven of healing, housing a vast and diverse range of treatment spaces for your therapeutic journey.

*   We have 23 spacious and serene treatment rooms for our integrated therapies.
*   This includes dedicated suites for Ayurveda, Naturopathy, and international wellness treatments.
*   The pavilion is a testament to our commitment to providing a comprehensive and world-class wellness experience.

**State-of-the-Art Fitness Centre**
Our fitness facilities are designed to build a strong, resilient, and optimized body (*Tann*), featuring cutting-edge equipment and dedicated training zones.

*   Our expansive gymnasium is equipped with the latest cardio and strength training machines.
*   We have a dedicated indoor spinning studio and a functional fitness zone for high-intensity training.
*   This state-of-the-art facility caters to all fitness levels, from beginners to advanced athletes.

**Gourmet Wellness Dining Venues**
Our award-winning spa cuisine is served in three distinct and beautiful dining venues, each offering a unique and therapeutic culinary experience.

*   **Vistara**, our main dining hall, serves gourmet wellness meals with stunning panoramic views.
*   **Chantara**, our barbecue lounge, offers a unique social dining experience in the evenings.
*   **Té-jus**, our juice bar, provides fresh, prescribed juices and broths to support your cleanse.

**Advanced Hydrotherapy Suites**
Experience the profound healing and detoxifying power of water in our advanced, state-of-the-art hydrotherapy facilities.

*   Our suites include a luxurious sauna and a purifying aromatherapy steam room.
*   Relax and restore in our stunning indoor vitality pool, with therapeutic water jets.
*   These facilities are an integral part of our detoxification and relaxation protocols.

**Dedicated Mind-Body Studios**
We provide serene, purpose-built spaces designed to facilitate the deep inner work of calming the mind (*Mana*) and connecting with the soul (*Atma*).

*   Practice in our dedicated Yoga pavilion, offering a peaceful and inspiring atmosphere.
*   We have quiet, designated areas for both guided and personal meditation practices.
*   These sacred spaces are free from external distractions, allowing you to fully immerse yourself in your practice.

**Indoor Saltwater Pool**
Our magnificent temperature-controlled indoor saltwater pool is a unique and luxurious facility for both fitness and therapeutic relaxation.

*   This stunning 25-meter pool is perfect for year-round lap swimming and aquatic exercises.
*   The use of saltwater is gentler on the skin and eyes compared to chlorinated pools.
*   It provides a beautiful and serene environment for both active and passive wellness.

**Integrated Restorative Physiotherapy Wing**
Our facility includes a dedicated, hospital-grade wing for our Restorative Physiotherapy program, led by expert professionals.

*   This clinical space is equipped with advanced diagnostic and rehabilitation equipment.
*   It is a professional environment for treating chronic pain, postural issues, and sports injuries.
*   This facility underscores our status as a serious, NABH-accredited medical wellness center.

**The Healing Landscape & Amphitheatre**
The entire 42-acre campus, nestled in the Crystal Healing Valley, is your ultimate facility for healing and connecting with nature.

*   Take mindful walks or challenging hikes on our dedicated trails with spectacular valley views.
*   Find countless private nooks for quiet contemplation amidst our beautifully landscaped gardens.
*   Our stunning outdoor amphitheater is a beautiful venue for gatherings, workshops, and soulful experiences.
