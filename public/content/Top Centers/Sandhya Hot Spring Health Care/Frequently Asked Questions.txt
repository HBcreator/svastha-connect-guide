### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness programs, a minimum of 7 days is recommended to experience the full benefits. For our more intensive medical or detoxification programs, a stay of 14 days or more is typically required for deep and effective healing.

**2. What is so special about the hot sulphur springs?**

Our geo-thermal hot springs are our most unique and powerful healing tool. The water is naturally enriched with 37 different minerals, especially sulphur, which has been scientifically proven to have profound therapeutic benefits for skin conditions, joint pain, and deep relaxation.

**3. What is the accommodation like? Is it a luxury resort?**

Our focus is on authentic, nature-based healing, not on 5-star luxury. Our accommodations are clean, comfortable, and designed to provide a peaceful environment for rest, many with beautiful views of the lake and mountains. We prioritize a serene, therapeutic atmosphere over resort frills.

**4. How do I get to the retreat?**

Our retreat is located in Tattapani, a scenic journey from Shimla in the Himalayas. The nearest airport is in Shimla (SLV). We are happy to help you coordinate your airport transfers and other transportation for a seamless and beautiful journey.

**5. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a simple, wholesome, and delicious vegetarian diet based on classical Ayurvedic and sattvic principles. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are customized by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha* and health condition.

**6. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our physicians will review them during your initial consultation and advise you on how to manage them safely and effectively during your stay.

**7. Are there other activities besides the treatments?**

Yes. Our unique Himalayan location offers wonderful opportunities to connect with nature. We can help you arrange gentle activities like trekking in the foothills, boating on the Sutlej River, and local village tours to complement your healing journey.

**8. Can a companion who is not taking treatment stay with me?**

Yes, companions are welcome to stay with you and enjoy the serene environment, our healthy Ayurvedic cuisine, and the natural beauty of Tattapani. Please note that treatments are only provided to those who have undergone a consultation and have a prescribed wellness plan.

**9. Who are the doctors and experts at the center?**

Your healing journey is guided by our team of qualified and experienced Ayurvedic and Naturopathy physicians. They are dedicated to a compassionate, patient-centric approach and will personally design and supervise your entire treatment plan.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing suitable for a mountain climate, which can be cooler in the evenings. Please also bring swimwear for the hot springs, sturdy walking shoes, personal toiletries, and your medical records. We will provide towels and any necessary attire for your treatments.
