### The Visionary Founder
### Mr. Prem Raina
MD (Ayurveda)
CEO & Chief Physician

The story of Sandhya Hot Spring Health Care is the story of its founder, **Mr. Prem Raina (Prem Lal)**. His journey began in 1992 with a humble homestay and a profound vision. Recognizing the immense therapeutic potential of Tattapani's natural Sulphur springs, he pioneered the concept of integrating this geothermal gift with clinical healthcare. His dedication transformed his initial venture into one of India’s first health centers built around this natural wonder, earning him the prestigious "Diamond of Himachal" award for his immense contribution to wellness tourism.
