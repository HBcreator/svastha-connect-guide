### Treatment Process & Patient Journey

Your path to natural healing, medically supervised and personally guided every step of the way.

1.  **Medical Consultation & Diagnosis**
    Your journey begins not with a spa menu, but with a thorough medical consultation. Our qualified resident doctor will conduct an in-depth assessment to understand your health history, concerns, and wellness goals.
    *   This is a one-on-one session led by a qualified, professional doctor.
    *   We conduct a careful evaluation to diagnose the root cause of your health issues.
    *   This crucial first step ensures your entire program is built on a solid medical foundation.

2.  **Integrated Treatment Protocol Design**
    Based on your diagnosis, our doctor will design your exclusive treatment protocol. This is not a generic package; it is your personalized, scientific roadmap to healing, integrating our different systems of medicine for the most effective results.
    *   A 100% customized plan tailored to your specific health condition and needs.
    *   It scientifically combines geothermal hydrotherapy, Ayurveda, Naturopathy, and Physiotherapy.
    *   Your plan clearly outlines your daily schedule of therapies, diet, and activities.

3.  **Daily Geothermal Hydrotherapy & Integrated Treatments**
    This is where your deep, natural healing begins. Each day, you will immerse your body in the therapeutic, mineral-rich waters of the natural hot springs, complemented by the other personalized treatments prescribed in your protocol.
    *   Begin your healing with daily prescribed sessions in the natural Sulphur hot springs.
    *   Receive your supporting Ayurvedic, Naturopathic, or Physiotherapy treatments from our skilled team.
    *   All therapies are delivered in a sequence designed to maximize their synergistic, healing effects.

4.  **Naturopathic Diet & Healing Nutrition**
    We practice the principle that food is medicine. Your meals are a core component of your treatment, personally prescribed by our doctor to support your healing process, reduce inflammation, and aid detoxification.
    *   Enjoy a simple, wholesome, and therapeutic vegetarian diet designed for healing.
    *   Your meal plan is customized to align with your specific health condition and treatment goals.
    *   This naturopathic approach cleanses your body from the inside out, accelerating your recovery.

5.  **Daily Medical Review & Progress Monitoring**
    Your health and safety are paramount. Unlike a general retreat, your progress is closely monitored with daily medical supervision, ensuring your treatment is both effective and responsive to your body's needs.
    *   Our resident doctor will personally check on your progress every single day.
    *   Your treatment plan is continuously assessed and can be fine-tuned for the best results.
    *   This high level of care provides peace of mind, especially for those with chronic conditions.

6.  **Discharge Plan & Lifestyle Guidance**
    Our support for you doesn’t end when you leave. We empower you with the knowledge to continue your healing journey at home. Before you depart, you will receive a comprehensive discharge plan and final consultation.
    *   Receive a detailed lifestyle and diet plan to follow after your stay.
    *   We provide guidance on simple exercises and practices to maintain your health.
    *   You leave not just treated, but empowered with the tools for a lifetime of well-being.
