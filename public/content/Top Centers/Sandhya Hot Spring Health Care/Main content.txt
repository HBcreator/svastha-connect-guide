**About Sandhya Health Care**
Welcome to Sandhya Hot Spring Health Care, a pioneering wellness destination nestled in the serene Himalayan landscape of Tattapani, on the banks of the Sutlej River. Our sanctuary is founded on the profound therapeutic power of natural Sulphur hot springs. Here, this geothermal gift of nature is integrated with the ancient sciences of Ayurveda and Naturopathy to create a unique and powerful healing experience, guided by the vision of our founder, Dr. Sandhya.

### Our Philosophy & Approach
Our philosophy is rooted in the belief that nature is the most potent healer. The geothermal hot springs of Tattapani, rich in Sulphur and other vital minerals, are a living medicine. Our approach is to harness this powerful natural resource and integrate it with a multi-disciplinary system of care. We combine the external purification from the springs with the internal balance offered by Ayurveda, Naturopathy, and modern Physiotherapy to address the root cause of illness, not just the symptoms.

**The Sandhya Healing Journey**
Your path to natural wellness is a supportive and medically supervised three-step process.

1.  **Consult & Diagnose**
    Your journey begins with an in-depth consultation with our resident doctor. Through a careful assessment of your medical history and health concerns, we diagnose the underlying imbalances and create your unique health profile.

2.  **Integrate & Customize**
    Based on the diagnosis, our experts design your personalized treatment protocol. This integrated plan will specify the duration and type of hot spring therapies, along with a supporting schedule of Ayurvedic treatments, naturopathic diet, and physiotherapy sessions.

3.  **Immerse & Heal**
    Begin your healing by immersing yourself in the therapeutic waters. Your daily treatments are delivered by our skilled team, and your progress is consistently monitored by our doctors to ensure you receive the most effective and transformative care possible.



**Systems of Medicine Practiced**
Our unique approach to health is built on the synergy of four powerful healing modalities.

1.  **Geothermal Hydrotherapy**
    This is the heart of our center. We utilize the natural, mineral-rich hot Sulphur water in specialized pools and private baths to treat chronic skin diseases, arthritis, and muscular pain, harnessing its proven anti-inflammatory and detoxifying properties.

2.  **Ayurveda & Panchakarma**
    We practice classical Ayurveda to complement the hydrotherapy. Authentic therapies and herbal medicines work to purify your body from within, balance your *doshas*, and address the internal root cause of your ailments for deep, lasting health.

3.  **Naturopathy & Yoga**
    We believe in healing through lifestyle. Our naturopathic approach includes a customized diet plan, mud therapy, and gentle yoga sessions conducted in the pristine Himalayan air, all designed to detoxify your system and harmonize your mind and body.

4.  **Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation**
    We integrate modern physiotherapy to enhance your healing, especially for musculoskeletal issues. Our experts use targeted exercises and techniques to restore mobility, relieve pain, and strengthen your body, ensuring a comprehensive recovery.
