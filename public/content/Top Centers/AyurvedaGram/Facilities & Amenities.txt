### Facilities & Amenities

At AyurvedaGram, every facility is a part of our unique heritage village, thoughtfully designed to support your healing in an environment of authenticity and serenity.

**Authentic Heritage Accommodations**

Rest and recover in a genuine piece of history, a traditional wooden cottage transplanted from Kerala.

*   Stay in one of our authentic, antique wooden cottages, some of which are centuries old.

*   Each cottage is a unique architectural masterpiece, offering a truly immersive cultural experience.

*   Enjoy modern comforts seamlessly blended with the classic elegance of traditional Kerala architecture.

**NABH-Accredited Hospital Facility**

Experience your healing journey in a facility that meets the highest standards of clinical excellence.

*   Trust in our care, certified by India's highest authority for healthcare quality and patient safety.

*   Our **'Green Leaf' certification** is your guarantee from the Government of Kerala for authentic Ayurveda.

*   This accreditation blends our traditional wisdom with modern standards of patient care and hygiene.

**Authentic Treatment Rooms**

Experience traditional therapies in spaces designed for your comfort, privacy, and authentic healing.

*   Heal in comfort in our dedicated, private therapy rooms for both men and women.

*   Our spaces are equipped for classical Ayurvedic procedures, ensuring maximum therapeutic benefit.

*   We maintain the highest standards of hygiene and safety for your complete peace of mind.

**Physician Consultation Rooms**

A private and professional space for deep, personal conversations with your expert physician.

*   This is where your unique healing journey is crafted through a deep, traditional diagnosis.

*   Experience the ancient art of pulse reading (*Nadi Pariksha*) with our skilled doctors.

*   Benefit from our approach of regular, unhurried consultations to monitor your progress.

**Wholesome Ayurvedic Dining**

Savor delicious meals where food is treated as the first and most powerful medicine.

*   Enjoy simple, wholesome, and delicious vegetarian meals prepared according to classical Ayurvedic principles.

*   Your meals are customized by our physicians to support your specific treatment plan and balance your *doshas*.

*   This nourishing cuisine helps to heal your body from the inside out, amplifying your results.

**Yoga & Meditation Hall**

Harmonize your mind and body in our serene spaces dedicated to mindful and therapeutic practice.

*   Find your inner peace in our dedicated 'Yoga Shala,' a space designed for authentic practice.

*   Our therapeutic yoga sessions are designed to complement your medical treatments.

*   Learn powerful pranayama and meditation techniques to calm your mind and accelerate healing.

**Serene Village Environment**

Heal in a naturally therapeutic environment, surrounded by lush greenery and the sounds of nature.

*   Our entire village is a green sanctuary, with beautiful gardens, medicinal plants, and tranquil spaces.

*   The serene, pollution-free atmosphere is a powerful medicine in itself, calming your nervous system.

*   This powerful connection with nature is an integral and essential part of your healing journey.

**Housekeeping & Support Services**

Feel completely supported with our attentive, professional, and discreet service.

*   Relax in a clean and hygienic environment with our meticulous daily housekeeping.

*   Our attentive and caring staff are always available to support your needs with a personal touch.

*   Our focus is on providing a comfortable, healing-focused environment, not on hotel frills.

**Transportation & Accessibility**

Enjoy a seamless journey to our secluded and peaceful heritage village sanctuary.

*   We are conveniently located just outside of Bangalore, near the International Airport (BLR).

*   We are happy to help you coordinate your transportation arrangements for a stress-free arrival.

*   Our location provides the perfect blend of easy accessibility and a complete escape from city life.
