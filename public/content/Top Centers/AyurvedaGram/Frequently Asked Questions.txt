### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness and rejuvenation programs, we recommend a minimum of 7 days to experience tangible benefits. For our medical treatment programs, a stay of 14 days or more is typically required for deep and effective healing, as advised by our physicians.

**2. Are the cottages real antique houses from Kerala?**

Yes, absolutely. This is what makes AyurvedaGram truly unique. Our accommodations are genuine antique wooden homes, some centuries old, that were meticulously dismantled in Kerala and reassembled, beam by beam, to create our heritage healing village. You are not just staying in a room; you are living in a piece of history.

**3. Why is a "Kerala" village located in Bangalore?**

AyurvedaGram was created to make the authentic healing environment of a traditional Kerala village accessible to a global audience, without the need for long travel to remote areas. By bringing the heritage of Kerala to the serene outskirts of Bangalore, we offer the best of both worlds: authentic healing and convenient accessibility.

**4. What is the difference between AyurvedaGram and a typical wellness resort?**

Our primary focus is on authentic, classical Ayurveda in a genuine heritage setting, not on luxury resort frills. We are a NABH-accredited hospital facility where your therapeutic outcome is the main priority. The entire environment is designed to support deep healing, not just a relaxing holiday.

**5. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a delicious and wholesome vegetarian diet based on classical Ayurvedic principles. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are customized by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha* and health condition, helping you to heal from the inside out.

**6. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, it is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our Ayurvedic physicians will review them during your initial consultation and advise you on how to manage them safely and effectively during your stay.

**7. How do I get to the centre?**

We are conveniently located on the outskirts of Bangalore, making us easily accessible from the Kempegowda International Airport (BLR). We are happy to help you coordinate your airport transfers for a seamless and stress-free journey to our peaceful heritage village.

**8. Are the treatments really personalized?**

Yes, completely. There are no one-size-fits-all packages here. Following your comprehensive initial consultation with our expert physicians, your entire treatment plan—from the specific therapies to your daily diet—is crafted exclusively for you and is adjusted based on your progress.

**9. Can a companion who is not taking treatment stay with me?**

Yes, companions are welcome to stay with you and enjoy the serene heritage environment and our healthy Ayurvedic cuisine. Please note that treatments are only provided to those who have undergone a consultation and have a prescribed wellness plan.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable cotton clothing suitable for Bangalore's pleasant climate. Please also bring any personal toiletries, your medical records, and a good book. We will provide towels and any necessary attire for your treatments.
