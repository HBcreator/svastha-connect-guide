
### Facilities & Amenities

**Luxurious Private Pool Villas**
*   Experience ultimate seclusion and comfort in our magnificent, eco-conscious villas, each featuring its own private pool or plunge pool and secluded garden.
*   These spacious sanctuaries are exquisitely designed with a blend of traditional Keralan aesthetics and modern five-star luxury.
*   Enjoy the ultimate in privacy for your healing journey, with personalized service and serene, natural surroundings.

**NABH-Accredited 'Aryavaidyasala' (Ayurveda Hospital)**
*   Heal with confidence in our award-winning, state-of-the-art wellness centre, fully accredited for the highest standards of quality and patient safety.
*   Our facility features numerous private and hygienic therapy rooms designed for authentic Ayurvedic and Panchakarma treatments.
*   Benefit from a professional medical environment, including on-site doctor consultation rooms and a well-stocked herbal pharmacy.

**Gourmet Wellness Restaurants & Dining**
*   Nourish your body at our elegant sea-facing restaurants, where gourmet cuisine and holistic wellness converge.
*   Savour personalized Ayurvedic menus at our "Ida" restaurant or enjoy multi-cuisine seafood delicacies at "Chimaera."
*   Unwind at the "Pooldside Bar" with healthy juices and infusions in a stunning tropical setting.

**Marari Beachfront Access**
*   Step directly from our lush resort grounds onto the pristine, sun-kissed sands of Marari Beach, one of Kerala’s most beautiful coastlines.
*   Enjoy private, serene beach access for peaceful sunrise walks, evening meditation, or simply relaxing to the sound of the waves.
*   The tranquil, natural energy of the Arabian Sea is an integral part of the healing environment at Carnoustie.

**Yoga & Meditation Centre**
*   Find your inner balance at our dedicated yoga and meditation pavilions, designed for serene and mindful practice.
*   Join daily sessions guided by our resident yoga masters in a peaceful environment that enhances your connection with yourself.
*   Benefit from personalized instruction tailored to your experience level and specific wellness goals.

**Central Swimming Pool & Fitness Centre**
*   Refresh your body in our large, beautifully designed swimming pool, a stunning centerpiece nestled within our tropical gardens.
*   Maintain your fitness routine in our fully equipped, state-of-the-art gymnasium with modern cardio and strength-training equipment.
*   Enjoy a holistic approach to wellness that balances restorative therapies with invigorating physical activity.

**Houseboats on the Backwaters**
*   Extend your wellness journey with an unforgettable stay on our luxurious, fully-equipped traditional Keralan houseboats.
*   Experience the serene and mesmerizing beauty of the Alleppey backwaters with all the comforts of our resort.
*   This unique facility offers a perfect way to immerse yourself in the tranquil culture and landscape of "God's Own Country."

**Organic Farm & Gardens**
*   Experience true farm-to-fork freshness, with many ingredients for your meals sourced directly from our on-site organic farm.
*   Heal in a sprawling, oxygen-rich environment with beautifully landscaped gardens, lush lawns, and serene water bodies.
*   The purity of our environment and the freshness of our produce are core components of our healing philosophy.

**Recreational & Business Facilities**
*   Nourish your mind in our well-stocked library or stay active with activities like cycling within the resort.
*   Host exclusive corporate wellness retreats or high-level meetings in our sophisticated, fully-equipped conference hall.
*   Benefit from a range of five-star services, including a travel desk, concierge, and cultural program arrangements.
