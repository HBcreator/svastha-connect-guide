### Insurance & Payment Info

Making your path to our luxurious healing sanctuary clear and accessible.

**Insurance Coverage**

*   As a NABH-accredited hospital, many of our medical treatments are eligible for coverage by Indian health insurance providers.

*   Our professional administrative team provides all necessary medical documentation to support your reimbursement process.

*   We are committed to helping you navigate the claims process as smoothly as possible.

*   We strongly advise you to confirm coverage details and eligibility directly with your insurance provider before your stay.

**Payment Options**

*   We accept all major international payment methods, including credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) and domestic/international bank transfers.

*   Our pricing is completely transparent, with all costs for your all-inclusive program clearly outlined at the time of booking.

*   Our all-inclusive packages cover your luxurious villa accommodation, personalized gourmet wellness meals, all scheduled treatments, and daily wellness activities.

*   You will receive a detailed cost estimate before you confirm your booking, with absolutely no hidden charges.

**For International Patients**

International guests are encouraged to have a detailed discussion with their insurance providers regarding coverage for Ayurvedic treatments at our NABH-accredited hospital. Our team is highly experienced in providing the necessary, detailed medical reports and documentation in the required format to support your international insurance claims.
