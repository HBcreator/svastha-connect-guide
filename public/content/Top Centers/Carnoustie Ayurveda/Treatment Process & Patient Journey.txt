### Treatment Process & Patient Journey


Your personalized path to profound well-being, guided with clinical expertise and five-star grace at every step.

1.  **Arrival at Your Private Villa & In-Depth Physician's Consultation**
    Your journey begins in the exclusive sanctuary of your private pool villa. Once settled, you will have a comprehensive, one-on-one consultation with our senior Ayurvedic physician in our NABH-accredited hospital wing.
    *   This is a private, thorough medical consultation led by a highly qualified Vaidya.
    *   Through traditional diagnostics, we create a deep understanding of your health, constitution, and goals.
    *   This foundational step ensures your entire program is built on a solid, scientific Ayurvedic framework.

2.  **Curating Your Bespoke Wellness Regimen**
    Based on your personal consultation, our physicians will craft your exclusive and detailed wellness regimen. This is not a generic package, but your unique, medically prescribed roadmap to healing, balancing therapy, diet, and yoga.
    *   This is your 100% customized, physician-led path to achieving your specific health objectives.
    *   It meticulously outlines your daily schedule of Ayurvedic therapies, herbal medicines, and activities.
    *   Your plan is a perfect synthesis of ancient healing wisdom and modern luxury living.

    This is where your deep physical healing and rejuvenation begin. Each day, you will receive your prescribed, powerful treatments, administered with exceptional skill and care by our certified therapists in our serene, world-class 'Aryavaidyasala'.

    We believe that food is powerful medicine that can also be a gourmet delight. Your meals are a vital part of your treatment, personally prescribed by your doctor and artfully crafted by our chefs to be both delicious and therapeutic.

5.  **Integrated Wellness Practices & Daily Medical Review**
    Your healing is a holistic experience, supported by mindful practices and constant medical supervision. Your day includes personalized yoga and meditation sessions, with your progress carefully monitored daily by our physicians.
    *   Harmonize your mind and body with one-on-one yoga and meditation sessions.
