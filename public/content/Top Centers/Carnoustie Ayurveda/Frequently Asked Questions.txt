### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness programs, a minimum of 7 nights is recommended. For our more intensive medical or detoxification programs like Panchakarma, a stay of 14 nights or more is typically required for deep, effective, and lasting healing, as advised by our physicians.

**2. What is the accommodation like?**

You will be staying in your own private, luxuriously appointed villa, many of which feature their own private pool or timber deck. Our accommodations are designed to provide the highest level of five-star comfort, privacy, and tranquility, all inspired by the beauty of Keralan design.

**3. What makes Carnoustie different from other luxury resorts in Kerala?**

While we offer world-class luxury, our heart is a serious, **NABH-accredited Ayurvedic hospital**. Our key difference is the deep clinical expertise of our six resident doctors, our unique integration of Marma and Pranic healing, and our proven track record in treating complex chronic conditions.

**4. What kind of food is served?**

You will be served a delicious, gourmet wellness cuisine, crafted by our award-winning Executive Chef. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are strictly prescribed by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha* and health goals, using fresh, organic ingredients.

**5. How do I get to the resort?**

Our resort is located on the pristine Marari Beach in Kerala. We are conveniently accessible from the Cochin International Airport (COK), which is approximately a 1.5-hour scenic drive away. We are happy to arrange your private luxury airport transfers for a seamless journey.

**6. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions and recent medical records. Our physicians will review them during your comprehensive initial consultation to create a safe and effective treatment plan for you.

**7. Can my companion stay with me if they are not on a wellness program?**

Yes, your companion is welcome to stay with you in your luxurious villa. They can enjoy our beautiful resort, our swimming pools, the pristine beach, and our gourmet wellness cuisine. Please note that personalized treatments are only for guests on a specific wellness program.

**8. Do I need to be experienced in yoga?**

Not at all. Our daily yoga and meditation sessions are designed to be therapeutic and are suitable for all levels, from complete beginners to advanced practitioners. Our masters provide personalized guidance to ensure the practice is safe, enjoyable, and perfectly supportive of your healing journey.

**9. What is Marma and Pranic Healing?**

These are powerful, traditional healing sciences that we uniquely integrate into our programs. **Marma** is the science of healing the body's vital energy points, while **Pranic Healing** works to clear and balance your body's energy field. Our physicians may recommend these therapies for deep physical and emotional healing.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing for yoga and relaxation, as well as smart casual attire for evenings. Please also bring swimwear, any personal toiletries, and your medical records. We will provide all other necessary amenities for a luxurious and comfortable stay.
