### Treatment Process & Patient Journey

Your personalized path to well-being, guided by ancient wisdom amidst award-winning luxury.

1.  **Arrival in a Living Heritage Museum & Consultation**
    Your journey begins as you are welcomed into our magnificent resort, a living museum of Keralan heritage. You will then have a comprehensive, one-on-one consultation at our esteemed 'Ayurmana' with a senior Ayurvedic physician to determine your unique constitution and wellness needs.
    *   This is a thorough consultation in our historic, 200-year-old Ayurveda centre.
    *   Through traditional diagnostics, our Vaidyas create a deep understanding of your health.
    *   This foundational step ensures your healing journey is both authentic and personalized.

2.  **Curating Your Bespoke Wellness Itinerary**
    Based on your personal consultation, our physicians will design your exclusive wellness itinerary. This is not a rigid plan, but a bespoke journey that harmoniously blends authentic healing therapies with the luxurious and serene experiences of our backwater resort.
    *   This is your unique, physician-led path to restoring balance and vitality.
    *   It meticulously outlines your daily schedule of specific Ayurvedic therapies, diet, and activities.
    *   Your plan is a perfect synthesis of ancient healing wisdom and five-star comfort.

3.  **Daily Authentic Ayurvedic Therapies**
    This is where your deep physical healing begins. Each day, you will receive your prescribed treatments, administered with exceptional skill and care by our certified therapists in the tranquil and historic sanctuary of Ayurmana.
    *   You will receive your personalized treatments daily in our clean, peaceful, and traditional therapy rooms.
    *   All therapies are performed using authentic, classical techniques and high-quality herbal preparations.
    *   The serene, heritage environment enhances the profound, healing effects of every therapy.

4.  **Gourmet Wellness Cuisine**
    We believe that food can be both healing and a gourmet experience. Our master chefs, in consultation with our Ayurvedic physicians, will guide you through our award-winning culinary offerings to create a diet that is both delicious and supportive of your treatment.
    *   Enjoy personalized dining at our magnificent "Ettukettu" restaurant.
    *   Savour authentic Keralan and international dishes, artfully prepared to align with your wellness goals.
    *   This unique culinary approach ensures your healing journey is a truly indulgent one.

5.  **Holistic Pursuits & Physician's Review**
    Your healing journey is a comprehensive experience, enriched by the soul of the backwaters and monitored with professional care. Your day includes mindful practices like yoga by the lake, with your progress carefully reviewed by our physicians.
    *   Harmonize your mind and body with yoga on the shores of Vembanad Lake or a meditative sunset cruise.
    *   Your progress is reviewed by our resident Vaidyas to ensure your journey is safe and effective.
    *   This combination of mindful activity and medical diligence ensures a truly holistic experience.

6.  **Departure with Lasting Serenity**
    Our support for you culminates in empowering you with the tools for a lifetime of well-being. Before you depart, you will receive a comprehensive final consultation with your physician to prepare you for your return home.
    *   Receive a detailed and personalized plan, including dietary and lifestyle advice.
    *   You leave not just rejuvenated, but with a deeper understanding of how to maintain your health.
    *   You depart our sanctuary empowered with the wisdom of Ayurveda and the timeless serenity of the backwaters.
