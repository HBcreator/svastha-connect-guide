### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness programs, a minimum of 7 nights is recommended. For our more intensive medical or detoxification programs like Panchakarma, a stay of 14 nights or more is typically required for deep, effective, and lasting healing, as advised by our physicians.

**2. What is the accommodation like?**

You will be staying in luxurious, authentic heritage villas and suites, some of which are centuries-old traditional Kerala homes that have been meticulously transplanted and restored. Many of our accommodations feature private pools and stunning views of the serene Vembanad Lake.

**3. Is this a hotel or a serious Ayurveda center?**

We are a unique blend of both. Our 'Ayurmana' spa is a world-class, accredited Ayurvedic hospital dedicated to authentic, physician-led healing. This is all set within the environment of one of India's most awarded luxury resorts, giving you the best of both clinical excellence and five-star comfort.

**4. What kind of food is served?**

You will be served a delicious and wholesome vegetarian diet based on classical Ayurvedic principles. Our meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are customized by our physicians. However, our resort also features other award-winning restaurants for any companions not on a wellness program.

**5. How do I get to the resort?**

Our resort is located on the serene banks of Lake Vembanad in Kumarakom, Kerala. The nearest airport is Cochin International Airport (COK), which is approximately a scenic 1.5 to 2-hour drive away. We are happy to arrange your private luxury airport transfers.

**6. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our physicians will review them during your comprehensive initial consultation and advise you on how to manage them safely during your stay.

**7. Can my family or a companion stay with me?**

Absolutely. Kumarakom Lake Resort is a perfect destination for families and companions. While you are on your wellness program, they can enjoy our other restaurants, the infinity pool, sunset cruises on the lake, and a wide range of other luxury resort activities.

**8. Do I need to be experienced in yoga?**

Not at all. Our daily yoga and meditation sessions, held in our beautiful, traditional yoga pavilion, are designed for all levels, from complete beginners to advanced practitioners. Our experienced masters provide personalized guidance to ensure the practice is safe and supportive.

**9. What makes Kumarakom Lake Resort so special?**

Our uniqueness lies in the perfect fusion of a legendary, award-winning luxury resort with a truly authentic and accredited Ayurvedic hospital. It is a place where you can experience a deep, medically supervised healing journey without compromising on five-star comfort and service.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing suitable for a warm, tropical climate, as well as smart casual attire for evenings. Please also bring swimwear, any personal toiletries, and your medical records. We will provide all other necessary amenities for a luxurious stay.
