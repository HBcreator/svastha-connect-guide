### Facilities & Amenities

**Exquisite Heritage Accommodations**
*   Stay in meticulously restored 16th-century traditional Keralan homesteads ('tharavadu'), offering a unique, living-heritage experience.
*   Indulge in ultimate luxury with our heritage villas, many featuring private courtyards and personal swimming pools.
*   Our magnificent Presidential Suite offers a private, royal sanctuary with its own pool on the tranquil shores of the lake.

**'Ayurmana' - The Heritage Ayurveda Centre**
*   Receive your authentic healing treatments in our dedicated Ayurveda centre, itself a 200-year-old heritage building ('mana').
*   Our facility is a serene sanctuary with professional, hygienic therapy rooms designed for all classical Ayurvedic treatments.
*   Benefit from a genuine, physician-led environment where ancient wisdom is practiced with deep respect and authenticity.

**Meandering Pool & Infinity Pools**
*   Swim and relax in our spectacular 250-meter-long meandering swimming pool that gracefully winds its way through the resort.
*   Enjoy breathtaking, uninterrupted views of Vembanad Lake from our stunning infinity pool.
*   These magnificent water bodies are the heart of the resort, echoing the tranquil spirit of the backwaters.

**Award-Winning Heritage Dining**
*   Dine in our majestic main restaurant, 'Ettukettu', a 200-year-old, eight-sided palace that was masterfully reassembled on our grounds.
*   Savour fresh seafood delicacies at our specialty restaurant, 'Vembanad', set on the shores of the lake.
*   Enjoy a cup of traditional tea at 'Thattukada', our charming village-style tea shop.

**Luxury Backwater Houseboats**
*   Embark on an unforgettable journey on our traditional Keralan houseboats ('kettuvallam'), luxuriously appointed for your comfort.
*   Experience the profound serenity of the backwaters with a day cruise or a magical overnight stay on the lake.
*   This unique facility offers a perfect way to immerse yourself in the tranquil culture and landscape of "God's Own Country."

**Yoga & Meditation Centre**
*   Harmonize your mind and body at our dedicated yoga and meditation pavilion, located in a serene setting on the lakefront.
*   Join daily sessions guided by our experienced instructors, with the tranquil waters providing an inspiring backdrop.
*   This peaceful space is central to our holistic approach to well-being, complementing your Ayurvedic journey.

**Health Club & Fitness Centre**
*   Maintain your fitness regimen in our fully equipped, modern gymnasium, located within our heritage Ayurmana building.
*   Benefit from a comprehensive range of cardio and strength-training equipment in a serene, air-conditioned environment.
*   Enjoy a holistic approach to wellness that balances restorative therapies with invigorating physical activity.

**Cultural Immersion & Activities**
*   Enrich your mind with daily evening performances of traditional Keralan art forms like Kathakali and classical music.
*   Connect with the local way of life through curated activities such as pottery lessons, village walks, and fishing.
*   Our resort is a living theatre of Keralan culture, offering a deep and soulful experience beyond wellness treatments.

**Business Centre & Conference Facilities**
*   Host exclusive corporate wellness retreats or high-level meetings in our sophisticated, fully-equipped conference hall.
*   Benefit from state-of-the-art technology and a dedicated events team to curate a flawless and prestigious event.
*   Our serene, inspiring environment provides the perfect backdrop for productive and rejuvenating business gatherings.
