### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay for a wellness immersion?**

Our wellness immersions are designed for deep, transformative results and typically have a minimum duration, often starting from 4 nights. For our more intensive medical or detoxification programs like Panchakarma, a stay of 14 nights or more is highly recommended.

**2. How do I get to Amanbagh?**

Amanbagh is a secluded sanctuary nestled in the Aravalli Hills of Rajasthan. The nearest major airport is Jaipur International Airport (JAI), which is approximately a scenic 2-hour drive from our retreat. Our world-class concierge team will be delighted to arrange your private luxury car transfers.

**3. What makes your Ayurvedic treatments "medical-grade"?**

Our Ayurvedic immersions are designed and supervised by a qualified resident Ayurvedic physician. Your journey begins with an in-depth, 90-minute consultation to create a precise and personalized clinical protocol. We focus on treating the root cause of your imbalances, not just providing a relaxing spa experience.

**4. What kind of food is served during an immersion?**

You will be served a delicious and nourishing gourmet wellness cuisine. Our chefs work in close collaboration with our Ayurvedic physician to create a menu that is customized to your specific *dosha*, your health goals, and the specific stage of your treatment, using fresh, organic ingredients from our own gardens.

**5. I have a serious medical condition. Is Amanbagh suitable for me?**

Amanbagh has a renowned reputation for successfully managing a wide range of complex and chronic conditions where conventional medicine may have offered limited hope. Your journey will begin with a detailed pre-arrival consultation with our Wellness Concierge to ensure our programs are the right fit for your specific medical needs.

**6. Do I need to be experienced in yoga to attend?**

Not at all. We offer private, one-on-one yoga and meditation sessions that are tailored to your specific body type, fitness level, and wellness goals. Our experienced masters provide personal guidance to ensure the practice is safe, enjoyable, and perfectly suited to you.

**7. Can a companion stay with me if they are not on a wellness immersion?**

Absolutely. Your companion is welcome to stay with you in our luxurious accommodations and enjoy all the world-class facilities of Amanbagh, including our main swimming pool, our restaurants, and the serene beauty of the Aravalli Hills.

**8. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing for yoga and other activities, as well as smart casual attire for evenings. Please also bring any personal toiletries and your medical records. We will provide you with all the necessary amenities for a luxurious and comfortable stay.

**9. What is a "Wellness Concierge"?**

Your Wellness Concierge is your personal guide who will connect with you two weeks before your arrival. They will conduct a detailed pre-arrival assessment of your health history and goals to ensure that your immersion is perfectly prepared and customized for you from the moment you arrive.

**10. Do you serve alcohol at the retreat?**

To honor our commitment to a pure and holistic healing environment during our wellness immersions, your personalized menu will be alcohol-free. This allows for a deeper and more authentic detoxification and rejuvenation experience for all our guests on a wellness path.
