### Treatment Process & Patient Journey


Your bespoke path to well-being, intuitively guided with serene grace at every sacred moment.

1.  **The Arrival & The Wellness Consultation**
    Your journey begins in the profound silence of our palatial sanctuary. Upon arrival, you will have a private, in-depth consultation with our resident wellness expert and Ayurvedic physician to explore your physical, mental, and spiritual goals.
    *   This is a deeply personal and unhurried conversation in a serene and confidential setting.
    *   Through traditional Ayurvedic diagnostics, we gain a deep understanding of your unique constitution.
    *   We listen intuitively to understand your needs and lay the foundation for your transformative journey.

2.  **Curating Your Bespoke Wellness Pathway**
    Based on your consultation, our physician will curate your completely bespoke Wellness Immersion. This is not a pre-set menu, but a living, breathing pathway where every element of your stay is thoughtfully integrated to support your healing.
    *   This is your unique, physician-led roadmap to achieving your specific wellness objectives.
    *   Your pathway holistically combines your spa treatments, personalized diet, yoga, and mindful activities.
    *   Your journey is designed to be a seamless and harmonious flow, with each day building upon the last.

3.  **The Daily Ritual of Healing**
    This is where your profound transformation unfolds. Each day, your prescribed therapies are administered in the tranquil sanctuary of the Aman Spa by our highly skilled therapists, often using fresh ingredients hand-picked from our own organic gardens.
    *   You will receive your bespoke treatments in the sublime privacy of our luxurious spa suites.
    *   Every therapy is a mindful ritual, delivered with intuitive touch and profound intention.
    *   The purity and potency of our garden-fresh ingredients elevate the healing effects of each treatment.

4.  **The Sacred Art of Nourishment**
    We believe that nourishment is a sacred practice. Your meals are a vital and delicious component of your immersion, personally prescribed by your physician and artfully crafted by our chefs to heal your body from the inside out.
    *   Enjoy a true garden-to-table experience with personalized menus based on Ayurvedic principles.
    *   Your meals are designed to support your specific dosha, aid detoxification, and delight your senses.
    *   This mindful approach to food transforms eating into a deeply healing and pleasurable ritual.

5.  **Mindful Immersion in the Sanctuary**
    Your pathway to well-being extends far beyond the spa, weaving itself into the very fabric of Amanbagh. Your journey is balanced with private, mindful experiences designed to connect you with the spirit of our serene valley.
    *   Harmonize your mind and body with private yoga and meditation sessions in sacred, historic locations.
    *   Reconnect with nature through mindful walks in the Aravalli Hills or quiet contemplation in our Mughal gardens.
    *   The sacred silence of Amanbagh is a powerful medicine in itself, deepening your healing.

6.  **A Departure with Lasting Serenity**
    Our support for you culminates in empowering you for your journey onward. Before you depart, you will have a comprehensive final consultation with your physician to review your progress and create a plan to sustain your well-being.
    *   Receive a detailed and personalized plan with lifestyle, dietary, and mindfulness advice.
    *   You leave not just rejuvenated, but with a deeper understanding of your own path to balance.
    *   In the true spirit of Aman, you depart with a lasting sense of serenity and inner peace.
