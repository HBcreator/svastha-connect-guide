**About Amanbagh**
Welcome to Amanbagh, a contemporary Mughal palace nestled in a secluded valley in the ancient Aravalli Hills of Rajasthan. A true oasis of serenity, our name translates to "garden of peace," a promise we fulfill in every detail. Once the site of the Maharaja of Alwar's royal hunting lodge, this magnificent estate is now a sanctuary of palatial suites, domed cupolas, and shaded courtyards, crafted from local pink marble. Here, surrounded by lush gardens and sacred silence, we offer a profound journey into the heart of holistic wellness.

### Our Philosophy & Approach
Our philosophy is embodied in our name: Amanbagh, the garden of peace. We believe that true well-being arises from a state of profound tranquility, where the body, mind, and spirit are in perfect harmony with their surroundings. Our approach is to create a deeply personal and intuitive journey for each guest. We integrate the ancient wisdom of Ayurveda and Yoga not merely as treatments, but as a complete way of being, allowing the sacred stillness of our location to be the most potent healer of all.
