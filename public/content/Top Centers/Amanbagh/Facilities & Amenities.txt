### Facilities & Amenities

**Palatial Suites & Pavilions**
*   Retreat to your private sanctuary of sublime space and serenity in one of our 40 palatial suites and pavilions.
*   Many of our accommodations feature private courtyards or terraces, and several offer their own private swimming pools.
*   Experience timeless elegance with Mughal-inspired architecture, high domed ceilings, and exquisite, understated decor.

**The Aman Spa Sanctuary**
*   Discover a tranquil haven dedicated to holistic healing, featuring spacious double treatment suites, steam rooms, and serene relaxation areas.
*   Experience our bespoke wellness journeys, deeply rooted in Ayurvedic wisdom and tailored to your personal needs.
*   Our therapies are elevated with fresh, organic ingredients, often hand-picked from our own on-site gardens.

**The Main Swimming Pool Oasis**
*   Refresh your body and spirit in our magnificent, 33-metre swimming pool, the stunning emerald-green centrepiece of the retreat.
*   Relax in serene privacy on comfortable sun loungers set beneath the shade of elegant colonnades and domed cupolas.
*   Enjoy attentive, discreet poolside service in an atmosphere of profound peace and tranquility.

**Garden-to-Table Dining & Library Bar**
*   Nourish your body in our majestic dining rotunda, where menus are curated daily based on the freshest harvest from our organic gardens.
*   Savor a personalized culinary journey, with our chefs crafting bespoke meals to support your wellness program.
*   Unwind in the evenings at our elegant Library Bar, enjoying classic cocktails and a curated selection of fine literature.

**Expansive Mughal-Inspired Gardens**
*   Heal in a sprawling, two-acre sanctuary of peace, with lush gardens, fruit trees, and fragrant floral beds.
*   Our name, "Amanbagh" (garden of peace), is a living reality, with countless serene nooks for private contemplation and meditation.
*   The entire estate is a beautifully manicured oasis, creating a powerful sense of harmony and connection with nature.

**Private Yoga & Meditation Spaces**
*   Practice yoga and meditation in our serene, rooftop pavilion, offering breathtaking views of the surrounding Aravalli Hills.
*   For a truly profound experience, allow us to arrange a private sunrise yoga session amidst the sacred, 17th-century ruins of a nearby fort.
*   These dedicated spaces are designed to enhance your mindful practice in an atmosphere of complete tranquility.

**State-of-the-Art Fitness Pavilion**
*   Maintain your fitness regimen in our fully equipped, state-of-the-art gymnasium.
*   Benefit from a comprehensive range of modern cardio and strength-training equipment in a light-filled, serene space.
*   Enjoy a peaceful and private environment for your personal workout, overlooking the lush greenery of the gardens.

**The Aman Boutique & Library**
*   Browse our thoughtfully curated boutique, offering a selection of exquisite local crafts, textiles, jewellery, and wellness products.
*   Nourish your mind in our serene library, a quiet sanctuary filled with a rich collection of books on Indian culture, history, and philosophy.
*   Find the perfect memento to remember your journey or a captivating book to accompany you by the pool.

**Exclusive Cultural Excursions**
*   Our facilities extend into the rich landscape and culture of Rajasthan. We offer private, curated excursions to nearby villages, ancient temples, and the protected Sariska Tiger Reserve.
*   Embark on a mindful "Cow Dust" tour at sunset to witness timeless scenes of rural Indian life.
*   These bespoke journeys are an integral part of the Amanbagh experience, connecting you deeply to the spirit of the land.
