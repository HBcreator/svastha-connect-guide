### Wellness Programs

Guiding your personal journey to find serenity, beauty, and profound peace in our Mughal garden sanctuary.

**Amanbagh Ritual**
This is our signature spa journey, a head-to-toe experience that embodies the soul of our "garden of peace." It combines a gentle, exfoliating scrub using ingredients from our garden, followed by a flowing, rhythmic massage and a nourishing facial to induce a state of sublime tranquility.
*   A complete sensory journey to harmonize the mind, body, and spirit.
*   Bespoke to your needs using the freshest, hand-picked organic ingredients.
*   Restores a profound sense of inner peace and visible radiance.

**Sunrise Yoga at Ruins**
An iconic Amanbagh experience. Journey at dawn to the 17th-century ruins of the Ajabgarh Fort, where a private yoga and meditation session is held as the sun rises over the Aravalli Hills. It is a profoundly spiritual practice that connects you to the history and energy of the land.
*   A truly unforgettable, private yoga session in a sacred, ancient setting.
*   Harmonize your breath with the awakening landscape of Rajasthan.
*   A powerful experience of stillness, presence, and connection.

**Garden Nourishment**
A culinary journey that begins in our two-acre organic garden. This experience includes a guided tour of our gardens with our chef, followed by a personalized, interactive cooking session where you will learn the art of creating delicious and healthy Ayurvedic-inspired dishes.
*   Discover the vital connection between the earth, your food, and your well-being.
*   Learn the principles of mindful, seasonal eating from our culinary experts.
*   A holistic and delicious experience that nourishes you from the inside out.

**king & Queen Couple's**
Share a journey of serene connection in the magnificent privacy of our Aman Spa suite. This romantic ritual includes a shared aromatic bath, a synchronized massage, and other bespoke treatments, allowing you to relax and reconnect in an atmosphere of regal intimacy.
*   A luxurious and deeply intimate experience for two in a palatial setting.
*   Deepens connection through shared moments of profound tranquility.
*   The ultimate celebration of togetherness in a sanctuary of peace.

**Aravalli Mindful Trek**
A wellness journey that extends into the majestic landscape surrounding our retreat. This guided trek through the Aravalli Hills is a moving meditation, culminating in a scenic spot for a silent picnic and contemplation as you absorb the serene beauty of rural Rajasthan.
*   Connects physical activity with mindfulness and a deep appreciation for nature.
*   A unique way to experience the local culture and breathtaking landscape.
*   Rejuvenates the body and clears the mind in the open, fresh air.

**Royal Rajasthani Ritual**
A tribute to the legendary beauty rituals of the Rajput queens. This indulgent journey uses the hydrating and calming properties of fresh roses from our garden. It includes a gentle rose scrub, a nourishing rose-petal body wrap, and a soothing facial.
*   A deeply hydrating and beautifying ritual for luminous skin.
*   Utilizes the therapeutic and aromatic properties of pure, organic roses.
*   A truly pampering experience that leaves you feeling cherished and radiant.

**Motherhood Blessing**
A gentle and deeply nurturing experience for expectant mothers. This program uses safe, specialized massage techniques performed by our expert therapists to relieve the aches and strains of pregnancy in a serene and supportive environment.
*   Safe, supportive, and profoundly comforting for expectant mothers.
*   Eases back pain, improves circulation, and promotes deep, restful sleep.
*   A serene ritual that honors and celebrates the sacred journey of motherhood.

**Silent Temple Meditation**
A private journey to a nearby, secluded temple for a guided meditation experience. In the sacred silence of this ancient space, our wellness expert will lead you through a practice designed to quiet the mind, open the heart, and connect you with a deeper sense of inner peace.
*   A powerful opportunity to experience meditation in a spiritually charged environment.
*   Cultivate profound stillness away from all worldly distractions.
*   A deeply moving experience for spiritual seekers.

**Grounding Earth Ritual**
Reconnect with the stabilizing energy of the earth. This therapeutic program begins with a Himalayan salt foot soak, followed by a full-body massage using grounding aromatic oils and warm stones to release tension and restore your body’s natural equilibrium.
*   A deeply calming ritual for those feeling anxious or ungrounded.
*   Restores a sense of stability, security, and physical ease.
*   Helps to quiet an overactive mind and soothe the nervous system.

**Half-Day Garden**
For guests with limited time, this is a beautifully curated immersion into the essence of Amanbagh. The journey includes a signature massage using herbs from our garden, private time to relax by the pool, and a nourishing three-course Swasthya lunch.
*   A perfectly condensed yet profound taste of the Amanbagh wellness philosophy.
*   The ideal way to reset and rejuvenate in just a few hours.
*   Leave feeling refreshed, nourished, and deeply relaxed.

**Henna Blessing**
Immerse yourself in the artistic traditions of Rajasthan with this beautiful and meditative ritual. A local artist will adorn your hands and feet with intricate, natural henna designs in a peaceful garden setting, a practice believed to bring joy and good fortune.
*   A unique and mindful cultural experience.
*   The cooling properties of henna are naturally soothing for the body.
*   A beautiful and temporary adornment that connects you to local traditions.

**Full Moon & Chanting**
On select nights, experience the magical energy of the full moon with a special outdoor yoga session. This gentle, lunar-inspired practice is followed by a meditative chanting (mantra) session to help you align with the cycles of nature and manifest your intentions.
*   A powerful practice to channel the creative and reflective energy of the full moon.
*   Cultivates a deep connection with the natural world.
*   A serene and spiritually uplifting community experience.
