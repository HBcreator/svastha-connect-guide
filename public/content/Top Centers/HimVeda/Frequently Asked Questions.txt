### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our medical treatment programs, a stay of 3 weeks (21 days) is typically the minimum required for a deep and effective healing process. The exact duration will be recommended by our physicians based on your specific condition and health goals.

**2. What makes your treatment approach "dynamic"?**

It means your treatment plan is not rigid or pre-set. Our physicians assess your condition every single morning, and based on the subtle changes in your body, they calibrate and decide on the exact therapies for that day. This highly responsive and personalized approach ensures your healing is always perfectly attuned to your body's needs.

**3. What is the accommodation like?**

Our focus is 100% on your healing, not on luxury frills. We have an intimate facility with only 8 rooms, all of which are simple, clean, comfortable, and designed to provide a peaceful environment for rest. Our small size is our strength, as it allows for unparalleled personal attention.

**4. Will I be treated by the founders directly?**

Yes. Our founders, Dr. J.P. Guleria and Dr. Vibhor Sharma, are the Chief Physicians who personally lead the healing journey of every guest. They will conduct your consultations, design your dynamic treatment plan, and oversee your progress, ensuring you receive the highest level of expert care.

**5. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a simple, wholesome, and delicious vegetarian diet based on classical Ayurvedic principles. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are customized by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha* and the specific stage of your therapy.

**6. Do you treat complex conditions like pancreatitis?**

Yes. We have a renowned reputation for successfully treating complex and challenging conditions where conventional medicine may offer limited hope. Our deep diagnostic approach and dynamic, physician-led treatment plans have proven to be highly effective for a wide range of chronic ailments.

**7. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, it is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our physicians will review them during your initial consultation and advise you on how to manage them safely and effectively during your stay.

**8. How do I get to the centre?**

Our sanctuary is located in the serene foothills of the Himalayas, near Dharamshala. The nearest airport is Gaggal Airport (DHM) in Dharamshala. We are happy to help you coordinate your airport transfers for a seamless journey to our peaceful forest retreat.

**9. Can a companion who is not taking treatment stay with me?**

Due to our intimate, 8-room facility and our deep focus on a therapeutic environment, we typically accommodate only the individuals undergoing treatment. This ensures that the entire sanctuary remains a quiet, focused space for healing for all our guests.

**10. Do you offer online consultations?**

Yes. We understand that not everyone can travel for an extended stay. We offer online consultations and can ship our customized herbal medicines worldwide, allowing you to benefit from our expertise from the comfort of your own home.
