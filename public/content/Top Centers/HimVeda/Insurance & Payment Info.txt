### Insurance & Payment Info

Making your path to our authentic healing sanctuary clear and accessible.

**Insurance Coverage**

*   While we are a traditional healing center, some international insurance providers may offer coverage for wellness retreats or alternative therapies.

*   Our professional administrative team is happy to provide all necessary invoices and documentation to support your reimbursement process.

*   We are committed to helping you navigate the claims process as smoothly as possible.

*   We strongly advise you to confirm coverage details and eligibility directly with your insurance provider before your stay.

**Payment Options**

*   We accept all major payment methods, including credit/debit cards and domestic/international bank transfers.

*   Our pricing is completely transparent, with all costs clearly outlined in a personalized quotation after your consultation.

*   Our pricing reflects our commitment to 'healing over frills,' offering exceptional value for physician-led, highly personalized care.

*   You will receive a detailed cost estimate before your treatment begins, with absolutely no hidden charges.

**For International Patients**

International guests are encouraged to check with their home insurance providers regarding coverage for Ayurvedic treatments. Our administrative team is highly experienced in providing the necessary, detailed medical reports and documentation in the required format to support your international insurance claims.
