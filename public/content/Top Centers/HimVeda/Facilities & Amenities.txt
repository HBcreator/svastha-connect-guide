### Facilities & Amenities

At HimVeda, every facility is thoughtfully designed to support your healing in an environment of authenticity, intimacy, and natural serenity.

**Himalayan Forest Sanctuary**

Heal in a naturally therapeutic environment, surrounded on three sides by serene pine forests.

*   Our center is a peaceful, 2-acre campus nestled in the pristine foothills of the Himalayas.

*   Breathe in pure, pristine mountain air, free from the noise and pollution of city life.

*   This powerful connection with nature is an integral and essential part of your healing journey.

**Intimate & Healing Accommodations**

Rest and recover in our intimate 8-room facility, designed for focused, personal healing.

*   Our accommodations are purposefully designed for rest and recovery, free from modern distractions.

*   Enjoy clean, comfortable rooms with essential amenities and serene forest views.

*   Our intimate setting ensures a quiet, peaceful, and deeply personal healing experience for every guest.

**Physician-Led Clinical Facilities**

Experience your healing in a professional clinical setting, personally guided by our founding physicians.

*   Your healing journey is crafted in our private consultation rooms through a deep, traditional diagnosis.

*   Heal in comfort in our dedicated therapy rooms, designed for authentic Ayurvedic procedures.

*   We maintain the highest standards of hygiene and safety for your complete peace of mind.
