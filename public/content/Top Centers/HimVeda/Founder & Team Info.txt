### Founder & Team Info

**Our Physician-Founders**

Dr. J.P. Guleria & Dr. Vibhor Sharma

The vision for HimVeda was born from a shared dream between two dedicated Ayurvedic physicians, Dr. J.P. Guleria (MD Ayurveda) and Dr. Vibhor Sharma (MS Ayurveda). They met in college in 1999 and were united by a passion to create a sanctuary for pure, classical Ayurveda, not just superficial spa treatments. As the active leaders and chief physicians of the center, their deep clinical expertise and hands-on, personalized approach are the heart and soul of every healing journey at HimVeda.
