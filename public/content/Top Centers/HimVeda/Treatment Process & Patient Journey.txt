### Treatment Process & Patient Journey

Your path to healing at Himveda is a structured and deeply personal journey, rooted in the classical principles of Himalayan Ayurveda. We guide you through every stage, ensuring clarity, comfort, and comprehensive care from start to finish.

1.  **Deep-Dive Consultation**
    *   Your journey begins with an in-depth consultation (*Rogi Pariksha*) with our Vaidya.
    *   We utilize traditional diagnostic methods, including Pulse Diagnosis (*Nadi Pariksha*), to understand your unique constitution.
    *   This initial session focuses on your health history, lifestyle, and personal wellness goals.

2.  **Pinpointing the Root Cause**
    *   Our primary focus is on *Nidaan*—identifying the fundamental root cause of your health concerns, not just the symptoms.
    *   This precise diagnosis forms the unshakable foundation of your entire treatment plan.
    *   We ensure you have a clear understanding of your body's imbalances and the proposed path to restoring harmony.

3.  **Crafting Your Wellness Blueprint**
    *   Based on the diagnosis, a completely personalized treatment blueprint (*Chikitsa*) is designed exclusively for you.
    *   This plan intelligently combines detoxification therapies (*Shodhan*), pacifying herbal treatments (*Shaman*), and rejuvenation techniques.
    *   Your blueprint will detail the therapies, herbal medicines, and daily schedule for your healing journey.

4.  **Authentic Healing Therapies**
    *   You will receive authentic Ayurvedic treatments administered by our highly skilled and compassionate therapists.
    *   We use only classical, self-prepared herbal formulations to ensure maximum purity and potency.
    *   Each session is designed to purify the body, calm the mind, and awaken your innate healing capacity.

5.  **Personalized Diet & Lifestyle Plan**
    *   A crucial part of your healing is a customized diet plan (*Pathya-Apathya*) that aligns with your treatment goals.
    *   Our experts provide detailed guidance on supportive lifestyle routines, including daily schedules and activities.
    *   This empowers you with the knowledge to sustain your wellness long after you leave our center.

6.  **Continuous Support & Follow-Up**
    *   Your journey with us doesn't end when you leave; we provide a clear plan for continued wellness at home.
    *   We schedule regular follow-up consultations to monitor your progress and make any necessary adjustments.
    *   Our team remains dedicated to supporting your long-term health and well-being.
