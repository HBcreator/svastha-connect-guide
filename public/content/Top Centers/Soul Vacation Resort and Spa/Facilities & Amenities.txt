### Facilities & Amenities

At Soul Vacation, every facility is thoughtfully designed to support your healing in an environment of modern luxury and serene beauty.

**Greek-Inspired Luxury Accommodations**

Rest and recover in our beautifully designed rooms, where Greek elegance meets Goan tranquility.

*   Stay in luxurious, air-conditioned rooms and suites with a serene, minimalist aesthetic.

*   Enjoy modern amenities and spacious layouts for a truly comfortable and relaxing stay.

*   Each room is a peaceful sanctuary, thoughtfully designed to support your deep rejuvenation.

**Authentic Ayurvedic Treatment Center**

Experience the best of traditional healing, powered by our esteemed partners at KARE Ayurveda.

*   Heal in comfort in our dedicated, private therapy rooms designed for authentic Ayurvedic procedures.

*   Benefit from a deep, personal diagnosis with our expert in-house physicians.

*   Trust in our care, delivered in a clean, hygienic, and deeply peaceful environment.

**Nourishing Sattvic Dining**

Savor delicious meals where food is treated as the first and most powerful medicine.

*   Enjoy fresh, dosha-specific vegetarian meals, customized by our doctors to support your healing.

*   Our organic, vegan-friendly cuisine is consistently praised by our guests as both delicious and transformative.

*   Dine in our serene restaurant, where every meal is a nourishing and mindful experience.

**Therapeutic Yoga Spaces**

Harmonize your mind and body in our serene spaces dedicated to mindful and therapeutic practice.

*   We offer various styles, including Iyengar Yoga for alignment and restorative yoga for deep relaxation.

*   Our guided sessions are designed to perfectly complement your Ayurvedic treatments.

*   Practice in a calm and beautiful environment that supports your inner journey.

**The Central Swimming Pool**

Refresh your body and mind in our beautiful pool, the tranquil heart of our retreat.

*   Enjoy a gentle swim in our inviting outdoor pool, a perfect complement to your daily treatments.

*   Relax and unwind on comfortable poolside loungers with serene garden views.

*   This low-impact activity is a wonderful way to enhance your circulation and calm your mind.
