### Frequently Asked Questions About Soul Vacation

**1. Where is Soul Vacation located?**

Soul Vacation is nestled in a serene setting in South Goa, just a short walk from the beautiful Colva Beach. Its location provides the perfect peaceful backdrop to retreat from the hustle and bustle of everyday life.

**2. What kind of wellness treatments do you offer?**

We have an Ayurvedic Wellness & Medical center that offers a journey of holistic healing rooted in ancient wisdom. Our offerings include full body detox programs, day treatments, restorative yoga sessions, and various massage therapies, all guided by qualified doctors and trained practitioners.

**3. Are the treatments personalized?**

Yes, absolutely. All our Ayurvedic therapies are tailored to your unique body constitution, or *dosha*. This is determined during a detailed, one-on-one consultation with our in-house Ayurvedic doctor when you begin your wellness journey with us.

**4. What style of accommodation can I expect?**

Our rooms and villas feature a distinctive Mediterranean design with peaceful, white interiors to create a place of pure relaxation. All rooms are fully equipped with modern amenities, including an air conditioner, TV, WiFi, and luxurious bathroom facilities.

**5. Is there a restaurant at the resort?**

Yes, our in-house restaurant, Sunrise Café, serves a delicious mix of traditional Indian favourites and contemporary international cuisine. We also have the Sunset Bar, which offers great views of the pool and is a perfect spot to relax.

**6. Do you offer any packages or special deals?**

We regularly have special offers and packages, such as our "Summer of '24" or "Take KARE of You" deals. These often include accommodation, meals, wellness treatments, yoga sessions, and airport transfers to provide a complete and seamless holiday experience. We recommend checking our website for the latest offers.
