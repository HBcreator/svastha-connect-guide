### Vision & Team Info

Your journey to profound well-being, guided by a team of authentic healing experts.

Soul Vacation was born from a passionate vision to create a unique sanctuary in South Goa, blending Mediterranean-inspired tranquility with the deep healing wisdom of Ayurveda. The founders' dream was to build a purposeful haven where guests could truly escape modern pressures, reconnect with their inner peace, and experience authentic, traditional healing in an environment of modern luxury and genuine care.
