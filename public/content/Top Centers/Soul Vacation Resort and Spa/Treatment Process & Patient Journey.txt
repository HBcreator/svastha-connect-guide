**Treatment Process & Patient Journey**

Your personalized path to wellness at Soul Vacation is a seamless and supportive experience, guided by our KARE Ayurveda experts in our serene Goan sanctuary.

1.  **Initial Consultation & Dosha Analysis**

    Your journey begins with a deep, personal consultation to uncover your unique path to wellness.

    *   You will be personally guided by our expert, in-house Ayurvedic physician.

    *   Through traditional diagnostics like pulse reading, we identify your unique mind-body constitution (*dosha*).

    *   Our focus is on understanding the root cause of any imbalance, not just the symptoms.

2.  **Your Personal Wellness Blueprint**

    Based on your consultation, our doctors will design your exclusive roadmap to health, crafted only for you.

    *   This is not a generic package; it is your personal prescription for deep and lasting health.

    *   Your blueprint details your bespoke schedule of therapies, customized yoga, and lifestyle guidance.

    *   It includes a tailored diet plan and herbal support to perfectly balance your *doshas*.

3.  **Daily Healing & Expert Care**

    This is where your deep healing begins, with a daily synergy of Ayurvedic treatments and mindful practices.

    *   You will receive your prescribed Ayurvedic treatments each day from our highly skilled therapists.

    *   All therapies are performed with the expertise and quality assurance of our KARE Ayurveda partners.

    *   Your progress is closely monitored by our physicians to ensure your journey is safe and effective.

4.  **Nourishing Sattvic Cuisine**

    We believe that food is powerful medicine, and our meals are a delicious and vital part of your healing.

    *   Enjoy delicious, wholesome vegetarian meals prepared according to classical Ayurvedic principles.

    *   Your meals are customized by our physicians to support your specific treatment plan.

    *   This nourishing cuisine helps to heal your body from the inside out, amplifying your results.

5.  **Mindful Living by the Beach**

    Your wellness journey extends beyond the treatment room into a world of mindful relaxation and connection with nature.

    *   Harmonize your mind and body with daily therapeutic Yoga, including restorative and Iyengar styles.

    *   Reconnect with nature through peaceful walks to the nearby, serene Colva beach.

    *   Our tranquil, Greek-inspired sanctuary is a powerful medicine in itself, deepening your healing.

6.  **Empowerment for Your Journey Home**

    Our support for you doesn’t end when you leave; we empower you with the tools for a lifetime of wellness.

    *   Before you depart, you will have a comprehensive final consultation with your physician.

    *   We will provide you with a detailed plan, including dietary and lifestyle advice, to continue your progress.

    *   You leave not just rejuvenated, but empowered with the knowledge to maintain your health for years to come.
