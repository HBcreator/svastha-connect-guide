### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness programs, a minimum of 7 days is recommended. For our medical treatment programs, a stay of 14 days or more is typically required for deep, effective, and lasting healing, as advised by our physicians based on your condition.

**2. What does your "100-year pharmaceutical legacy" mean for my treatment?**

It means that the herbal medicines and oils used in your treatment come from the legendary Sitaram Ayurveda Pharmacy, a name trusted for quality and purity since 1921. You are benefiting from a century of trusted expertise in every therapy you receive.

**3. Will I be treated by Dr. Vignesh Devraj directly?**

As the Chief Physician and fourth-generation leader, Dr. Vignesh Devraj personally designs and oversees the care of every guest. He and his expert team will be deeply involved in your day-to-day care, ensuring you receive the highest level of personal and expert attention.

**4. What is the benefit of a high-altitude location in Munnar?**

The pure, cool, high-altitude mountain air is a powerful medicine in itself. It is naturally anti-inflammatory and particularly beneficial for those with respiratory and autoimmune conditions. The serene, pollution-free environment also has a profoundly calming effect on the mind and nervous system.

**5. What is the accommodation like? Is it a luxury resort?**

Our focus is 100% on your healing, not on luxury frills. Our accommodations are simple, clean, comfortable, and designed to provide a peaceful environment for rest, with breathtaking views of the mountains. We are a genuine healing hospital, not a 5-star hotel.

**6. Do you treat complex conditions like autoimmune disorders?**

Yes. We have a renowned reputation for successfully treating complex and challenging conditions, with a special focus on autoimmune disorders. Our unique high-altitude environment and physician-led protocols are ideal for calming an overactive immune response.

**7. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our physicians will review them during your initial consultation and advise you on how to manage them safely and effectively during your stay.

**8. How do I get to the retreat?**

Our mountain sanctuary is located in Munnar, Kerala, a beautiful and scenic journey from the Cochin International Airport (COK). We are happy to help you coordinate your airport transfers for a seamless and stress-free journey.

**9. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a simple, wholesome, and delicious vegetarian diet based on classical Ayurvedic principles. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are customized by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha* and health condition.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing suitable for a mountain climate, which can be cooler in the evenings. Please also bring any personal toiletries, your medical records, and a good book. We will provide towels and any necessary attire for your treatments.
