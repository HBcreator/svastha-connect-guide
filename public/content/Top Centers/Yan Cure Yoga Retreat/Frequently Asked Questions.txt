### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness and rejuvenation programs, we recommend a minimum stay of 7 days to experience tangible benefits. For our medical treatment programs or for a deeper transformation, a stay of 14 days or more is typically required.

**2. Will I be treated by the founder directly?**

Yes. Our founder and expert physician, Dr. Rana, personally guides the healing journey of every guest. He will conduct your initial consultation, design your personalized treatment plan, and oversee your progress, ensuring you receive the highest level of expert care.

**3. I am a beginner in Yoga. Is this retreat suitable for me?**

Absolutely. Our daily yoga sessions are designed for all levels, from complete beginners to advanced practitioners. Our experienced masters provide personal attention and modifications to ensure the practice is safe, enjoyable, and perfectly suited to your body and goals.

**4. What is the accommodation like?**

Our focus is on providing a clean, comfortable, and serene environment for your healing, not on luxury frills. Our rooms are simple yet well-appointed with all essential amenities, and many offer peaceful views of the tranquil lake, allowing you to rest and recover in a natural setting.

**5. How do I get to the retreat?**

We are located in Arambol, North Goa, nestled by a serene lake and just a short walk from the famous Arambol beach. The nearest airport is Manohar International Airport, Mopa (GOX). We are happy to help you coordinate your airport transfers for a seamless journey.

**6. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a simple, wholesome, and delicious vegetarian diet based on classical Ayurvedic and sattvic principles. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are customized by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha* and health condition.

**7. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, it is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our physicians will review them during your initial consultation and advise you on how to manage them safely and effectively during your stay.

**8. What is the difference between a wellness retreat and a Yoga Teacher Training Course (YTTC) at Yan Cure?**

Our wellness retreats are designed for your personal healing and rejuvenation, with a balanced schedule of Ayurvedic treatments and yoga. Our YTTC is an intensive, certified course for those who wish to become a Yoga Alliance registered teacher, focusing on the deep study and practice of yoga.

**9. Are the treatments really personalized?**

Yes, completely. There are no one-size-fits-all packages here. Following your comprehensive initial consultation with Dr. Rana, your entire treatment plan—from the specific therapies to your daily yoga practice—is crafted exclusively for you.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing suitable for both yoga practice and Goa's warm climate. Please also bring any personal toiletries, your medical records, and a good book. We will provide towels and any necessary attire for your treatments.
