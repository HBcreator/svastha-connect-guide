**Yan Cure: Your Personal Journey to Holistic Harmony**

**About Yan Cure**
Welcome to Yan Cure, a serene and intimate healing sanctuary nestled by a tranquil lake in Arambol, North Goa. We are a small, family-like retreat where the ancient sister sciences of Ayurveda and Yoga are practiced with deep authenticity and a personal touch. Our name, "Yan," means a path or a journey, and that is our promise to you: to guide you on a personalized journey back to your natural state of balance, health, and inner peace, all under the compassionate guidance of our founder, Dr. Neha.




### Accreditations & Recognition

1.   We are a registered **Yoga Alliance USA** school, a global standard of excellence that validates the high quality and authenticity of our yoga and teacher training programs.

2.   Our greatest recognition is the **trust and high praise from our global community** of guests, who consistently highlight our personalized care, authentic treatments, and the transformative power of our serene environment.

3.   Your healing is personally guided by our founder, **Dr. Neha**, an experienced and compassionate Ayurvedic physician who is dedicated to the pure, classical practice of this ancient science.




### Philosophy & Approach

1.   Our philosophy is rooted in **deep personalization**. We reject the one-size-fits-all model; your entire healing journey is crafted exclusively for you after a deep, traditional diagnosis by Dr. Neha.

2.   We believe your healing is a **'Yan'—a journey**, not just a treatment. Our focus is on empowering you with the knowledge and tools to continue your path to wellness long after you leave us.

3.   Our approach is built on the powerful **synergy of Ayurveda and Yoga**. We believe that by healing the body through Ayurveda and uniting the mind through Yoga, we can create a state of complete and lasting well-being.




**Systems of Medicine Practiced**

1.   **Classical Ayurveda & Panchakarma**
 This is the heart of your physical healing. Your journey is guided by our expert physicians, using authentic, classical therapies and the profound detoxification of *Panchakarma* to restore your body's natural balance.

2.   **Traditional Yoga (Hatha & Ashtanga)**
 This is the path to uniting your mind and body. We offer daily, in-depth yoga sessions for all levels, as well as renowned Yoga Teacher Training Courses (YTTC) for those who wish to deepen their practice.

3.   **Meditation & Mindfulness**
 This is the tool for cultivating inner peace. We integrate guided meditation and mindfulness practices into your daily routine to help you calm your mind, reduce stress, and connect with your inner self.
