**Treatment Process & Patient Journey**

Your personalized path to wellness at Yan Cure is a seamless and supportive experience, guided by our founder, Dr. Neha, every step of the way.

1.  **Initial Consultation with Dr. Neha**

    Your healing 'Yan' (journey) begins with a deep, personal consultation to understand your unique path to wellness.

    *   You will be personally guided by our founder and expert Ayurvedic physician, Dr. Neha.

    *   Through traditional diagnostics, we identify your unique body constitution and the root cause of any imbalance.

    *   We focus on understanding your health goals from both an Ayurvedic and a Yogic perspective.

2.  **Your Personal Healing Blueprint**

    Based on your consultation, we design your exclusive roadmap to health, crafted only for you.

    *   This is not a generic package; it is your personal prescription for deep and lasting health.

    *   Your blueprint details your bespoke schedule of therapies, yoga practices, and meditation sessions.

    *   It includes a tailored diet plan and herbal support to perfectly balance your *doshas*.

3.  **Daily Healing: Ayurveda & Yoga in Synergy**

    This is where your deep healing begins, with a daily synergy of Ayurvedic treatments and authentic Yoga practice.

    *   You will receive your prescribed Ayurvedic treatments each day from our highly skilled therapists.

    *   You will participate in daily, in-depth Yoga sessions designed to perfectly complement your healing.

    *   Your progress is closely monitored by Dr. Neha to ensure your journey is safe and effective.

4.  **Wholesome Sattvic Cuisine**

    We believe that food is powerful medicine, and our meals are a delicious and vital part of your healing journey.

    *   Enjoy delicious, wholesome vegetarian meals prepared according to classical Ayurvedic principles.

    *   Your meals are customized by our physicians to support your specific treatment plan.

    *   This nourishing cuisine helps to heal your body from the inside out, amplifying your results.

5.  **Mindful Living by the Lake**

    Your wellness journey extends beyond the treatment room into a world of mindful relaxation and connection with nature.

    *   Harmonize your mind and body with guided meditation sessions in our serene lakeside sanctuary.

    *   Reconnect with nature through peaceful walks to the nearby Arambol beach.

    *   Our tranquil, healing-focused environment is a powerful medicine in itself.

6.  **Empowerment for Your Journey Home**

    Our support for you doesn’t end when you leave; we empower you with the tools for a lifetime of wellness.

    *   Before you depart, you will have a comprehensive final consultation with Dr. Neha.

    *   We will provide you with a detailed plan, including dietary and lifestyle advice, to continue your progress.

    *   You leave not just rejuvenated, but empowered with the knowledge to continue your 'Yan' at home.
