### Facilities & Amenities

At Yan Cure, every facility is thoughtfully designed to support your healing journey in an environment of authenticity, intimacy, and serenity.

**Serene Lakeside Sanctuary**

Heal in our peaceful haven, nestled directly on the tranquil banks of a lake in Arambol.

*   Immerse yourself in a serene, natural environment that is a powerful medicine in itself.

*   Enjoy the deep peace and quiet of our lakeside location, perfect for healing and contemplation.

*   The tranquil energy of the lake and the surrounding nature will deepen your healing journey.

**Authentic Yoga Shala**

Practice in our dedicated shala, a sacred space for authentic Yoga and a registered Yoga Alliance school.

*   Our spacious, open-air shala is filled with natural light and the gentle breeze of the lake.

*   This is where you will deepen your practice with daily Hatha and Ashtanga yoga sessions.

*   A perfect, serene environment to unite your mind, body, and spirit.

**Ayurvedic Treatment Rooms**

Experience traditional therapies in spaces designed for your comfort, privacy, and authentic healing.

*   Heal in comfort in our dedicated, private therapy rooms for both men and women.

*   Our spaces are equipped for classical Ayurvedic procedures, ensuring maximum therapeutic benefit.

*   We maintain the highest standards of hygiene and safety for your complete peace of mind.

**Founder-Led Consultations**

A private and personal space for your deep, one-on-one consultation with our founder, Dr. Neha.

*   This is where your unique healing 'Yan' (journey) is crafted through a traditional diagnosis.

*   Benefit directly from the expertise and compassionate guidance of our founder.

*   A supportive environment to discuss your health goals and create your personalized healing path.

**Wholesome Sattvic Dining**

Savor delicious meals where food is treated as the first and most powerful medicine.

*   Enjoy simple, wholesome, and delicious vegetarian meals prepared according to classical Ayurvedic principles.

*   Your meals are customized by Dr. Neha to support your specific treatment plan and balance your *doshas*.

*   This nourishing cuisine helps to heal your body from the inside out, amplifying your results.

**Simple & Serene Accommodations**

Rest and recover in clean, comfortable rooms designed to be a sanctuary for pure healing.

*   Our accommodations are purposefully designed for rest and healing, free from modern distractions.

*   Enjoy clean, comfortable rooms with essential amenities and a peaceful ambiance.

*   Many of our rooms offer serene views of the lake, enhancing your sense of tranquility.

**Library & Learning Corner**

Nourish your mind and deepen your understanding of the ancient sciences of Yoga and Ayurveda.

*   Our curated collection includes classical texts on Yoga, Ayurveda, and philosophy.

*   An essential resource for our Yoga Teacher Training students and curious guests.

*   Find a quiet, comfortable space for peaceful reading, study, and contemplation.

**Arambol Beach Proximity**

Enjoy the best of both worlds—our tranquil lakeside peace and the vibrant energy of Arambol beach.

*   We are just a short and pleasant walk away from the famous Arambol beach.

*   Perfect for mindful morning walks, gentle swims, or watching the beautiful Goan sunset.

*   This connection to the ocean is a wonderful complement to your healing journey.

**A Nurturing & Supportive Community**

Feel completely supported in our warm, intimate, and family-like retreat environment.

*   Our entire team, led by Dr. Neha, is dedicated to providing compassionate and heartfelt care.

*   Our small size creates a safe and supportive community space where you feel at home.

*   We are happy to assist with all your needs, from transportation to local guidance.
