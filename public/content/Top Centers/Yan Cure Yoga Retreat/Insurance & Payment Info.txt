### Insurance & Payment Info

Making your path to authentic healing clear and accessible.

**Insurance Coverage**

*   While we are a traditional healing and yoga center, some insurance providers may offer coverage for wellness retreats or alternative therapies.

*   Our professional administrative team provides all necessary invoices and documentation to support your claims process.

*   We are committed to helping you navigate the reimbursement process as smoothly as possible.

*   We strongly advise you to confirm coverage details and eligibility directly with your insurance provider before your stay.

**Payment Options**

*   We accept all major payment methods, including credit/debit cards and domestic/international bank transfers.

*   Our pricing is completely transparent, with all costs clearly outlined in a personalized quotation after your consultation.

*   Flexible payment options and special pricing are available for our longer stays and Yoga Teacher Training Courses.

*   You will receive a detailed cost estimate before your treatment begins, with absolutely no hidden charges.

**For International Patients**

International guests are encouraged to check with their home insurance providers regarding coverage for wellness retreats and Ayurvedic treatments. Our administrative team is highly experienced in providing the necessary, detailed reports and documentation in the required format to support your international insurance claims.
