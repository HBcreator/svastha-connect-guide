**About Sitaram Beach Retreat**
Welcome to Sitaram Beach Retreat, a sanctuary of profound healing where a 100-year-old legacy of Ayurvedic excellence meets the tranquil shores of the Arabian Sea. Located in Thrissur, the cultural capital of Kerala, we are not just a retreat; we are a genuine Ayurvedic hospital born from the Sitaram Ayurveda Pharmacy, founded in 1921. Here, in our serene beachfront haven, we practice pure, classical Ayurveda with the wisdom of four generations, inviting you on a transformative journey to find your true health.



### Accreditations & Recognition
<bold>Our reputation is forged from a century of trust, clinical excellence, and an unwavering commitment to pure Ayurveda.</bold>
*   **A 100-Year-Old Legacy:** We are the direct descendant of Sitaram Ayurveda, an institution with a century-old history that includes serving as the Royal Physician to the Maharaja of Cochin.

*   **'Green Leaf' Certified:** We hold the 'Green Leaf' certification from the Government of Kerala, its highest rating, signifying our adherence to the most authentic and traditional Ayurvedic practices.
