### Facilities & Amenities

**NABH-Accredited & 'Green Leaf' Certified Ayurveda Hospital**
*   Heal with confidence in our professional, multi-certified treatment centre, adhering to India's highest standards for clinical excellence and authentic Ayurveda.
*   Our facility features 14 well-equipped, clean, and hygienic therapy rooms designed for all classical Ayurvedic and Panchakarma treatments.
*   Experience a genuine hospital environment where every facility is focused solely on your prescribed medical healing.

**Direct Nattika Beach Access**
*   Step directly from our serene, green campus onto the pristine and peaceful sands of Nattika Beach.
*   The tranquil Arabian Sea is a core part of our healing environment, perfect for mindful walks and quiet contemplation.
*   Enjoy the therapeutic benefits of the fresh sea air and the calming rhythm of the waves, free from crowds.

**Private & Serene Cottage Accommodations**
*   Rest and recover in one of our 24 private, comfortable cottages, nestled within a lush, green landscape.
*   Each cottage is a peaceful sanctuary, designed with traditional Keralan aesthetics to ensure deep rest and privacy.
*   Our accommodations are consciously free from distractions like televisions to support your inward healing journey.

**Connection to Sitaram's 100-Year-Old Pharmacy**
*   Benefit from the unique amenity of receiving medicines from our own century-old, GMP-certified Sitaram Ayurveda Pharmacy.
*   This direct link guarantees the utmost purity, potency, and authenticity of every herbal formulation used in your treatment.
*   This is a cornerstone of our clinical efficacy, ensuring you receive medicines backed by a legacy of trust.

**Prescriptive Ayurvedic Dining Hall**
*   Nourish your body in our dedicated dining hall, a calm and mindful space for your therapeutic meals.
*   There is no buffet or menu; you are served your doctor-prescribed Sattvic vegetarian diet as part of your treatment.
*   This facility is central to our philosophy that food is a potent and personalized form of medicine.

**Beach-Facing Yoga & Meditation Hall**
*   Harmonize your mind and body in our beautiful, open-air yoga hall with serene views of the surrounding nature and the nearby beach.
*   Join daily yoga and meditation sessions with practices specifically prescribed by your doctor to support your healing.
*   This dedicated space for spiritual practice is essential for our integrated, holistic approach to wellness.

**On-Site Physician Consultation Rooms**
*   Begin your journey and monitor your progress in our private and professional on-site consultation rooms.
*   Benefit from the confidentiality and comfort of a dedicated clinical space for your daily reviews with our senior Vaidyas.
*   This facility underscores our commitment to a medically supervised, safe, and effective healing experience.

**Lush Gardens & Healing Environment**
*   Heal in a serene, oxygen-rich environment with beautifully maintained gardens and peaceful walkways.
*   Our campus is a green oasis designed to calm the senses and provide a powerful natural backdrop for your recovery.
*   Discover numerous quiet nooks for silent contemplation, reading, and reconnecting with nature.

**Library & Essential Guest Services**
*   Nourish your mind in our well-stocked library, featuring a curated collection of books on Ayurveda, yoga, and Indian philosophy.
*   Our friendly and attentive team is always available to assist with any needs, ensuring a comfortable and supportive stay.
*   Browse our small on-site store for essentials and authentic Ayurvedic products from our pharmacy to take home.
