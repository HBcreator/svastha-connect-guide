### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness programs, a minimum of 7 nights is recommended. For our more intensive medical or detoxification programs like Panchakarma, a stay of 14 nights or more is typically required for deep, effective, and lasting healing.

**2. What does your "100-year pharmaceutical legacy" mean for my treatment?**

It means that the herbal medicines and oils used in your treatment come from the legendary Sitaram Ayurveda Pharmacy, the world's first GMP-certified Ayurveda pharmacy, established in 1921. You are benefiting from a century of trusted expertise and unparalleled purity in every therapy you receive.

**3. Will I be treated by Dr. Vignesh Devraj directly?**

As the Founder and fourth-generation Chief Physician, Dr. Vignesh Devraj personally designs and oversees the care of every guest. He and his expert team of specialized doctors will be deeply involved in your day-to-day care, ensuring you receive the highest level of personal and expert attention.

**4. What is the accommodation like?**

You will be staying in your own private, luxuriously appointed villa or cottage, many of which feature stunning, direct views of the Arabian Sea. Our accommodations are designed to provide the highest level of five-star comfort, privacy, and tranquility.

**5. How do I get to the retreat?**

Our beach retreat is located near Thrissur, Kerala. The nearest airport is Cochin International Airport (COK), which is approximately a 1.5-hour scenic drive away. We are happy to coordinate your private luxury airport transfers for a seamless journey.

**6. What kind of food is served?**

You will be served a delicious, gourmet *sattvic* cuisine at our beautiful sea-facing restaurant. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are strictly prescribed by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha* and health goals, using fresh, organic ingredients.

**7. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions and recent medical records. Our physicians will review them during your comprehensive initial consultation to create a safe and effective treatment plan for you.

**8. Is this a suitable place for a casual holiday?**

While our setting is as beautiful as any luxury beach resort, our heart is a serious, **NABH-accredited Ayurvedic hospital**. We are a sanctuary for those who are serious about their health and are seeking a deep, transformative healing experience.

**9. Do I need to be experienced in yoga?**

Not at all. Our daily yoga sessions, held in our beautiful beachfront shala, are designed to be therapeutic and are suitable for all levels. Our experienced masters provide personalized guidance to ensure the practice is safe, enjoyable, and perfectly supportive of your healing journey.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing suitable for a warm, tropical climate. Please also bring swimwear, any personal toiletries, and your medical records. We will provide all other necessary amenities for a luxurious and comfortable stay.
