### Facilities & Amenities

At SWAN, every facility is thoughtfully designed to support your healing journey in an environment of authenticity, simplicity, and natural harmony.

**Eco-Friendly Accommodations**

Rest and recover in our unique, eco-conscious huts, built with natural materials for a truly grounding experience.

*   Stay in simple, clean, and comfortable huts designed to be in harmony with nature.

*   Our accommodations are purposefully designed for rest and healing, free from modern distractions.

*   Enjoy the natural ambiance and peaceful surroundings from your private space.

**Authentic Yoga Shala**

Practice in our dedicated shala, a sacred space for authentic Yoga and a registered Yoga Alliance school.

*   Our spacious, open-air shala is filled with natural light and the serene sounds of nature.

*   This is where you will deepen your practice with daily Hatha and Vinyasa yoga sessions.

*   A perfect, serene environment to unite your mind, body, and spirit.

**Ayurvedic Treatment Huts**

Experience traditional therapies in private spaces designed for your comfort and authentic healing.

*   Heal in comfort in our dedicated, private treatment huts for both men and women.

*   Our spaces are equipped for classical Ayurvedic procedures, ensuring maximum therapeutic benefit.

*   We maintain the highest standards of hygiene and safety for your complete peace of mind.

**Physician Consultation Room**

A private and personal space for your deep, one-on-one consultation with our expert physician.

*   This is where your unique healing journey is crafted through a traditional, authentic diagnosis.

*   Benefit directly from the expertise and compassionate guidance of our Ayurvedic doctors.

*   A supportive environment to discuss your health goals and create your personalized healing path.

**Sattvic Community Dining**

Savor delicious meals where food is treated as the first and most powerful medicine.

*   Enjoy simple, wholesome, and delicious vegetarian meals prepared according to classical Ayurvedic principles.

*   Your meals are customized by our physicians to support your specific treatment plan and balance your *doshas*.

*   Dine in our communal space, fostering a sense of community and shared wellness.

**A Serene & Green Sanctuary**

Heal in a naturally therapeutic environment, surrounded by the lush, tranquil greenery of Assagao.

*   Our entire retreat is a green sanctuary, designed to be eco-conscious and close to nature.

*   The serene, pollution-free atmosphere is a powerful medicine in itself, calming your nervous system.

*   This powerful connection with nature is an integral and essential part of your healing journey.

**Library & Learning Corner**

Nourish your mind and deepen your understanding of the ancient sciences of Yoga and Ayurveda.

*   Our curated collection includes classical texts on Yoga, Ayurveda, and philosophy.

*   An essential resource for our Yoga Teacher Training students and curious guests.

*   Find a quiet, comfortable space for peaceful reading, study, and contemplation.

**Proximity to Goan Nature**

Enjoy the best of both worlds—our tranquil, secluded retreat and the vibrant nature of North Goa.

*   We are nestled in the peaceful greenery of Assagao, a perfect escape from the crowds.

*   Our location provides easy access to the famous beaches of Anjuna and Vagator.

*   This allows you to complement your healing journey with mindful walks in nature.

**A Warm & Supportive Community**

Feel completely supported in our warm, intimate, and family-like retreat environment.

*   Our entire team is dedicated to providing compassionate and heartfelt care.

*   Our small size creates a safe and supportive community space where you feel at home.

*   We are happy to assist with all your needs, from transportation to local guidance.
