### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness retreats, we recommend a minimum stay of 7 days to experience tangible benefits. For our medical programs or for a deeper transformation, a stay of 14 days or more is typically required.

**2. What is the accommodation like? Is it a luxury resort?**

Our focus is on an authentic, grounding, and eco-conscious experience. You will stay in clean, comfortable, and simple eco-huts built with natural materials. We are a genuine healing sanctuary, not a luxury hotel, which allows us to keep our focus entirely on your therapeutic outcome.

**3. I am a beginner in Yoga. Is this retreat suitable for me?**

Absolutely. Our daily yoga sessions are designed for all levels, from complete beginners to advanced practitioners. Our experienced masters provide personal attention and modifications to ensure the practice is safe, enjoyable, and perfectly suited to your body and goals.

**4. Who will be my teachers and doctors?**

Your journey is personally guided by our founders, Gyanmitra and Shivendra, and our dedicated team of Ayurvedic doctors and yoga masters. Our intimate, family-like setting ensures you receive direct, personal, and expert guidance throughout your stay.

**5. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a simple, wholesome, and delicious vegetarian diet based on classical Ayurvedic and sattvic principles. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are customized by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha* and health condition.

**6. What is the Yoga Alliance certification?**

The Yoga Alliance USA certification is a global standard of excellence. It is your assurance that our yoga instruction and our Yoga Teacher Training Courses (YTTC) are of the highest quality, authenticity, and are internationally recognized.

**7. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, it is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our physicians will review them during your initial consultation and advise you on how to manage them safely and effectively during your stay.

**8. How do I get to the retreat?**

We are located in the peaceful greenery of Assagao, North Goa. The nearest airport is Manohar International Airport, Mopa (GOX). We are happy to help you coordinate your airport transfers and other transportation for a seamless journey.

**9. Can a companion who is not taking treatment stay with me?**

Yes, companions are welcome to stay with you and enjoy the serene environment and our healthy sattvic cuisine. Please note that treatments and specialized classes are only for guests who have undergone a consultation and have a prescribed wellness plan.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing suitable for both yoga practice and Goa's warm climate. Please also bring any personal toiletries, your medical records, and a good book. We will provide towels and any necessary attire for your treatments.
