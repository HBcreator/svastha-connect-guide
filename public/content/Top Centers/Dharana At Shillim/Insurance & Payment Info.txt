### Insurance & Payment Info

We believe that your journey to conscious living should be seamless and serene from the very beginning.

**Our Secure Payment & Booking Process**

Our process is designed to be straightforward, ensuring your place in our UNESCO World Heritage sanctuary is reserved effortlessly.

*   To confirm your bespoke wellness journey, an advance deposit is required at the time of booking.
*   The final balance for your program is typically due upon your arrival at the retreat.
*   We accept payments through secure international bank transfers and all major credit cards for your convenience.

**Support for Your Insurance Claims**

We are committed to supporting you in receiving any eligible benefits from your health insurance provider for our medically-supervised programs.

*   While we do not offer direct billing, our medically-supervised programs may be eligible for reimbursement from many international providers.
*   Our dedicated wellness team will provide you with all necessary detailed invoices and medical summaries to support your claim.
*   We strongly advise you to contact your insurance provider beforehand to confirm the extent of your coverage for wellness programs.

**A Note for Our International Guests**

We have extensive experience in welcoming and assisting a discerning global clientele.

*   We encourage you to check with your home country's insurance provider regarding their policies on reimbursing preventative wellness programs.
*   Our team is proficient in preparing the necessary documentation in a clear and professional format to facilitate your claims.
*   We are here to support you in making the entire administrative process as smooth and simple as possible.
