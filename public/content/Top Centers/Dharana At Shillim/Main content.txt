**About Dharana At Shillim**
Welcome to Dharana at Shillim, an award-winning wellness sanctuary nestled within the **320-acre private Hilton Shillim Estate Retreat & Spa**, a realm of serene luxury in the heart of a UNESCO World Heritage site. Inspired by the profound yogic principle of *Dharana* (focused awareness), we guide our guests on a transformative journey of conscious living. Here, amidst the pristine beauty of the Western Ghats, ancient healing wisdom and contemporary luxury converge to create an unparalleled path to sustainable well-being.

### Philosophy & Approach

1.  Our philosophy is rooted in the **'Dharana Way of Life'**, a journey of focused awareness that empowers you to achieve sustainable well-being long after you leave our sanctuary.

2.  We believe in a **multi-disciplinary approach guided by our Five Pathways**: Preventative Medicine, Spiritual Wellbeing, Nutrition & Dietetics, Fitness, and Conflict Resolution.

3.  Our primary focus is on **preventative medicine and holistic healing**, addressing the root causes of imbalance to prevent future ailments and enhance your lifelong vitality.

4.  We believe the **pristine Shillim Valley is our greatest healer**, integrating the therapeutic power of the forest, mountains, and pure air into every aspect of your wellness journey.
