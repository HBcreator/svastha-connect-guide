### Facilities & Amenities

Our 320-acre sanctuary is a world-class wellness destination where every facility is designed to the highest standards of luxury, seamlessly integrated into the pristine beauty of a UNESCO World Heritage site.

**Luxurious Private Villas**
Your accommodation is a private and serene sanctuary, designed to offer unparalleled comfort and a deep connection with the surrounding nature.

*   Stay in one of our 99 beautifully appointed villas, each nestled discreetly within the natural landscape.
*   Every villa features a private balcony or deck with stunning views of the forest or mountains.
*   Experience the perfect blend of minimalist elegance, modern luxury, and natural harmony.

**The Expansive Dharana Wellness Centre**
Our 70-acre dedicated wellness zone is the heart of your journey, housing an extensive range of world-class therapeutic and relaxation spaces.

*   We offer 17 treatment rooms with private balconies for our diverse range of therapies.
*   This expansive facility includes dedicated suites for Ayurveda, hydrotherapy, and meditation.
*   The centre is a testament to our commitment to providing a comprehensive, world-class wellness experience.

**Dedicated Yoga & Meditation Pavilions**
We provide serene, purpose-built spaces designed to deepen your spiritual practice while immersing you in the sacred energy of the Shillim Valley.

*   Practice in our stunning open-air Yoga pavilion with panoramic views of the Western Ghats.
*   Find stillness in our dedicated Meditation Cave, a unique space for deep introspection.
*   These sacred spaces are designed to enhance focus, tranquility, and your connection with nature.

**Personalized Wellness Dining Venues**
Our seven distinct dining venues offer a journey of the senses, where our Shillim Food Philosophy comes to life in beautiful and unique settings.

*   Enjoy personalized wellness meals at our dedicated wellness restaurant, **The Green Table**.
*   Experience our unique tea house, **The Tea House**, offering a curated selection of herbal infusions.
*   Our "farm-to-table" approach ensures every meal is fresh, therapeutic, and delicious.

**Advanced Hydrotherapy Suites**
Experience the profound healing and detoxifying power of water in our advanced, state-of-the-art hydrotherapy facilities.

*   Our suites include a luxurious sauna, steam room, and invigorating hydro-jet pools.
*   We offer specialized aquatic therapies like Watsu in our dedicated temperature-controlled Watsu pool.
*   These facilities are an integral part of our detoxification and pain management protocols.

**The Shillim Institute & Cooking School**
We believe in empowering you through knowledge. Our dedicated institutes provide spaces for learning, creativity, and personal growth.

*   Deepen your understanding of wellness with lectures and workshops at the Shillim Institute.
*   Learn the art of healthy, gourmet cooking at our state-of-the-art Cooking School.
*   These facilities transform your stay from a retreat into a rich, educational experience.

**State-of-the-Art Fitness Centre & Pools**
Our fitness facilities are designed to build a strong and resilient body, offering a range of options for all activity levels.

*   Our expansive gymnasium is equipped with the latest cardio and strength training machines.
*   We offer multiple stunning swimming pools, including a dedicated wellness hydrotherapy pool.
*   Our fitness offerings are complemented by our beautiful outdoor yoga and aqua aerobics sessions.

**The Therapeutic Landscape**
The entire 320-acre estate is your ultimate facility for healing, offering endless opportunities to connect with the restorative power of our UNESCO World Heritage setting.

*   Explore over 25 kilometers of dedicated walking, trekking, and cycling trails within the property.
*   Discover serene meditation spots, cascading waterfalls, and breathtaking viewpoints.
*   This vast natural canvas is perfect for our signature 'Forest Bathing' and mindful nature walks.

**Creative & Contemplative Spaces**
Our retreat features unique spaces designed to nurture your creativity and facilitate quiet contemplation, aligning with our holistic philosophy.

*   Find your creative flow in our dedicated Pottery Studio, guided by our resident artist.
*   Relax and unwind with a book from our curated collection in the peaceful Library Lounge.
*   These tranquil spaces provide the perfect balance to your active therapies and fitness sessions.
