### Founder & Team Info

Your journey to profound well-being, guided by a team of authentic healing experts.

**Gavin de Souza is the founder**

Founders & Custodians of a Healing Valley

The vision for Dharana is a story that spans two generations. It began in 1985 with brothers **William and Denzil de Souza**, who embarked on a mission to conserve the pristine 320-acre Shillim Valley. This legacy of conservation was then beautifully transformed by William's son, **Gavin de Souza**, who in 2013, created the world-class wellness retreat you see today—a sanctuary where healing and nature are in perfect harmony.
