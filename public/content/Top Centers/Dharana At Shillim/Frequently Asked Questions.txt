### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. How do I reach Dharana at Shillim?**

Our sanctuary is nestled in a pristine, secluded valley within the Western Ghats, yet remains conveniently accessible from two major international airports.

*   From **Mumbai International Airport (BOM):** This is a scenic 3 to 3.5-hour drive through the beautiful countryside.
*   From **Pune International Airport (PNQ):** This is a shorter and comfortable 2 to 2.5-hour drive.
*   We are delighted to arrange a private luxury transfer for you from either airport upon request to ensure a seamless arrival.

**2. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

The duration of your stay is key to achieving deep, sustainable results, and is always recommended based on your personal wellness goals.

*   For our deep, transformative wellness programs, a stay of **7, 14, or 21 nights** is essential for profound results.
*   Shorter wellness immersions of **3 or 5 nights** are also available for a powerful introduction to the Dharana Way of Life.
*   Our doctors will provide a final recommendation on the ideal duration after your personal consultation.

**3. What is included in my all-inclusive program?**

Our programs are designed to be comprehensive and all-inclusive, allowing you to immerse yourself fully in your healing journey without any distractions.

*   Your program includes your luxurious private villa accommodation and all initial, ongoing, and final doctor consultations.
*   It covers all your prescribed therapies, treatments, and daily wellness activities like yoga, meditation, and hikes.
*   All your personalized wellness meals and beverages, crafted according to the Shillim Food Philosophy, are also included.

**4. What is the best time of year to visit the Shillim Valley?**

The beautiful Western Ghats offer a pleasant and healing climate year-round, with each season offering a unique and magical experience.

*   The winter, from **October to February**, is crisp, pleasant, and sunny, perfect for outdoor activities and trekking.
*   The monsoon, from **June to September**, transforms the valley into a lush, mystical green paradise, and is traditionally potent for Ayurveda.
*   The summer, from **March to May**, is warmer but still pleasant due to the high altitude, ideal for enjoying our stunning pools and facilities.

**5. What kind of clothing should I bring?**

We recommend packing comfortable, high-quality clothing suitable for a range of wellness activities within a luxury resort setting.

*   Bring activewear for your fitness, yoga, and trekking sessions through our stunning 320-acre estate.
*   Pack smart-casual resort wear for your relaxing evenings and gourmet wellness dining experiences.
*   Remember to include comfortable walking shoes, swimwear, and a light shawl or jacket for the cool mountain evenings.

**6. Is Dharana suitable for someone new to wellness?**

Absolutely. Our philosophy is to meet you wherever you are on your journey and guide you forward with personal care and expertise.

*   All our programs are completely personalized, so a beginner's journey will be crafted differently from that of an experienced practitioner.
*   The environment is supportive, luxurious, and non-judgmental, making it the perfect place to begin your wellness journey.
*   Our core goal is to introduce you to the 'Dharana Way of Life' in a way that is accessible, meaningful, and sustainable for you.
