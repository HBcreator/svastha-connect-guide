### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Do I need to be a hotel guest to book a treatment at the spa?**

While our residential wellness programs are for in-house guests, we do welcome non-resident guests for our individual spa therapies and day packages. We highly recommend booking in advance to ensure availability.

**2. What makes your Ayurvedic treatments "authentic"?**

Our Ayurvedic treatments are designed and supervised by a qualified resident Ayurvedic physician. We have a dedicated, private **Kerala Suite** with authentic equipment, and our therapies are performed by highly skilled therapists, many of whom are trained in Kerala, the home of Ayurveda.

**3. Do you have a resident Ayurvedic doctor?**

Yes. We have a qualified Ayurvedic physician on-site. Your journey for any of our Ayurvedic programs will begin with a thorough, personalized consultation with our doctor to ensure your treatment is perfectly tailored to your unique needs and *dosha*.

**4. What kind of food is served during a wellness program?**

You will be served a delicious and healthy wellness cuisine, crafted by our expert chefs in consultation with our doctors. We can cater to specific dietary needs, including sattvic, dosha-specific, and other healthy culinary preferences, all while maintaining our five-star gourmet standards.

**5. How do I get to the hotel?**

The Imperial is a landmark hotel, conveniently located in the heart of New Delhi. We are approximately a 30 to 45-minute drive from the Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL). Our concierge is happy to arrange your private luxury airport transfers.

**6. Do you offer yoga sessions?**

Yes. We have a serene, dedicated **Yoga Sanctum** and a resident Yoga Master. We focus on providing one-on-one, personalized yoga instruction that is tailored to your specific body type, fitness level, and wellness goals.

**7. Can a companion stay with me if they are not on a wellness program?**

Absolutely. Your companion is welcome to stay with you in our luxurious accommodations and enjoy all the world-class facilities of The Imperial hotel, including our award-winning restaurants and swimming pool.

**8. What should I pack for my spa experience?**

We recommend bringing comfortable clothing. For your time at the spa, we will provide you with a robe, slippers, and any necessary attire for your treatments. Swimwear is recommended for our hydrotherapy areas and swimming pool.

**9. Can I book a treatment for my specific health concern, like jet lag or stress?**

Yes. Our wellness menu is extensive, and our experts are skilled at customizing treatments for specific concerns. We highly recommend our "Abhyanga" massage for jet lag and our "Shirodhara" therapy for deep stress relief. Our Ayurvedic doctor can provide a detailed recommendation.

**10. What makes The Imperial Spa unique compared to other luxury spas?**

Our uniqueness lies in the perfect fusion of a legendary, art-filled heritage hotel with a truly authentic and deep wellness philosophy. We offer a level of clinical Ayurvedic expertise and personalization that is rare to find in a five-star hotel spa, creating an experience that is both profoundly healing and incredibly luxurious.
