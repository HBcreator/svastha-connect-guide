### Facilities & Amenities

At The Imperial, every facility is thoughtfully designed to create a luxurious and serene environment where healing happens in an atmosphere of regal elegance.

**The Imperial Spa**

A serene, multi-level sanctuary inspired by the mystical philosophy of Sufism.

*   Immerse yourself in our sprawling wellness haven, dedicated to your complete mind-body renewal.

*   Our unique Sufi-inspired philosophy guides every aspect of your deeply restorative journey.

*   This is a tranquil oasis where ancient wisdom meets modern luxury in the heart of Delhi.

**Luxurious Heritage Accommodations**

Rest and recover in elegant rooms and suites that blend colonial charm with five-star luxury.

*   Stay in beautifully appointed rooms with classic, high-ceilinged colonial architecture.

*   Enjoy world-class amenities for a truly comfortable and seamless wellness stay.

*   Each room is a peaceful sanctuary, offering serene views of the hotel's lush, historic gardens.

**The Authentic Kerala Suite**

Experience the deep healing of traditional Ayurveda in our dedicated, private suite.

*   A specialized space designed for classical Ayurvedic therapies like *Panchakarma* and *Shirodhara*.

*   Features authentic equipment, including the traditional wooden 'Droni' massage table.

*   Allows for a truly immersive and authentic Keralan healing experience in a private, serene setting.

**Hydrotherapy & Thermal Suites**

Purify your body and soothe your muscles in our world-class hydrotherapy facilities.

*   Benefit from private suites equipped with steam, sauna, and invigorating showers.

*   The perfect way to prepare your body for therapies or to deepen your detoxification.

*   A truly restorative experience that melts away tension and soothes tired muscles.

**The Path of Yoga & Meditation**

Harmonize your mind and body with personalized guidance from our resident Yoga Master.

*   Receive one-on-one, personalized instruction tailored to your specific needs and wellness goals.

*   Our sessions are designed to complement your wellness journey and cultivate deep inner peace.

*   Practice in a serene and dedicated space, away from the distractions of the city.

**The Verandah Swimming Pool**

Refresh your body and mind in our stunning pool, a tranquil oasis surrounded by royal palms.

*   Enjoy a gentle swim in our beautiful, temperature-controlled outdoor pool.

*   Relax and unwind on comfortable poolside loungers in a serene, colonial-era setting.

*   This low-impact activity is a wonderful way to enhance your circulation and calm your mind.

**The MAPI Salon**

Complete your wellness journey with our world-class beauty and grooming services.

*   An exclusive salon offering a range of luxury hair, skin, and nail care services.

*   Perfect for both men and women seeking professional grooming and pampering.

*   The perfect finishing touch to leave you looking as radiant as you feel inside.

**The Technogym Fitness Centre**

Maintain your fitness goals in our state-of-the-art gymnasium with expert guidance.

*   Our fitness center is fully equipped with world-class Technogym equipment.

*   Benefit from personalized training sessions with our expert fitness instructors.

*   A perfect complement to your wellness program to build strength and stamina.

**Gourmet Wellness Cuisine**

Savor delicious meals where healthy cuisine is elevated to a gourmet art form.

*   Our chefs and wellness experts collaborate to create a menu that is both nourishing and delicious.

*   Enjoy a range of healthy, balanced options, including sattvic and dosha-specific meals upon request.

*   Dine in one of our award-winning restaurants, where every meal is a five-star experience.
