### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness programs, a minimum of 7 days is recommended. For our medical treatment programs, a stay of 14 days or more is typically required for deep and effective healing, as advised by our physicians based on your condition.

**2. What does your "120-year legacy" mean for my treatment?**

It means your treatment is not based on theory, but on a living legacy of healing wisdom passed down through five generations since 1901. Our physicians have an intuitive understanding of Ayurveda that comes from over a century of clinical experience, ensuring your care is both authentic and exceptionally effective.

**3. What is the accommodation like? Is it a luxury resort?**

We are a modern, NABH-accredited wellness retreat focused on providing a comfortable, serene, and healing-focused environment. Our rooms are luxurious and well-appointed with all modern amenities to ensure your stay is restful and of the highest quality.

**4. How close are you to the airport?**

Our retreat is incredibly convenient for travelers. We are located on the serene outskirts of Bangalore, just a 15-minute drive from the Kempegowda International Airport (BLR), making your arrival and departure seamless and stress-free.

**5. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a delicious and wholesome vegetarian diet based on classical Ayurvedic and sattvic principles. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are customized by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha* and health condition.

**6. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our physicians will review them during your initial consultation and advise you on how to manage them safely and effectively during your stay.

**7. Will I be treated by a member of the founding family?**

Yes. Our retreat is personally led by Dr. Mruthyunjaya Swamy, the fifth-generation torchbearer of our 120-year healing legacy. He and his team of expert physicians, including other senior family members, will be directly involved in your care.

**8. Are the treatments really personalized?**

Yes, completely. There are no one-size-fits-all packages here. Following your comprehensive initial consultation with our fifth-generation physicians, your entire treatment plan—from therapies to diet—is crafted exclusively for you.

**9. Can a companion who is not taking treatment stay with me?**

Yes, companions are welcome to stay with you and enjoy the serene environment and our healthy Ayurvedic cuisine. Please note that treatments are only provided to those who have undergone a consultation and have a prescribed wellness plan.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable cotton clothing suitable for both yoga practice and Bangalore's pleasant climate. Please also bring any personal toiletries, your medical records, and a good book. We will provide towels and any necessary attire for your treatments.
