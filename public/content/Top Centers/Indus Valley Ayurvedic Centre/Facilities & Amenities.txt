### Facilities & Amenities

Our entire 14-acre campus is a thoughtfully designed healing sanctuary, where every facility is an extension of our therapeutic philosophy.

**A Serene Ashram Campus & Location**

We believe that a peaceful environment is the foundation for healing. Our centre is a tranquil ashram nestled at the foothills of the majestic Chamundi Hills in Mysore.

*   You will heal in a vast, green, and completely pollution-free atmosphere, far from the stress of urban life.
*   The entire campus is designed according to the ancient principles of *Vastu Shastra* to maximize positive energy.
*   This is not a resort; it is a genuine sanctuary purpose-built for deep rest, introspection, and healing.

**Certified Organic Farm & Herbal Gardens**

We believe that pure food and medicine are essential for true healing. We are one of the few Ayurvedic centers with our own on-site, certified organic farm.

*   Your personalized Ayurvedic meals are prepared using fresh vegetables and grains harvested daily from our own farm.
*   Many of the potent herbs used in your treatments are grown in our medicinal gardens, ensuring maximum purity and efficacy.
*   This unique "farm-to-table" approach means your healing is supported by the most vibrant and life-giving nourishment.

**An Eco-Friendly & Sustainable Sanctuary**

We believe in living in complete harmony with nature. Our commitment to sustainability is woven into the very fabric of our center, creating a pure and high-vibrational healing space.

*   Our campus is primarily powered by clean, renewable solar energy, minimizing our carbon footprint.
*   We practice extensive rainwater harvesting and water recycling, conserving every drop of this precious resource.
*   This eco-conscious infrastructure creates a truly pure environment that is conducive to deep healing.

**Vastu-Compliant Accommodation**

Your living space is your private haven for rest and integration. Our accommodation is thoughtfully designed to be both comfortable and energetically supportive of your well-being.

*   We offer a range of spacious, clean, and well-ventilated rooms, including heritage-style cottages.
*   Each room is designed according to *Vastu* principles to promote positive energy, peace, and restorative sleep.
*   Your room provides a serene and comfortable sanctuary to relax and process the deep healing work you are undertaking.

**Authentic & Hygienic Treatment Centre**

Our treatment center is a sanctum of healing where classical Ayurvedic therapies are administered with the highest standards of authenticity and hygiene.

*   We have separate, private, and well-equipped therapy rooms for our male and female guests.
*   Our center upholds the stringent cleanliness and procedural standards of an award-winning Ayurvedic hospital.
*   We use only high-quality, authentic herbal oils and preparations for all our treatments.

**Sattvic Ayurvedic Dining Hall**

Our dining hall is where the principle of "food as medicine" comes to life. It is a calm and welcoming space where your meals become a delicious part of your therapy.

*   Enjoy pure vegetarian, *sattvic* cuisine, personally prescribed by our doctors to align with your *dosha*.
*   Our skilled chefs transform the fresh produce from our organic farm into delicious and healing meals.
*   The dining experience is designed to be mindful, peaceful, and supportive of your digestive health.

**Dedicated Yoga & Meditation Halls**

We provide serene, dedicated spaces for the practice of our therapeutic mind-body disciplines, allowing you to go deep within.

*   Our spacious and airy yoga hall is perfect for daily asana practice, regardless of your experience level.
*   We have a separate, tranquil meditation hall designed to facilitate deep states of peace and introspection.
*   These sacred spaces are free from external distractions, allowing you to fully immerse yourself in your practice.

**Library & Knowledge Centre**

We believe in empowering you with knowledge. Our library provides a quiet space to deepen your understanding of the ancient sciences you are experiencing.

*   You can browse our curated collection of books on Ayurveda, Yoga, Indian philosophy, and spiritual wellness.
*   This peaceful lounge offers a perfect sanctuary for reading, study, and quiet contemplation.
*   It provides a wonderful opportunity to enrich your mind and support your healing journey on an intellectual level.

**Natural Walking & Jogging Paths**

We encourage you to connect with the healing power of nature within our expansive and beautiful campus.

*   Our 14-acre property features well-maintained, serene pathways perfect for mindful walks or gentle jogs.
*   You can stroll through our lush gardens, past our organic farms, and breathe in the fresh, clean air.
*   This gentle movement in a natural setting is a simple yet powerful therapy that calms the mind and energizes the body.
