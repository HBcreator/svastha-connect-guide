### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the best time of year to visit Mysore for treatment?**

Mysore's pleasant, stable climate makes it an ideal destination for Ayurvedic healing throughout the year.

*   The most popular season is from **September to March**, when the weather is dry, sunny, and pleasantly cool.
*   The traditional monsoon season, from **June to August**, is also considered a very powerful time for Ayurveda, as the body is highly receptive to the healing therapies.
*   Unlike coastal regions, Mysore does not experience extreme humidity, making it a comfortable environment for healing year-round.

**2. What is the minimum stay for an effective Panchakarma?**

For a deep and authentic detoxification process, the duration of your stay is crucial for achieving lasting results.

*   For a comprehensive and effective *Panchakarma* program, a minimum duration of **21 to 28 days** is essential.
*   This allows for the proper execution of all three critical stages: preparation, cleansing, and post-treatment rejuvenation.
*   Our senior doctors will give you the final recommendation for the ideal duration based on your specific health needs after your consultation.

**3. How do I reach the Indus Valley Ayurvedic Centre?**

Our healing ashram is located in a serene area of Mysore, easily accessible from major transport hubs.

*   The nearest international airport is **Kempegowda International Airport in Bengaluru (BLR)**, which is a comfortable 3 to 4-hour drive away.
*   The nearest domestic airport and major railway station is in **Mysore (MYS)** itself, just a short 20-minute drive from our centre.
*   We are happy to arrange a reliable and comfortable taxi transfer for you from either Bengaluru or Mysore upon your request.

**4. What does a typical all-inclusive package include?**

Our packages are designed to be comprehensive, allowing you to immerse yourself fully in your healing journey without any worries.

*   Your package includes your private accommodation, an in-depth initial consultation, and daily follow-up consultations with our senior doctor.
*   It covers all your prescribed Ayurvedic therapies, internal herbal medicines, and daily therapeutic yoga and meditation sessions.
*   Three delicious, organic, and customized Ayurvedic meals per day (breakfast, lunch, and dinner) from our own farm are also included.

**5. Is IVAC a resort or a hospital?**

We offer a unique blend of both, which is our greatest strength: we are a healing ashram with the expertise of a hospital.

*   We have the seriousness, expertise, and capability to treat complex chronic illnesses like a specialized Ayurvedic hospital.
*   However, we provide this care in the peaceful, green, and spiritually nourishing environment of a traditional ashram, not a sterile clinic.
*   This approach, envisioned by our founder Dr. Krishna, ensures that you receive the best of both worlds: profound healing in profound peace.

The absolute purity of what you consume is a cornerstone of our healing philosophy, and we take extraordinary steps to ensure it.
