### Founder & Team Info

The soul of the Indus Valley Ayurvedic Centre is its unique and visionary leadership.
**Dr. Talavane Krishna - The Visionary Founder**

Dr. Talavane Krishna is the founder, owner, and chairman who established our healing sanctuary in 1999. His vision is uniquely rooted in his profound experience as a medical doctor, having trained and practiced as an anesthesiologist in both the UK and North America.

*   He founded IVAC with a clear mission: to practice and promote classical Ayurveda with the safety, standards, and professionalism of modern healthcare.
*   His unique background as a Western-trained medical doctor provides our center with an exceptional level of clinical excellence and a scientific approach.
*   As the guiding force, Dr. Krishna ensures that our center remains true to its founding principles of authenticity, evidence-based practice, and compassionate patient care.

**Our Expert Medical Leadership**

Your healing journey is architected and supervised by a team of highly experienced and dedicated Ayurvedic doctors, ensuring the highest standards of medical care.

*   Your care is overseen by our Head of the Medical Team, **Dr. Deepthi Niranjan**, who ensures meticulous planning and daily monitoring of your progress.
*   Our medical team is fortified by the deep classical wisdom of senior physicians like **Dr. U.K. Krishna**, the Director of our Nippon Institute of Ayurveda.
*   This expert leadership guarantees that every diagnosis is precise, every treatment plan is deeply personalized, and your health is always in the most capable hands.

**Our Dedicated Management Team**

A seamless healing experience requires exceptional management. Our professional leadership team ensures that our ashram operates with efficiency and grace, allowing you to focus completely on your well-being.

*   The center's daily operations are professionally steered by our Chief Executive Officer, **Shibashish Chakraborty**.
*   He is supported by a dedicated management team, including our Deputy General Manager and Resort Manager, who oversee every aspect of your stay.
*   This professional structure ensures that the highest international standards of hospitality, hygiene, and guest care are consistently maintained.

**Our Holistic Ecosystem of Care**

True wellness is supported by a complete ecosystem of care, and our team extends beyond the leadership to every corner of our ashram.

*   Our team of skilled and compassionate therapists administers your treatments with precision, mindfulness, and deep respect for tradition.
*   Our experienced yoga and meditation instructors guide you through practices that are essential for calming the mind and supporting your physical healing.
*   Our Ayurvedic chefs work in perfect synergy with our doctors, transforming the fresh harvest from our organic farm into delicious and healing meals.
