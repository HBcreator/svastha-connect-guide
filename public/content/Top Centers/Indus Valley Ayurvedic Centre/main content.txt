**Indus Valley: A Pioneering Sanctuary of Healing**

**About Indus Valley**
Welcome to Indus Valley Ayurvedic Centre, a pioneering sanctuary of healing nestled at the foothills of the sacred Chamundi Hills in Mysore. Founded on the vision of Dr. Krishna, our center is a testament to over 25 years of authentic Ayurvedic practice. Our entire campus is a 15-acre "Garden of Life," a lush, organic farm where the herbs that heal you are grown. Here, in this serene and natural environment, you will discover a profound path back to your innate state of balance and vitality.

### Philosophy & Approach

1.   Our philosophy is rooted in **deep personalization**. Your journey begins with an in-depth consultation with our expert physicians to discover your unique constitution (*Prakriti*) and craft a healing path exclusively for you.

2.   We believe in **healing in a "Garden of Life."** Our serene, 15-acre organic farm is an integral part of your therapy, providing a healing environment and the pure, potent herbs used in your treatments.

3.   Our focus is on treating the **root cause of your illness**, not just the symptoms, to provide you with a gradual, sustainable transformation and lasting health.

4.   We are committed to **both curative and preventive care**, empowering you with the knowledge of diet and lifestyle to not only heal but also to maintain your well-being for life.
