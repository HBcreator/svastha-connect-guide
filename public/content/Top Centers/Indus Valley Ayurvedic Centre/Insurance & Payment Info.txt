### Insurance & Payment Info

We believe that your journey to profound healing should be as clear and seamless as possible. Our payment and insurance support processes are designed with transparency and care, allowing you to focus completely on your well-being in our serene ashram environment.

**Our Payment & Booking Process**

We have a straightforward and secure process to ensure your booking is confirmed and your arrival is effortless.

*   To confirm your place in our healing sanctuary, an advance deposit is required at the time of booking.
*   The remaining balance for your program is to be paid in full upon your arrival at the centre.
*   We accept payments through secure international bank transfers and all major credit cards for your convenience.

**Support for Your Insurance Claims**

We are committed to supporting you in receiving any eligible benefits from your health insurance provider.

*   As a nationally acclaimed healing centre, our documentation may be accepted by many international insurance providers for reimbursement.
*   Our experienced administrative team will provide you with all the necessary detailed invoices and medical treatment summaries to support your claim.
*   We strongly advise you to contact your insurance provider beforehand to confirm the extent of your coverage for treatments at a traditional Ayurvedic centre in India.

**A Note for Our International Guests**

We have over two decades of experience in welcoming and assisting guests from every corner of the globe.

*   We encourage you to check with your home country's insurance provider regarding their specific policies on reimbursing wellness treatments received abroad.
*   Our team is proficient in preparing the necessary documentation in a clear, professional, and timely manner.
*   We are here to support you in making the entire administrative process as smooth and simple as possible.
