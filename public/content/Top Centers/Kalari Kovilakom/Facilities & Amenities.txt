### Facilities & Amenities

**NABH-Accredited Ayurveda Hospital**
*   Heal with absolute confidence in our fully accredited hospital facility, adhering to India's highest standards for clinical excellence and patient safety.
*   Benefit from our well-equipped, hygienic, and dedicated treatment rooms designed for authentic Panchakarma procedures.
*   Experience a professional medical environment where every aspect is focused solely on your prescribed Ayurvedic treatment.

**The Historic Vengunad Palace**
*   Live and heal within the sacred, restored walls of a magnificent 19th-century royal palace.
*   The majestic architecture, serene courtyards, and palpable history of the space create a powerful healing ambiance.
*   The environment itself, steeped in heritage and profound stillness, is an integral part of your cure.

**Austere & Purposeful Accommodations**
*   Rest in simple, elegant, and comfortable rooms within the palace, consciously designed to support your healing journey.
*   Our rooms are free from modern distractions like televisions, creating a sanctuary for true inner peace and rest.
*   The entire living space is designed to be a seamless extension of the serene and disciplined healing environment.

**In-House Ayurvedic Pharmacy**
*   Receive medicines of the highest purity and potency, prepared fresh in our traditional, on-site pharmacy.
*   Our herbal oils and formulations are crafted with precision according to ancient texts for maximum therapeutic effect.
*   This commitment ensures your treatments are completely authentic and free from any commercial additives.

**Extensive Herbal Gardens**
*   Experience the life force of nature with herbs grown in our own extensive, organic gardens on the palace grounds.
*   Many of the vital ingredients used in your freshly prepared medicines and treatments are sourced directly from our land.
*   This farm-to-pharmacy approach is a cornerstone of our commitment to purity and potency.

**Serene Ayurvedic Dining Hall**
*   Receive your prescribed meals in our peaceful, community dining hall—a space dedicated to mindful nourishment.
*   There is no menu; you are served the specific Sattvic vegetarian diet that your physician has prescribed for your healing.
*   This disciplined approach to food is a fundamental and powerful part of your purification process.

**Yoga, Meditation & Chanting Spaces**
*   Harmonize your mind and body in our dedicated halls and serene spaces designed for spiritual practice.
*   Join daily yoga and meditation sessions with practices specifically tailored to complement your Ayurvedic treatments.
*   The sacred vibrations of daily chanting and traditional rituals further purify the atmosphere of the palace.

**Library for Self-Study**
*   Nourish your mind with our curated collection of books on Ayurveda, yogic philosophy, Indian scriptures, and culture.
*   Find a quiet, comfortable space for peaceful reading, study, and deepening your understanding of your journey.
*   We provide the resources to empower you with the profound wisdom of these ancient healing sciences.

**Contemplative Gardens & Courtyards**
*   Heal in a serene, green environment with beautifully maintained gardens and peaceful walkways.
*   Discover numerous quiet nooks within the palace's courtyards and grounds for silent contemplation and personal reflection.
*   The pristine natural setting in the foothills of the Annamalai mountains enhances your deep connection to nature.
