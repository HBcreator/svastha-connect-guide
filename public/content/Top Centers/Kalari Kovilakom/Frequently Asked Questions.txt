### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

To honor the depth and authenticity of our healing process, we require a minimum stay of 14 nights for all our programs. For more deep-seated or chronic conditions, a stay of 21 or 28 nights is highly recommended for lasting results.

**2. What does "uncompromising Ayurveda" mean in practice?**

It means we are a true "Palace for Ayurveda," not a wellness resort. During your treatment, there is no alcohol, no smoking, no meat, and no leaving the palace grounds. This disciplined environment is essential for the therapies to work at their deepest level, ensuring a profound and authentic healing experience.

**3. What is the accommodation like? Is it a real palace?**

Yes, you will be staying in the historic and beautifully restored 19th-century palace of the Vengunad kingdom. The accommodations blend the simple elegance of the past with essential modern comforts. There are no televisions in the rooms, creating a peaceful sanctuary for your healing.

**4. What kind of food is served?**

We serve a pure, delicious, and strictly vegetarian sattvic cuisine. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are prescribed by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha*. We do not use any oil in our cooking, and the food is a consistently praised part of the Kalari Kovilakom experience.

**5. How do I get to Kalari Kovilakom?**

Our palace is located in Kollengode, Palakkad, Kerala. The nearest airport is Coimbatore International Airport (CJB), which is approximately a 1.5 to 2-hour scenic drive away. We are happy to coordinate your airport transfers for a seamless journey.

**6. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our physicians will review them during your comprehensive initial consultation and advise you on how to manage them safely and effectively during your stay.

**7. Is this a suitable place for a casual holiday?**

Kalari Kovilakom is a dedicated Ayurvedic hospital for those who are serious about their health. It is not a destination for a casual holiday. The disciplined environment is designed for deep, transformative healing, and we ask that all guests commit fully to this process.

**8. Do I need to be experienced in yoga?**

Not at all. Our daily yoga and meditation sessions are designed to be therapeutic, not athletic. Our experienced instructors provide personalized guidance to ensure the practice is safe, gentle, and perfectly supportive of your specific health condition and your Ayurvedic treatment plan.

**9. Can a companion stay with me if they are not taking treatment?**

Due to our deep focus on a disciplined, therapeutic environment, we typically only accommodate guests who are undergoing a full treatment program. This ensures that the entire palace remains a quiet, focused space for healing for all our guests.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable cotton clothing. We will provide you with a fresh set of traditional, loose-fitting cotton pajamas (*kurta*) to wear during your stay, which most guests find very comfortable. Please also bring any personal toiletries and your medical records.
