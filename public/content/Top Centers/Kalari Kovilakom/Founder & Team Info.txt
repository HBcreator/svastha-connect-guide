**Jose Dominic**

Co-Founder, CGH Earth

Jose Dominic is a legendary figure in sustainable tourism and the visionary co-founder behind the CGH Earth philosophy. He transformed a historic 19th-century palace into Kalari Kovilakom, the world's first "Palace for Ayurveda." His vision was to create a sanctuary of uncompromising, classical healing, blending India's rich heritage and sustainable practices with the deepest principles of authentic Ayurvedic medicine.
