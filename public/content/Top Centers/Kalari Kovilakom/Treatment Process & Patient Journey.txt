### Treatment Process & Patient Journey

Your path to a new state of being, a disciplined and medically supervised journey of complete surrender.

1.  **The Ritual of Surrender & In-Depth Consultation**
    Your journey begins at our gates, where you physically and mentally leave the world behind. You will exchange your attire for our simple cotton garments and have your first, comprehensive consultation with our Vaidya (physician) to determine your deep-seated imbalances.
    *   This is a complete surrender; outside food, leather, and personal habits are left at the door.
    *   Through ancient diagnostics, our physicians create a profound understanding of your unique constitution.
    *   This initial step is the foundation for your entire prescribed healing path.

2.  **The Prescribed & Unchanging Healing Regimen**
    Based on your consultation, our physicians will design your rigorous and completely personalized healing regimen. This is not a menu of choices; it is your singular, medically prescribed path to purity and balance.
    *   This is your unique, unchangeable, and physician-led path to deep healing.
    *   It meticulously details your specific Ayurvedic therapies, herbal medicines, diet, and daily practices.
    *   This removes all decision-making, allowing you to focus completely on your inner journey.

3.  **Daily Rituals of Pure, Classical Ayurveda**
    This is where your profound physical purification begins. Each day, you will receive your prescribed, powerful treatments, administered with exceptional skill by our therapists using classical techniques and freshly prepared medicines.
    *   You will receive your treatments in our serene, traditional, and hygienic therapy rooms.
    *   All therapies are performed using pure, potent oils and herbs prepared in our own pharmacy.
    *   These ancient, intensive treatments work to dislodge and eliminate deep-seated toxins.

4.  **The Prescribed Healing Diet (Ahara)**
    At Kalari Kovilakom, food is potent medicine. Your meals are a vital and non-negotiable part of your treatment, personally prescribed by your doctor to purify your system, reduce inflammation, and support your healing.
    *   There is no menu; you are served a strict, Sattvic vegetarian diet tailored to your specific needs.
    *   Every meal is a carefully crafted prescription to support your body’s healing process.
    *   This profound dietary discipline is one of the most powerful tools for your transformation.

5.  **Immersive Ayurvedic Living & Daily Medical Review**
    Your healing is a 24-hour process, guided by a disciplined lifestyle and constant medical supervision. Your day is structured around yoga, meditation, and quiet contemplation, with your progress carefully monitored by our physicians.
    *   Harmonize your mind and body with prescribed yoga and meditation sessions.
    *   Your progress is reviewed every single day by our resident Vaidyas to ensure your journey is safe and effective.
    *   Observing noble silence after dinner deepens the purification of the mind.

6.  **Departure with Empowering Wisdom**
    Our support culminates in empowering you with the knowledge for a lifetime of health. Before you depart, you will receive a comprehensive final consultation with your physician to understand your journey and prepare you for your return to the world.
    *   Receive a detailed and personalized plan, including dietary, herbal, and lifestyle advice.
    *   You leave not just cleansed, but with a profound understanding of your own body.
    *   In the true spirit of Ayurveda, you depart empowered with the wisdom to maintain your health for years to come.
