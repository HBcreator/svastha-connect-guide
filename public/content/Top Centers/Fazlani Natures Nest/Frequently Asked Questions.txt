### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness programs, we recommend a minimum stay of 3 to 5 nights to experience tangible benefits. For our more immersive or medical-focused programs, a stay of 7 nights or more is typically required for deep and lasting results.

**2. How do I get to Fazlani Nature's Nest?**

Our sanctuary is nestled in a serene landscape near Lonavala, Maharashtra. We are conveniently accessible from both Mumbai (approx. 2.5-hour drive) and Pune (approx. F.5-hour drive). We are happy to arrange your private luxury airport transfers for a seamless journey.

**3. What makes Fazlani Nature's Nest different from other wellness retreats?**

Our most unique feature is our deep focus on "One Health," which includes the powerful therapeutic connection between humans and animals. Our on-site 'Animal-Assisted Therapy' programs, with our beautiful horses and other rescued animals, offer a profound and unique path to emotional and psychological healing that you won't find anywhere else.

**4. What kind of food is served?**

We practice a true "farm-to-fork" philosophy. You will be served a delicious, gourmet wellness cuisine, with most ingredients sourced directly from our own extensive organic farms. Our chefs collaborate with our wellness experts to create a menu that is customized to your health needs, is deeply nourishing, and incredibly flavorful.

**5. Do I need to be experienced in yoga to attend?**

Not at all. Our daily yoga and meditation sessions are designed for all levels, from complete beginners to advanced practitioners. Our experienced masters provide personalized guidance to ensure the practice is safe, enjoyable, and perfectly suited to your body and goals.

**6. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing for yoga and other activities, as well as smart casual attire for evenings. Please also bring any personal toiletries and your medical records. We will provide you with all the necessary amenities for a luxurious and comfortable stay.

**7. How personalized is my wellness program?**

Your program is completely personalized. Your journey begins with an in-depth consultation with our wellness experts, who then design a unique "wellness blueprint" just for you. Your entire schedule—from therapies and meals to fitness and wellness activities—is tailored to your specific health goals.

**8. Can a companion stay with me if they are not on a wellness program?**

Yes, your companion is welcome to stay with you and enjoy our serene environment and gourmet wellness cuisine. They can also participate in our daily complimentary wellness activities. Please note that personalized treatments are only for guests on a specific wellness program.

**9. Is this a suitable place for a family wellness vacation?**

Absolutely. We offer a wide range of activities for all ages, from our unique 'Animal-Assisted Therapy' to organic farming and nature walks. It is a wonderful place for families to disconnect from screens, reconnect with nature, and bond in a healthy, healing environment.

**10. Do you serve alcohol at the retreat?**

To honor our commitment to a pure and holistic healing environment, we are a completely alcohol-free and smoking-free sanctuary. This allows for a deeper and more authentic detoxification and rejuvenation experience for all our guests.
