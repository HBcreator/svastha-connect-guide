### Facilities & Amenities

Our sprawling 68-acre sanctuary is a self-contained Eden, where every facility is designed to immerse you in nature, luxury, and profound healing.

**Luxurious Garden & Lake-View Villas**

Your accommodation is a private haven of peace, designed for deep rest with breathtaking views of our lush estate.

*   Stay in beautifully appointed, spacious stand-alone villas.
*   Each villa features a private balcony overlooking the gardens or lake.
*   Enjoy modern luxury and amenities in perfect harmony with nature.

**The Stallion Castle (Equine Centre)**

Our professional equestrian centre is home to our gentle horse companions and our pioneering Equine Therapy program.

*   This is a world-class facility for our unique healing modality.
*   Experience safe, guided sessions with our trained therapists.
*   This is a dedicated space for profound emotional healing.

**The "Amarana" Spa & Wellness Centre**

Our expansive spa is a sanctuary of rejuvenation, offering a curated menu of therapies from around the world.

*   Receive authentic Ayurvedic and Naturopathic treatments.
*   Experience a range of international massages and beauty therapies.
*   Our expert therapists ensure a deeply healing and restorative experience.

**Farm-to-Fork Organic Dining**

Our restaurant, "The Hive," offers a gourmet wellness experience where every meal is a vital and delicious part of your healing journey.

*   Enjoy personalized meals crafted from our on-site organic farms.
*   Our cuisine is designed by our doctors to be therapeutic and delicious.
*   Experience the vibrant taste of true "farm-to-fork" nourishment.

**The Aviary & Animal Farm**

We offer a unique opportunity to connect with our friendly farm animals, a gentle therapy proven to soothe the soul.

*   Interact with our diverse family of birds in the aviary.
*   Experience the calming presence of our farm animals.
*   This is a joyful, heart-opening space for all our guests.

**Serene Yoga & Meditation Pavilions**

We provide beautiful, dedicated spaces designed to deepen your practice and connect you with the tranquility of nature.

*   Practice in our serene pavilions with calming views of the landscape.
*   Join daily guided yoga, pranayama, and meditation sessions.
*   These sacred spaces are designed to harmonize your mind and body.

**Luxury Recreation & Leisure**

We offer a range of luxurious facilities for you to relax, recharge, and enjoy the stunning natural beauty of our estate.

*   Unwind in our stunning swimming pool with panoramic views.
*   Explore our beautiful property on dedicated walking and jogging trails.
*   Our amphitheater hosts various cultural and wellness events.

**Our On-Site Organic Farms**

The heart of our wellness philosophy is our sprawling organic farm, which you can see and experience firsthand.

*   This is the source of the pure, life-giving food you enjoy daily.
*   Join our farm tours to learn about sustainable organic agriculture.
*   Connect with the earth and the source of your nourishment.

**The Therapeutic Landscape**

The entire 68-acre estate is your facility for healing, offering endless opportunities to connect with the restorative power of nature.

*   Our property is nestled on the serene waters of a beautiful lake.
*   Discover countless private nooks for quiet contemplation.
*   This vast natural canvas is perfect for 'Forest Bathing' and mindful walks.
