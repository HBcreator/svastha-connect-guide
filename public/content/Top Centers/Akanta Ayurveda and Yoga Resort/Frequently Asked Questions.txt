### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the best time of year to visit Varkala for treatment?**

Varkala is beautiful year-round, but different seasons offer unique benefits for healing.

*   The main season, from **October to March**, offers pleasant, dry, and sunny weather, perfect for enjoying the cliff and beach.
*   The traditional monsoon season, from **June to September**, is considered the most powerful time for Ayurvedic treatments as the body is more receptive.
*   The climate is tropical, so even in the cooler months, the weather remains warm and pleasant.

**2. What is the minimum recommended stay for a program?**

The duration of your stay depends on your personal health goals and the type of program you choose.

*   For a comprehensive detoxification program like *Panchakarma*, a minimum of **21 to 28 days** is essential for deep and lasting results.
*   For rejuvenation, de-stress, or specific pain management programs, a stay of **10 to 14 days** is highly effective.
*   Shorter wellness escapes of 5 to 7 days are also available for a quick and refreshing reset.

**3. How do I reach Akanta Ayurveda and Yoga Resort?**

Our seaside sanctuary is easily accessible via the region's main transportation hub.

*   The nearest international airport is **Thiruvananthapuram (Trivandrum) International Airport (TRV)**, which is approximately a one-hour drive away.
*   The nearest major railway station is **Varkala Sivagiri Railway Station (VAK)**, just a short 10-minute drive from our resort.
*   We are happy to arrange a reliable and comfortable taxi transfer for you from the airport or railway station upon request.

**4. What is included in a typical all-inclusive package?**

Our packages are designed to be comprehensive, so you can relax and immerse yourself in your healing.

*   Your package includes your private accommodation, a detailed initial consultation, and daily follow-up consultations with our doctor.
*   It covers all prescribed Ayurvedic therapies, internal herbal medicines, and daily therapeutic yoga and meditation sessions.
*   Three delicious and customized Ayurvedic meals per day (breakfast, lunch, and dinner) are also included.

**5. What kind of clothing should I bring?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing that is suitable for a warm, tropical climate and a relaxed healing environment.

*   Bring loose-fitting cotton clothes for your daily activities, yoga sessions, and relaxation time.
*   Include swimwear for the beach and a light shawl or sweater for the cooler evenings.
*   Remember to pack sunscreen, a sun hat, and comfortable walking shoes or sandals for exploring the beautiful Varkala cliff path.

**6. Are the yoga classes suitable for all levels, including beginners?**

Yes, our yoga classes are designed to be accessible and beneficial for everyone, regardless of prior experience.

*   Our experienced instructors teach in a way that accommodates all levels, offering modifications for both beginners and advanced practitioners.
*   The focus is not on difficult postures but on mindful movement, correct breathing, and the therapeutic benefits of the practice.
*   This approach ensures that everyone can participate safely and gain the maximum healing benefits from their daily sessions.
