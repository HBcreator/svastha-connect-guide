### Insurance & Payment Info

We believe that your journey to healing should be as smooth and stress-free as possible.

**Our Payment Process**

We have a straightforward process to ensure your booking is secured and your arrival is seamless.

*   To confirm your place in our sanctuary, an advance deposit is required at the time of booking.
*   The remaining balance is to be paid upon your arrival at the resort.
*   We accept payments through secure international bank transfers and all major credit cards.

**Support for Insurance Claims**

We are committed to helping you receive any eligible benefits from your health insurance provider.

*   While we do not offer direct billing to insurance companies, many providers may reimburse the cost of your treatments.
*   Our administrative team will provide you with all the necessary detailed invoices and treatment summaries required for your claim.
*   We strongly advise you to contact your provider beforehand to confirm the extent of your coverage for Ayurvedic therapies in India.

**A Note for International Guests**

We have extensive experience in welcoming and assisting guests from all over the world.

*   We encourage you to check with your home country's insurance provider regarding their policies on reimbursing treatments received abroad.
*   Our team is proficient in preparing the necessary documentation in a clear and professional format.
*   We are here to support you in making the entire process as easy as possible.
