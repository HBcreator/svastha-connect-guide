### Wellness Programs

Personalized wellness journeys on the majestic Varkala cliffs, designed to cleanse, calm, and revitalize.

**Classical Panchakarma**

*   The ultimate Ayurvedic detoxification to remove deep-seated metabolic toxins (*Ama*) and reset your entire system.
*   A comprehensive, multi-stage process involving preparatory therapies, core cleansing, and post-treatment rejuvenation.
*   A fully personalized program designed for a deep, transformative healing experience.

**Holistic Rejuvenation**

*   Our signature program for complete mind-body renewal, designed to nourish tissues and boost vitality (*Ojas*).
*   A powerful integration of nourishing massages, herbal steams, and internal revitalizing medicines.
*   Focuses on promoting healthy aging, enhancing skin lustre, and improving immunity.

**Ayurvedic Stress & Burnout**

*   A sanctuary for an overwhelmed nervous system, combining calming therapies to restore inner peace.
*   Features deeply relaxing and meditative treatments like *Shirodhara*, Abhyanga, and gentle head massages.

**Body Purification Therapy**

*   A focused therapy aimed at purifying the body from accumulated toxins that cause sluggishness and disease.

**Spine & Joint Care**

*   A dedicated program for relieving pain and strengthening the entire musculoskeletal system.

**Ayurvedic Beauty & Skin**
