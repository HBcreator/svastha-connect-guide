### Facilities & Amenities

To ensure your healing journey is comfortable, holistic, and deeply connected to nature, Akanta provides a thoughtful range of facilities.

**Spectacular Cliff-Top Location**

Our resort is uniquely situated atop the majestic red cliffs of Varkala, offering a powerful healing environment found nowhere else.

*   You will enjoy breathtaking, panoramic views of the boundless Arabian Sea from our property.
*   The pure, ionized sea breeze provides natural therapy, calming the mind and rejuvenating the body.
*   We offer direct access to scenic cliff pathways for mindful walks and stunning, unforgettable sunsets.

**Serene & Comfortable Accommodation**

We offer clean, comfortable, and thoughtfully designed rooms that serve as your private sanctuary for rest and deep healing.

*   Many of our rooms feature private balconies with stunning sea views to enhance your sense of peace.
*   Each room is equipped with a clean, attached bathroom and all essential amenities for a comfortable stay.
*   The simple, uncluttered design promotes a calm mind and supports deep, restorative sleep.

**Rooftop Ocean-View Yoga Shala**

Our dedicated yoga space is designed to provide an inspiring and deeply connecting practice, harmonizing your mind, body, and nature.

*   You can practice daily therapeutic yoga on our stunning rooftop shala with uninterrupted views of the Arabian Sea.
*   The open-air design allows you to connect with the elements, practicing with the soothing sound of the waves.
*   This peaceful and sacred space is perfect for both guided sessions and personal meditation.

**Authentic Ayurvedic Treatment Rooms**

Our treatment center is a clean, peaceful, and hygienic space where classical Ayurvedic therapies are administered with precision and care.

*   We have separate, private therapy rooms for our male and female guests to ensure complete comfort.
*   Our rooms are equipped with traditional equipment, including the wooden *Droni* table for oil treatments.
*   We uphold the highest standards of cleanliness and authenticity in every single procedure.

**Ayurvedic Dining with a View**

Our dining area is a calm and welcoming space where your food becomes an integral and delicious part of your medicine.

*   Enjoy pure-vegetarian Ayurvedic meals, freshly prepared by our skilled chefs every day.
*   Your diet is personally customized by our doctors to align with your *dosha* and support your treatment.
*   Our open and airy restaurant setting allows you to dine comfortably while enjoying the serene resort atmosphere.

**Lush Gardens & Relaxation Areas**

Our resort is dotted with green spaces and tropical gardens, providing ample areas for quiet reflection and deep relaxation.

*   You can find peaceful corners with hammocks and seating areas perfect for reading or quiet contemplation.
*   The natural beauty of our gardens, filled with local flora, is an essential part of the healing environment.
*   Serene pathways offer the perfect opportunity for gentle, mindful strolls within our cliff-top property.

**Library & Reading Lounge**

To nourish your mind during your healing journey, we offer a quiet and comfortable library space for study and relaxation.

*   You can browse our curated collection of books on Ayurveda, Yoga, philosophy, and spiritual well-being.
*   This peaceful lounge provides a perfect sanctuary for reading and reflection during your leisure time.
*   It offers a wonderful opportunity to deepen your understanding of the ancient sciences you are experiencing.

**Personalized Guest Support**

Our warm and attentive team is always available to ensure your stay is seamless, comfortable, and completely stress-free.

*   Our front desk can assist with all your needs, from local travel arrangements to airport transfers.
*   We offer laundry services and other conveniences to make your long-term stay feel like home.
*   As a family-run retreat, we provide a level of personal care and assistance that makes all the difference.

**Essential Modern Conveniences**

While we encourage you to disconnect and immerse yourself in nature, we provide essential amenities for your comfort and convenience.

*   We offer complimentary Wi-Fi access in designated common areas of the resort.
*   This allows you to stay in touch with loved ones without disrupting the peace of your private sanctuary.
*   Our team is always on hand to assist with any of your practical needs during your stay.
