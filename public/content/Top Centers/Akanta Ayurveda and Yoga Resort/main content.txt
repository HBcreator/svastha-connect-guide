**Akanta: Authentic Healing on the Varkala Cliffs**

**About Akanta Ayurveda**
Welcome to Akanta Ayurveda and Yoga Resort, a family-run sanctuary of healing perched atop the majestic red cliffs of Varkala, overlooking the Arabian Sea. Guided by our resident physicians, **Dr. Sruthy and Dr. Krishnadas**, we offer a deeply authentic and personal wellness experience. Here, in an environment where the ocean breeze and the sound of the waves are powerful medicines, you will discover a profound path back to your natural state of balance and vitality.



### Accreditations & Recognition

1.  We are an officially **classified and approved Ayurvedic retreat** by the Department of Tourism, Government of Kerala, assuring you of quality and authenticity.

2.  Your healing is guided by our highly qualified and **government-certified resident physicians**, who bring both expertise and a deep sense of personal care to your journey.

3.  We are a **cherished family-run retreat**, a recognition that speaks to the warmth, dedication, and personal attention that large commercial centers simply cannot offer.

4.  Our greatest recognition is the **heartfelt trust of our international guests**, who travel to our cliff-top sanctuary for our authentic approach and transformative results.



### Philosophy & Approach

1.  Our philosophy is to practice **classical Ayurveda with a deeply personal touch**. We reject generic packages; your entire healing journey is crafted exclusively for you after a thorough consultation.

2.  We believe the **powerful seaside environment is a natural medicine**. The pure sea air, the negative ions from the ocean, and the serene natural beauty are an integral part of our healing protocol.

3.  We believe in the **synergy of authentic Ayurveda and traditional Yoga**. Your body is cleansed by Ayurveda, while your mind is calmed and centered through therapeutic yoga practiced overlooking the ocean.

4.  Your healing is **physician-led and closely monitored**. Our doctors are deeply involved in your day-to-day care, ensuring your treatment is always perfectly aligned with your progress and needs.



**Systems of Medicine Practiced**

1.  **Classical Kerala Ayurveda & Panchakarma**
    The heart of our practice. Your healing is guided by the authentic traditions of Kerala, using classical therapies and the profound detoxification of *Panchakarma* to restore your body’s innate balance.

2.  **Therapeutic Yoga & Meditation**
    We integrate daily Yoga and meditation as essential complements to your treatment. These are not fitness classes but therapeutic sessions practiced on our rooftop shala, using the ocean's rhythm to calm the mind and deepen your healing.

3.  **Personalized Ayurvedic Diet**
    Your healing is supported by a delicious, customized Ayurvedic diet. Every meal is prescribed by our physicians to align with your *dosha* and treatment goals, turning your food into a powerful medicine.
