### Insurance & Payment Info

Making your path to authentic beachfront healing clear and accessible.

**Insurance Coverage**
*   As a physician-led wellness center, our prescribed medical programs like Panchakarma may be eligible for reimbursement from your Indian health insurance provider.
*   We provide all the necessary medical documentation, including detailed invoices and treatment summaries, to support your claims process.
*   We strongly recommend that you speak with your insurance company before your stay to understand the specifics and extent of your coverage.

**Payment Options**
*   We accept all major payment methods for your convenience, including credit/debit cards, UPI, and direct bank transfers.
*   Our pricing is transparent, with all costs for your personalized treatment plan clearly outlined following your doctor's consultation.
*   Special pricing may be available for long-duration stays, such as our intensive Panchakarma programs and Yoga Teacher Trainings.

**For International Patients**
We advise our international guests to consult with their home country's health insurance provider regarding coverage for Ayurvedic and wellness treatments availed in India. Our administrative team is happy to provide the required medical reports to assist with your international claims.
