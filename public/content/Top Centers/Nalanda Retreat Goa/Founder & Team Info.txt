**Chandra Prakash Parihar**

Founder

Chandra Prakash Parihar is the visionary founder of Nalanda Retreat. With over 17 years of deep experience in the wellness space, including managing the renowned 'Devarya Wellness,' his dream was to create a true 'home' where guests could find a deep connection with themselves through Yoga and meditation. His hands-on, personal approach and his passion for creating a warm, community-focused environment are the heart and soul of the Nalanda experience.
