### Facilities & Amenities

**Ocean-Front Yoga Shala**
*   Practice yoga in our stunning, purpose-built shala with panoramic, uninterrupted views of the Arabian Sea.
*   Feel the ocean breeze and hear the soothing sound of the waves, creating a truly immersive and meditative experience.
*   Our shala is fully equipped with high-quality mats, props, and everything needed to support your practice.

**Beachfront Ayurvedic Centre**
*   Receive your healing treatments in our clean, serene, and professional Ayurveda centre, located just steps from the sea.
*   Our facility features private and hygienic therapy rooms designed for authentic Ayurvedic and Panchakarma procedures.
*   Heal in a peaceful environment where the calming energy of the ocean enhances every treatment.

**Sea-View & Eco-Chic Accommodations**
*   Rest and rejuvenate in our beautiful and comfortable accommodations, including sea-view cottages and eco-chic rooms.
*   Our rooms are built with natural materials, designed to blend seamlessly with the beachfront landscape.
*   Enjoy modern comforts and a private sanctuary where you can fall asleep to the sound of the waves.

**Beachfront Sattvic Restaurant**
*   Nourish your body at our beautiful beachfront restaurant, where every meal comes with a spectacular ocean view.
*   Savor delicious, wholesome, and therapeutic Sattvic (pure vegetarian) cuisine prescribed by our resident doctor.
*   Experience a true "food is medicine" philosophy in a serene and scenic dining environment.

**Direct Mandrem Beach Access**
*   Step directly from our retreat onto the soft, clean sands of Mandrem Beach, one of North Goa’s most peaceful shorelines.
*   Enjoy the ultimate convenience of having the ocean at your doorstep for sunrise walks, evening meditation, or a refreshing swim.
*   Our prime location allows you to be fully immersed in the healing power of the sea throughout your stay.

**On-Site Physician Consultation Rooms**
*   Begin your healing journey in our private and professional consultation room with our resident Ayurvedic doctor.
*   Benefit from a confidential and comfortable space for your in-depth diagnosis and daily progress reviews.
*   Feel completely supported with our expert medical team available on-site to guide your entire journey.

**Library & Reading Lounge**
*   Nourish your mind with our curated collection of books on yoga, Ayurveda, philosophy, and well-being.
*   Find a quiet, comfortable space for peaceful reading, study, and contemplation away from the sun.
*   Deepen your understanding of your healing journey with our valuable educational resources.

**Lush Tropical Gardens & Relaxation Nooks**
*   Heal in a serene, green environment, with our cottages nestled amongst lush palm groves and tropical gardens.
*   Discover numerous peaceful spots and comfortable hammocks throughout the property for quiet reflection and rest.
*   The beautiful natural surroundings create a tranquil and oxygen-rich atmosphere that enhances healing.

**Holistic Guest Services**
*   Stay connected if you need to with complimentary Wi-Fi available in our common areas.
*   Allow our friendly and helpful front desk team to assist you with travel arrangements, taxi services, and local information.
*   Enjoy the peace of mind that comes from being cared for by an attentive team dedicated to your comfort and well-being.
