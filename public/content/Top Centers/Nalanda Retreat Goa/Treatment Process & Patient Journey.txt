### Treatment Process & Patient Journey

Your personalized path to healing by the sea, guided by our expert physician every step of the way.

1.  **Beachfront Arrival & In-Depth Physician's Consultation**
    Your healing journey begins with the calming sound of the ocean and a comprehensive, one-on-one consultation. Our resident Ayurvedic physician will conduct a deep diagnostic session to understand your health history, goals, and unique mind-body constitution.
    *   This is a private and thorough consultation led by a qualified Ayurvedic doctor.
    *   Through traditional diagnostics like pulse reading (*Nadi Pariksha*), we uncover your unique *Prakriti*.
    *   We listen deeply to understand your health concerns and identify the root cause of any imbalance.

2.  **Your Personalized Ayurvedic & Yoga Blueprint**
    Based on your personal consultation, our physician will craft your exclusive wellness blueprint. This is a detailed and scientific roadmap to restoring your balance, integrating the sister sciences of Ayurveda and Yoga for a holistic result.
    *   This is not a generic package; it is your unique, physician-led path to healing.
    *   It specifies your bespoke schedule of Ayurvedic therapies, including any *Panchakarma* procedures.
    *   Your plan also includes a tailored Sattvic diet, herbal medicines, and a recommended yoga practice.

3.  **Daily Authentic Ayurvedic Therapies**
    This is where your deep physical healing and rejuvenation begin. Each day, you will receive your prescribed treatments from our highly skilled therapists, who perform each therapy using authentic, classical techniques for maximum therapeutic benefit.
    *   You will receive your personalized treatments each day in our serene and hygienic therapy rooms.
    *   All therapies are performed using pure, herbal oils and preparations.
    *   Our serene, beachfront environment enhances the profound, healing effects of every treatment.

4.  **Prescribed Sattvic Healing Cuisine**
    We practice the principle that food is medicine. Your meals are a delicious and vital part of your treatment, personally prescribed by your doctor to support your specific *dosha*, aid detoxification, and accelerate your healing.
    *   Enjoy delicious, therapeutic Sattvic (pure vegetarian) meals at our beachfront restaurant.
    *   Your meal plan is customized daily to align with your specific treatment goals.
    *   This nourishing cuisine helps to heal your body from the inside out.

5.  **Beachfront Yoga & Daily Doctor's Review**
    Your healing journey is a comprehensive experience that is monitored with medical precision. Each day includes transformative yoga practice in our ocean-facing shala and a personal review with your physician to ensure your progress is on track.
    *   Harmonize your mind and body by syncing your breath with the rhythm of the waves.
    *   Your progress is reviewed daily by our resident doctor, who makes any necessary adjustments to your plan.
    *   This combination of mindful practice and medical supervision ensures a safe and effective journey.

6.  **Departure with Empowering Knowledge**
    Our support for you doesn’t end when you leave our shores; we empower you with the tools for a lifetime of well-being. Before you depart, you will receive a comprehensive final consultation and a detailed take-home plan.
    *   Receive a personalized plan, including dietary and lifestyle advice to continue your progress at home.
    *   You leave not just rejuvenated, but with a deeper understanding of your own body and its needs.
    *   In the true spirit of Nalanda, you depart empowered with the knowledge to maintain your health for years to come.
