### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What makes Nalanda Retreat different from other Goan retreats?**
Nalanda offers a unique combination of three core elements: an absolute beachfront location where you live by the ocean's rhythm, physician-led authentic Ayurveda ensuring genuine medical guidance, and a world-class, Yoga Alliance-accredited school for transformative yoga. Our intimate, boutique size ensures deeply personalized care.

**2. How do I get to Nalanda Retreat?**
We are located directly on Mandrem Beach in North Goa. The closest airport is Manohar International Airport, Mopa (GOX), about a 45-60 minute drive. Dabolim Airport (GOI) is about 90 minutes away. We can easily arrange a pre-paid taxi from the airport or nearest railway station for your convenience.

**3. Do I need to be experienced in Yoga or Ayurveda to visit?**
Absolutely not. Nalanda is a perfect sanctuary for beginners. Every guest's journey begins with a detailed doctor's consultation. Your program will be completely tailored to your experience level, health goals, and comfort, ensuring a safe and supportive environment for your first steps into wellness.

**4. What is the food like?**
We serve delicious and wholesome Sattvic (pure vegetarian) cuisine that is a core part of your healing. Your entire menu is personally prescribed by our Ayurvedic physician based on your unique dosha (mind-body constitution) to support your treatments and enhance your well-being.

**5. Is the beach at Mandrem suitable for swimming?**
Yes, Mandrem Beach is famous for its gentle slope and relatively calm, shallow waters, making it one of the safer and more pleasant beaches for swimming in Goa. It's perfect for a refreshing dip between your yoga sessions and treatments.

**6. What is the minimum stay you recommend?**
For deep therapeutic programs like Panchakarma, a minimum of 14-21 days is essential to experience the full benefits. For a rejuvenation program or a yoga holiday, we recommend at least 7 days. We also offer shorter 3-day wellness resets for those with limited time.

**7. What should I pack for my stay?**
Pack light, comfortable cotton clothing suitable for a warm climate, yoga wear, swimwear, sunscreen, a hat, and a light shawl for cooler evenings. Please bring any personal medications and previous medical reports. We provide yoga mats, props, towels, and basic toiletries.

**8. Can I come just for a relaxing holiday?**
While we are a dedicated healing center, our "Beachfront Yoga Holiday" package is perfect for those seeking a balance of wellness and relaxation. It gives you the freedom to join daily yoga classes while leaving ample time to simply relax, swim, and enjoy the serene beach environment.
