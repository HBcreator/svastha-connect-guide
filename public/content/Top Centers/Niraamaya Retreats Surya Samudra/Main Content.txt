**About Niraamaya Retreats**
Perched atop a dramatic cliff overlooking the Arabian Sea, Niraamaya Retreats Surya Samudra is a breathtaking sanctuary where award-winning wellness, traditional Keralan heritage, and contemporary luxury converge. Comprised of authentic tharavadu cottages transplanted from across Kerala, our Relais & Châteaux resort offers an idyllic escape in Kovalam. Surrounded by lush greenery and the infinite horizon, we invite you on a bespoke journey to find your "Niraamaya"—a profound state of well-being for the body, mind, and soul.
