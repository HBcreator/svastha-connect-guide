### Facilities & Amenities

**Authentic Keralan Heritage Cottages**
*   Rest and rejuvenate in one of our 31 traditional Keralan 'tharavadu' cottages, offering a unique blend of heritage charm and modern luxury.
*   Each meticulously restored cottage provides a private sanctuary, ensuring complete peace and seclusion for your healing journey.
*   Enjoy breathtaking, panoramic views of the Arabian Sea from your private clifftop accommodation.

**The Award-Winning Niraamaya Spa**
*   Heal in our globally acclaimed clifftop spa, a serene sanctuary dedicated to integrated wellness.
*   Our facility features 10 luxurious spa rooms, many with private sit-outs overlooking the ocean, creating a sublime healing environment.
*   Benefit from a comprehensive wellness centre that includes consultation rooms, steam facilities, and a dedicated Ayurveda & Yoga academy.

**Cliff-Edge Infinity Swimming Pool**
*   Refresh your body and spirit in our spectacular cliff-edge infinity pool, which appears to merge seamlessly with the Arabian Sea.
*   Relax on comfortable sun loungers on our spacious deck, offering one of the most stunning ocean vistas in all of Kerala.
*   Enjoy attentive, discreet poolside service while you soak in the sun and serene atmosphere.

**Gourmet Wellness Dining**
*   Nourish your body at our main restaurant, 'Café Samsara', offering panoramic ocean views and a menu of global and regional Keralan cuisine.
*   Experience the art of gourmet wellness with personalized menus crafted by our chefs to support your specific health program.
*   Enjoy intimate, fine-dining experiences at our specialty restaurant, 'Essence', perched on a cliff directly above the sea.

**Clifftop Yoga & Meditation Pavilion**
*   Harmonize your mind and body in our dedicated, open-air yoga pavilion, situated at the edge of the cliff for an inspiring practice.
*   Practice yoga and meditation to the soothing sounds of the ocean waves, with the infinite horizon as your backdrop.
*   Join daily sessions guided by our resident experts, with practices tailored to all experience levels.

**Two Private Beach Coves**
*   Enjoy exclusive access to our two secluded beach coves, perfect for private relaxation and quiet contemplation.
*   Descend from the clifftop sanctuary to the pristine sands below for a mindful walk or to simply connect with the sea.
*   These private beaches offer a serene escape, free from the crowds of public shorelines.

**The Madira Bar**
*   Unwind in the evenings at our sophisticated bar, offering spectacular sunset views over the Arabian Sea.
*   Enjoy a curated selection of fine wines, classic cocktails, and healthy, freshly prepared infusions.
*   The elegant, tranquil setting provides the perfect space to relax and reflect at the end of your day.

**State-of-the-Art Fitness Centre**
*   Maintain your fitness routine in our fully equipped, modern gymnasium.
*   Benefit from a comprehensive range of cardio and strength-training equipment in a serene, air-conditioned environment.
*   Enjoy a holistic approach to wellness that balances restorative therapies with invigorating physical activity.

**Library & Boutique**
*   Nourish your mind in our serene library, offering a curated collection of books on wellness, culture, and literature.
*   Browse our thoughtfully curated boutique for a selection of exquisite local crafts, wellness products, and unique souvenirs.
*   Find the perfect memento to remember your clifftop journey or a captivating book to enjoy by the pool.
