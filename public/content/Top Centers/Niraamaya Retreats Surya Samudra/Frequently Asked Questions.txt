### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness programs, we recommend a minimum of 7 nights to experience tangible benefits. For our more intensive medical or detoxification programs, a stay of 14 nights or more is typically required for deep and lasting results.

**2. How do I get to Niraamaya Surya Samudra?**

Our cliff-side sanctuary is located near Kovalam in Kerala. The nearest airport is Trivandrum International Airport (TRV), which is approximately a 30 to 45-minute scenic drive away. We are happy to arrange your private luxury airport transfers.

**3. What makes Niraamaya different from other luxury resorts?**

Our key difference is the authentic, deep wellness focus within a world-class luxury setting. We are a member of **Relais & Châteaux**, and we offer genuine, physician-led Ayurvedic and Functional Medicine programs, not just superficial spa treatments. Our unique heritage cottages and stunning cliff-top location also set us apart.

**4. What kind of food is served?**

You will be served a delicious, gourmet wellness cuisine at our beautiful sea-facing restaurant, Café Samsara. Our chefs work in close collaboration with our Ayurvedic doctors to create a menu that is customized to your specific *dosha*, your health goals, and is incredibly flavorful.

**5. Do I need to be experienced in yoga?**

Not at all. Our daily yoga and meditation sessions are designed for all levels, from complete beginners to advanced practitioners. Practicing yoga on our dedicated cliff-side shala, overlooking the Arabian Sea, is a truly unique and unforgettable experience.

**6. What is the accommodation like?**

You will be staying in your own private, traditional Keralite heritage cottage. These cottages have been beautifully restored to blend authentic architectural charm with modern five-star luxury. Many feature open-to-sky showers and private terraces with stunning sea views.

**7. Can a companion stay with me if they are not on a wellness program?**

Absolutely. Your companion is welcome to stay with you in your luxurious cottage. They can enjoy our beautiful resort, our stunning rock-cut infinity pool, the pristine beaches, and our gourmet wellness cuisine.

**8. Should I bring my regular medications with me?**

Yes, it is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions. Our physicians will review them during your comprehensive initial consultation to create a safe and effective treatment plan for you.

**9. What is "Functional Medicine"?**

Functional Medicine is part of our integrative approach. It uses modern diagnostics to get a clear, scientific picture of your health. Our physicians use these insights to make your traditional Ayurvedic treatment plan even more targeted and effective.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable clothing for yoga and relaxation, as well as smart casual attire for evenings. Please also bring swimwear for our infinity pool, any personal toiletries, and your medical records. We will provide all other necessary amenities for a luxurious stay.
