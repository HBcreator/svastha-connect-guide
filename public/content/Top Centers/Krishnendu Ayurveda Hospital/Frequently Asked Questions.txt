### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

For our wellness programs, a minimum of 7 days is recommended. For our medical treatment programs, a stay of 14 to 28 days is typically required for deep, effective, and lasting healing, as advised by our physicians based on your condition.

**2. What does your "four-generation legacy" mean for my treatment?**

It means your treatment is not based on theory, but on a living legacy of healing wisdom passed down since 1908. Our physicians have an intuitive and profound understanding of Ayurveda that comes from generations of clinical experience, and they often use proprietary family formulations that are exceptionally effective.

**3. How is physiotherapy integrated with your Ayurvedic treatments?**

We have a well-equipped, in-house physiotherapy unit that works in synergy with our Ayurvedic treatments. While our Ayurvedic therapies work to reduce inflammation and pain, our expert physiotherapists guide you through targeted exercises to restore your strength, mobility, and function, providing a truly comprehensive recovery.

**4. What kind of food will I be served?**

You will be served a delicious and wholesome vegetarian diet based on classical Ayurvedic principles, with a taste of authentic Kerala cuisine. Your meals are considered a vital part of your treatment and are customized by our physicians to suit your unique *dosha* and health condition.

**5. Should I bring my current medications and medical records?**

Yes, absolutely. It is crucial that you bring any prescribed medications you are currently taking, along with the relevant prescriptions and recent medical records. Our physicians will review them during your initial consultation to create a safe and comprehensive treatment plan for you.

**6. What does NABH accreditation mean for me as a patient?**

The NABH accreditation is the highest mark of quality and patient safety in India. It is your assurance that even though we are a traditional healing center, we adhere to the most stringent modern standards of clinical excellence, hygiene, and patient care.

**7. What is the difference between the accommodation options?**

We offer a range of clean and comfortable rooms to suit different budgets, from basic non-AC rooms to deluxe AC rooms and premium poolside cottages. The primary difference is the level of modern amenities and space, but all rooms are maintained to the same high standard of cleanliness and provide a peaceful environment for healing.

**8. Are the treatments really personalized?**

Yes, completely. There are no one-size-fits-all packages here. Following your comprehensive initial consultation, our fourth-generation physicians will design a completely unique treatment plan tailored to your specific health needs, goals, and constitution.

**9. How do I get to the hospital?**

Our hospital is located in the peaceful village of Chingoli, Alappuzha. We are accessible from both Cochin International Airport (COK) and Trivandrum International Airport (TRV). We are happy to help you coordinate your airport transfers for a seamless and stress-free journey.

**10. What should I pack for my stay?**

We recommend packing light, comfortable cotton clothing suitable for a warm climate and for wearing during yoga sessions. Please also bring any personal toiletries, your medical records, and a good book. We will provide towels and any necessary attire for your treatments.
