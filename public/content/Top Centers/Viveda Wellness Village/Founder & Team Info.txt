### Founder & Team Info

Your journey to well-being, guided by a team driven by vision and authentic expertise.

**The Visionary Behind the Village**
The soul of Viveda begins with the vision of **Tejas Kiran Chavan**. As the Founder and Director, he imagined a sanctuary that was more than just a retreat—he envisioned a "Viveda Wellness Village." His mission was to create a destination dedicated to fostering a sustainable and healthy lifestyle, deeply rooted in the timeless wisdom of Naturopathy and Ayurveda.

**Our Expert Leadership Team**

Guided by a Synergy of Diverse Talents

Your transformative experience at Viveda is supported by a multi-disciplinary team of strategists, architects, clinicians, and hospitality experts.
