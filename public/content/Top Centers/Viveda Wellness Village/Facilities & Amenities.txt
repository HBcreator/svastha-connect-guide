### Facilities & Amenities

Our sprawling sanctuary is a modern temple of well-being, where every facility is designed to the highest standards of luxury and clinical excellence, creating a perfect ecosystem for your healing journey.

**Luxurious Villa Accommodation**
Your accommodation is your private sanctuary of peace, designed to provide deep, restorative rest in an environment of modern luxury.

*   Stay in one of our 40 spacious and elegantly appointed villas, ensuring absolute comfort and privacy.
*   Each villa features a private sit-out or garden space, connecting you with the serene natural surroundings.
*   The wellness-focused design promotes a calm, uncluttered mind and deep, restful sleep.

**The NABH-Accredited Wellness Centre**
Our state-of-the-art wellness centre is the heart of your healing journey, where therapies are administered with clinical precision and care.

*   We have dedicated, private suites for our diverse range of Ayurvedic and Naturopathic treatments.
*   Our facility operates with the hygiene, protocols, and professionalism of a premium, NABH-accredited hospital.
*   The centre is equipped with modern therapeutic equipment to ensure your treatments are both safe and effective.

**The 'Naivedhyam' Sattvic Restaurant**
Our restaurant is where our "food as medicine" philosophy comes to life, offering a gourmet journey that is both delicious and therapeutic.

*   Enjoy personalized, pure vegetarian meals in our elegant and serene dining hall.
*   Our menus are designed by our doctors and crafted by our expert chefs from the daily harvest of our organic farm.
*   Experience the vibrant taste and healing power of our true "farm-to-plate" sattvic cuisine.

**Our On-Site Organic Farms**
The soul of our cuisine and the source of its purity is our own sprawling organic farm, a living testament to our commitment to wellness.

*   This is the source of the pure, life-giving, and pesticide-free food you will enjoy during your stay.
*   The fresh, vibrant produce is harvested daily to ensure maximum vitality and nutritional value.
*   Our farms are an integral part of our sustainable ecosystem and our promise of authentic nourishment.

**Serene Yoga & Meditation Spaces**
We provide beautiful, dedicated spaces designed to deepen your spiritual practice and connect you with the profound energy of our sacred location.

*   Practice in our dedicated Yoga Hall, a peaceful sanctuary designed for focus and introspection.
*   Join our daily meditation and 'Agni Hotra' fire ceremony sessions in specially designated areas.
*   These sacred spaces are designed to harmonize your mind and body in a serene atmosphere.

**Luxury Pool & Hydrotherapy Zone**
Our stunning swimming pool and hydrotherapy facilities offer the perfect space for gentle exercise, relaxation, and detoxification.

*   Unwind in our magnificent temperature-controlled swimming pool, a centerpiece of our resort.
*   Benefit from our luxurious jacuzzi, steam, and sauna facilities to enhance your detoxification process.
*   These amenities provide the perfect balance of active and passive wellness during your stay.

**Therapeutic Outdoor Spaces**
Our beautifully landscaped grounds offer a range of unique facilities designed for active, nature-based healing and relaxation.

*   Experience the benefits of our specially designed reflexology track, which stimulates vital energy points.
*   Take mindful walks on our serene jogging paths that wind through the lush property and along the riverside.
*   These spaces encourage you to connect with the healing power of nature as a part of your daily routine.

**The 'Yagya Shala' (Fire Ceremony Pavilion)**
We offer a rare opportunity to connect with ancient Vedic traditions in our dedicated and sacred 'Yagya Shala'.

*   This is a special pavilion designed for the daily practice of the 'Agni Hotra' fire ceremony.
*   This ancient ritual is performed to purify the environment and the inner self, creating positive energy.
*   It is a unique spiritual facility that adds a profound dimension to your healing journey.

**The Library & Reading Lounge**
Our library provides a peaceful sanctuary for the mind, offering a quiet space for study, reading, and contemplation.

*   Browse our curated collection of books on wellness, spirituality, Ayurveda, and philosophy.
*   Relax in a comfortable and serene environment designed to support your inner journey.
*   This is a perfect space to balance your active therapies with moments of quiet stillness and learning.
