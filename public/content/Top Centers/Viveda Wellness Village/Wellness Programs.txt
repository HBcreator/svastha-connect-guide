### Wellness Programs

Bespoke wellness journeys in a luxurious, NABH-accredited sanctuary, designed to detoxify, rejuvenate, and harmonize your entire being.

**Viveda Signature Detox**

This is a scientifically designed purification experience. It combines the deep cleansing of Ayurveda with the gentle, elemental power of Naturopathy to reset your system in a safe and supportive environment.

*   A personalized and medically-supervised program to effectively remove accumulated toxins (*Ama*).
*   Integrates a nourishing diet of sattvic, "farm-to-plate" cuisine from our organic gardens.
*   Aims to restore your metabolism, improve energy levels, and enhance mental clarity.

**Holistic Rejuvenation**

This is our signature anti-aging and vitality-boosting program. It is designed to deeply nourish your body on a cellular level, combating the effects of stress and promoting graceful longevity.

*   Integrates Ayurvedic *Rasayana* therapies with restorative yoga and a nutrient-rich diet.
*   Focuses on enhancing immunity (*Ojas*), boosting vitality, and restoring a youthful radiance.
*   A perfect retreat to rediscover your vitality in our serene, riverside sanctuary.

**Stress Management**

This program is a sanctuary for a stressed mind and a fatigued body. It uses our integrated therapies to release deep-seated tension and restore your emotional equilibrium.

*   Combines calming Ayurvedic therapies like *Shirodhara* with grounding Naturopathy treatments.
*   Integrates daily yoga, meditation, and mindfulness practices to soothe the nervous system.
*   Empowers you with effective tools and techniques for long-term stress resilience.

**The Yogic Path**

This is an immersive experience designed to deepen your yoga practice. It focuses on Yoga as a therapeutic science for harmonizing the mind, body, and spirit in a spiritually charged environment.

*   Integrates traditional Hatha yoga with pranayama, guided meditation, and philosophical discourse.
*   The practice is enhanced by our serene environment on the banks of the holy Godavari river.
*   Aims to improve flexibility, build strength, and cultivate a profound sense of inner peace.

**The Art of Mindful Living**

This is a profound journey designed for those seeking spiritual well-being and a deeper connection to the present moment. It uses introspective practices to quiet the mental chatter and find your inner center.
