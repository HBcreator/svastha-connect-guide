### Treatment Process & Patient Journey

Your journey at Viveda is a structured, medically-supervised, and deeply personal path to rebirth, where every step is meticulously designed to harmonize your body and mind in our NABH-accredited sanctuary.

**The Initial Wellness Consultation**
Your journey begins with a comprehensive, one-on-one consultation with our senior doctors to create a complete picture of your health.

*   This in-depth session includes traditional Ayurvedic diagnostics and modern health assessments.
*   We listen carefully to understand your health history, lifestyle, and unique wellness goals.
*   This forms the foundation for your bespoke wellness blueprint and your path to healing.

**Your Personalized Wellness Blueprint**
Following your consultation, our doctors design your unique and personalized wellness blueprint—a detailed roadmap for your transformative journey.

*   This is not a pre-set package, but a dynamic plan crafted exclusively for your specific needs.
*   It intelligently integrates therapies from Ayurveda, Naturopathy, and Yoga for a synergistic effect.
*   Your blueprint includes your daily schedule of therapies, your personalized diet, and wellness activities.

**Daily Therapeutic Sessions**
Each day, you will immerse yourself in your prescribed healing therapies, administered by our skilled practitioners in our state-of-the-art wellness centre.

*   You will experience a powerful blend of treatments designed to detoxify, heal, and re-balance your system.
*   Every therapy is medically supervised to ensure it is perfectly suited to your body's needs and progress.
*   These sessions cleanse the body, which in turn brings a natural calm and clarity to the mind.

**The 'Farm-to-Plate' Healing Diet**
Your healing is profoundly supported by our sattvic wellness cuisine, where every meal is a delicious and integral part of your prescribed therapy.

*   Your personalized menus are designed by our doctors and crafted by our expert chefs.
*   Meals are made from fresh, organic produce harvested daily from our own on-site farms.
*   This conscious nutrition supports your body’s detoxification and rejuvenation process from the inside out.

**Mind-Body Harmony**
An essential part of your daily routine involves practices designed to harmonize your mind and body, enhancing the effects of your treatments.

*   Your schedule includes daily therapeutic yoga and guided meditation sessions.
*   You are invited to participate in the ancient 'Agni Hotra' fire ceremony for inner and outer purification.
*   These practices are crucial for reducing stress and integrating the healing on a deeper level.

**Empowerment for Your Path Forward**
Our commitment to your health extends beyond your stay. We empower you with the knowledge and tools to maintain your well-being upon returning home.

*   Your journey concludes with a final consultation to review your progress and health gains.
*   You receive a detailed post-retreat plan, including personalized diet and lifestyle recommendations.
*   You leave not just rejuvenated, but truly empowered with a blueprint for a healthier, more conscious life.
