### Insurance & Payment Info

We believe your journey to wellness should be clear and seamless from the very beginning. Our processes are designed with the professionalism and transparency of an NABH-accredited medical facility.

**Our Secure Payment & Booking Process**

Our process is designed to be straightforward, ensuring your place in our sacred sanctuary is reserved effortlessly.

*   To confirm your bespoke wellness journey, an advance deposit is required at the time of booking.
*   The remaining balance for your program is typically due upon your arrival at our wellness village.
*   We accept payments through secure international bank transfers and all major credit cards for your convenience.

**Support for Your Insurance Claims**

As a NABH-accredited wellness centre, our medically-supervised programs may be eligible for reimbursement from your health insurance provider.

*   Our NABH accreditation is the gold standard for clinical care, often a prerequisite for insurance claims.
*   Our experienced administrative team will provide you with all necessary medical documentation and detailed invoices to support your claim.
*   We strongly advise you to contact your insurance provider beforehand to confirm the extent of your coverage for treatments at our facility.

**A Note for Our International Guests**

We have extensive experience in welcoming and assisting guests from every corner of the globe.

*   We encourage you to check with your home country's insurance provider regarding their policies on reimbursing wellness treatments received abroad.
*   Our team is proficient in preparing the necessary paperwork in a clear, professional, and timely manner.
*   We are here to support you in making the entire administrative process as smooth and simple as possible.
