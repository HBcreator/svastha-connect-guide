### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. How do I reach Viveda Wellness Village?**

Our sanctuary is nestled in the holy land of Nashik, offering a peaceful escape while remaining conveniently accessible from major cities.

*   From **Mumbai International Airport (BOM):** This is a scenic 3.5 to 4-hour drive.
*   From **Pune International Airport (PNQ):** This is approximately a 5 to 5.5-hour drive.
*   The nearest airport is **Nashik Airport (ISK)**, which is just a short and comfortable 45-minute drive from our retreat.
*   We are delighted to arrange a reliable and comfortable private transfer for you from any of these airports upon request.

**2. What is the minimum recommended stay?**

The duration of your stay is key to achieving deep, lasting results and is always recommended by our doctors based on your personal health goals.

*   For a deep detoxification program like *Panchakarma*, a minimum stay of **14 to 21 days** is essential for profound results.
*   For transformative results in weight management or stress management, a stay of **7 to 10 nights** is highly effective.
*   Shorter wellness immersions of **3 or 5 nights** are also available for a powerful introduction to the Viveda way of life.

**3. What is included in my all-inclusive package?**

Our programs are designed to be comprehensive and all-inclusive, allowing you to immerse yourself fully in your healing journey without any distractions.

*   Your package includes your luxurious private villa accommodation and all initial, ongoing, and final doctor consultations.
*   It covers all your prescribed therapies from Ayurveda and Naturopathy, as well as daily wellness activities like yoga.
*   Three gourmet, personalized sattvic meals per day from our "farm-to-plate" restaurant are also included.

**4. What is the best time of year to visit Nashik?**

Nashik's climate offers a pleasant experience year-round, with each season lending a unique charm to our riverside sanctuary.

*   The winter months, from **October to February**, are the most popular, with cool, dry, and sunny weather perfect for all activities.
*   The monsoon season, from **July to September**, transforms the landscape into a lush green paradise and is traditionally potent for Ayurveda.
*   The summer, from **March to June**, is warmer but still a good time to visit for a focused, indoor wellness journey.

**5. What kind of clothing should I bring?**

We recommend packing comfortable, modest clothing suitable for a range of therapeutic and leisure activities in a luxury wellness setting.

*   Bring loose-fitting cotton clothes (e.g., kurta, track pants) for your daily therapies and relaxation.
*   Pack activewear for your Yoga and fitness sessions.
*   Remember to include swimwear for our stunning pool, and comfortable walking shoes for exploring our beautiful property.

**6. Is Viveda suitable for someone new to wellness?**

Absolutely. Our philosophy is to meet you wherever you are on your journey and guide you forward with personal care, expertise, and luxury.

*   All our programs are completely personalized, so a beginner's journey is crafted differently from that of an experienced practitioner.
*   The environment is supportive, luxurious, and non-judgmental, making it the perfect place to begin your wellness journey.
*   Our team of doctors and experts excels at making ancient wisdom accessible, practical, and enjoyable for everyone.
